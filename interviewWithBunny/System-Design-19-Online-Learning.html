01Requirements

1. Functional Requirements — system ko kya-kya karna hai

Functional requirements wo kaam hain jo system ko karna hi hai — user ko dikhai dene wale features. Is platform me 8 features hain, aur har ek ke peeche ek design decision chhupa hai.

Feature 1 — User accounts, roles ke saath

Teen roles hain: student, instructor, admin. Har user ka account banta hai, lekin instructor banne se pehle verification zaroori hai — bina verify hue koi padha nahi sakta. Verification me identity check hota hai aur payout ke liye Stripe Connect account judta hai, taaki instructor ki kamai seedha uske account me ja sake.

Feature 2 — Instructor course banata hai

Instructor course banata hai jisme hota hai: videos, quizzes, assignments, category, difficulty level, rating, price aur resources (PDFs, notes, code files). Course ek container hai — andar sections hote hain, sections ke andar lessons hote hain, aur har lesson video, quiz ya assignment ho sakta hai.

Feature 3 — Student enroll karta hai, progress track hoti hai

Student course me enroll karta hai — free ya paid. Enroll ke baad uski progress track hoti hai: video completion aur quiz scores ke basis par. Progress ka matlab hai — kitna course dekh liya, kitne quiz pass kiye, aur kahan ruka tha (resume).

Feature 4 — Assessment system

Do tarah ke assessment hain. Quizzes auto-grade hote hain — MCQ ka answer sahi hai ya galat, system khud check karta hai, turant score milta hai. Assignments manual grade hote hain — essay-type answers ko instructor khud padh kar grade karta hai. Dono ka pass/fail status track hota hai.

Feature 5 — Progress tracking aur resume

Course completion status dikhta hai — kitna percent complete hua. Sabse important: resume functionality — student ne video 140 second par chhodi thi, to dobara kholne par video wahin se chalegi, shuru se nahi. Ye last_position_sec field se hota hai (Step 3 me poora detail hai).

Feature 6 — Review aur rating system

Student course ko 1–5 stars de sakta hai aur written review likh sakta hai. Reviews par helpful votes bhi hote hain — achhi review upar aati hai. Ye ratings course listing aur search ranking me kaam aati hain.

Feature 7 — Instructor onboarding & verification

Naya instructor pehle onboard hota hai: identity check, phir payout setup via Stripe Connect. Iske bina instructor course publish nahi kar sakta aur paise nahi kama sakta. Ye fraud rokne ke liye zaroori hai.

Feature 8 — Bade files ka support

Videos 5 GB tak per file aur resources (PDF, zip) 100 MB tak per file support karte hain. 5 GB video seedha server se upload nahi hota — iske liye presigned URL + chunked, resumable upload use hota hai (Step 1 me detail hai).

Interview tip: Functional requirements bolte waqt sirf list mat ginao — har feature ke saath ek line me kyun zaroori hai, ye bhi bolo. Jaise: "verification isliye kyunki paise aur trust dono judte hain" — interviewer ko lagta hai tumne socha hai, ratta nahi maara.

Non-functional requirements batate hain ki system kitna bada, kitna tez aur kitna bharosemand hona chahiye. Ye numbers hi aage ki poori architecture decide karte hain — CDN kyun, Kafka kyun, sharding kyun, sab inhi numbers se nikalta hai.

03Core Design

3. Core Entity — 8 tables ka data model

Data model system ki haddi hai. Yahan 8 core entities hain. Har entity ke fields dhyaan se dekho — kuch detail diagram (image) se li gayi hai, jaise difficulty ke enum values aur DECIMAL(5,2) types; jahan detail sirf image me thi, wahan ye page me saaf likha hai.

Entity 1 — User / Instructor

userID, name, email, role (student / instructor / admin), instructor_profile {bio, expertise, verification_status, stripe_account_id}, status, metadata. Ek hi User table dono roles ko cover karti hai — instructor ka extra data instructor_profile object me hai, jisme Stripe account bhi juda hai (payout ke liye).

Entity 2 — Course

courseID, instructorID, title, description, category, difficulty, rating (DECIMAL), price, students_enrolled, published_status, thumbnail_url, created_at. Difficulty ke teen values hain (diagram se): BEGINNER / INTERMEDIATE / ADVANCED. rating DECIMAL hai aur reviews se denormalized hai — har baar reviews se ginna nahi padta. students_enrolled bhi denormalized counter hai.

Entity 3 — Enrollment

enrollmentID, userID, courseID (FK), owner_instructor_id, enrolled_at (DATETIME / TIMESTAMP — diagram se), completion_status (DECIMAL 5,2 — matlab 0.00 se 100.00% tak), payment_id, metadata. owner_instructor_id denormalized hai — course ke instructor ko baar-baar join kiye bina pata chal jaye. completion_status batata hai course kitna percent complete hua.

Entity 4 — Progress

user_id, course_id, lesson_id, video_id (agar lesson video hai — diagram se), last_position_sec (resume point — diagram se), max_position_sec (sabse door tak dekha hua point — diagram se), status (NOT_STARTED / IN_PROGRESS / COMPLETED), completed_at, metadata. Do position fields hi is design ki jaan hain: last_position_sec batata hai resume kahan se karna hai, max_position_sec batata hai sabse aage tak kahan dekha — completion check isi se hota hai.

Entity 5 — Quiz

quiz_id, lesson_id, title, pass_score (70%), time_limit_sec, attempts_allowed. Questions: question_id (FK — diagram se), question, type (MCQ_SINGLE / MULTI_SELECT / FILL / SELECT / TRUE_FALSE / TEXT — diagram se), options (JSON — diagram se), correct_answer, points, metadata. Question types 6 hain — sirf MCQ nahi, fill-in-the-blank aur true/false bhi.

Entity 6 — Payment

payment_id, user_id, course_id, amount (DECIMAL), status (PENDING / COMPLETED / FAILED / REFUNDED), stripe_payment_id, payment_method, created_at, metadata. stripe_payment_id Stripe ke payment intent se link karta hai — refund aur reconciliation isi se hoti hai.

Entity 7 — Review

review_id, user_id, course_id, rating (1–5 INT), comment (TEXT), helpful_votes (INT), created_at. Simple entity hai, lekin helpful_votes se best reviews upar aate hain aur avg_rating course_stats me denormalized hota hai.

Entity 8 — Course_stats (denormalized — diagram se)

course_id, avg_rating, total_reviews, enrolled, completion_rate (%) — Kafka se async update hota hai, taaki course listing ke queries tez rahen. Agar har listing par reviews table se COUNT aur AVG nikalna pade, to listing <200ms target kabhi nahi milega. Isiliye ye alag denormalized table hai — thoda stale chalega, tez zaroor hoga.

Denormalization ka rule: Jo data baar-baar padha jata hai aur kam badalta hai (rating, enrolled count), use denormalize karo. Jo data paise se juda hai (payment, enrollment), use hamesha normalized aur consistent rakho. Ye farak interview me bolna bahut strong point hai.

4. API Designing — courses, enrollments, progress

API design me teen groups hain — Courses, Enrollments, Progress. Har endpoint ke saath ye bhi samjho ki wo kyun aisa hai: kaunsa fast hona chahiye, kaunsa consistent, kaunsa batched.

Courses — khojo, dekho, banao

EndpointKya karta hai
GET /courses?search={}&category={}&price={}&rating={}&level={}&sort=Search/filter courses — category, price range, rating, difficulty level se filter; sort by popularity / rating / newest. Ye sabse zyada chalane wala endpoint hai, isiliye iska target <200ms hai (Elasticsearch + Redis + course_stats se milta hai).
GET /courses/{courseId}Course ki poori detail — curriculum, instructor info, reviews, enrollment count. Ek call me sab kuch, taaki course page ek hi round-trip me khul jaye.
POST /instructor/coursesInstructor naya course banata hai — title, description, category, price, difficulty. Course pehle DRAFT me banta hai, publish alag step hai.
POST /instructor/courses/{courseId}/publishCourse publish karta hai — students ko dikhai dene lagta hai. Agar instructor ka pehla course hai to admin approval ke baad hi visible hota hai (quality + fraud control).

Enrollments — enroll karo, apne courses dekho

EndpointKya karta hai
POST /courses/{courseId}/enroll (free enroll)Free course me enroll — enrollment record banta hai aur content ka access mil jata hai. Paid course ke liye yehi flow payment ke baad Kafka se complete hota hai (Step 4).
GET /me/enrollments?courseId=...User ke enrolled courses — progress percentage, completion status aur last accessed ke saath. "My learning" page isi se banta hai.

Progress — dekhna track karo

EndpointKya karta hai
POST /progress/events (batched events)Video watch events, quiz completion, assignment submission — har 30 sec me batched bheje jate hain (1 event/10sec ki jagah 1 batch/30sec — API calls 3× kam). Response turant 200 OK, DB write async hota hai.
GET /me/courses/{courseId}/progressEk course ki user progress — percentage, last watched position, completed lessons. Resume button isi se banta hai.
Design ka sawaal jo interviewer poochta hai: "Progress event har 10 sec me kyun, har 1 sec me kyun nahi?" — Jawab: 1M students × har second = 60M events/min, system doob jayega. 10-sec heartbeat + 30-sec batching se load 3× kam hota hai, aur resume ke liye 10-sec accuracy kaafi hai. Accuracy aur cost ka trade-off hai.

Ek monolith me teeno ko ek saath scale karna mushkil hota; alag services me har ek ko apne hisaab se scale kar sakte ho — yahi microservices ka asli reason hai, "trend" nahi.

06.1Deep Dive — LLD

Step 1: Video Upload & Transcoding

Instructor 2 GB ki video upload karta hai. Sabse badi galti jo log karte hain: video ko server se hokar upload karwana aur sync transcode karna. Dono galat hain — upload seedha S3 me jata hai, aur transcode async hota hai.

1. Upload request

Instructor video upload karta hai: POST /instructor/courses/{courseId}/lessons/{lessonId}/video.

2. Presigned S3 URL

Media Uploader S3 ka presigned URL generate karta hai (1 ghante ke liye valid):

Presigned URL — server file ko chhoo-ta bhi nahi
presignedURL = s3.generatePresignedUrl('putObject', {
  Bucket: 'course-videos-raw',
  Key: 'courses/C123/lessons/L456/original.mp4',
  Expires: 3600
})
Response: { uploadURL: presignedURL, videoId: 'V789' }

3. Client seedha S3 me upload karta hai

Client PUT {presignedURL} se video file seedha S3 me bhejta hai — server ko bypass karke, isiliye tez hai. Bade files (5 GB max) ke liye chunked upload hota hai, aur network fail ho jaye to resumable hai — dobara shuru se nahi bhejna padta.

4. S3 event → Lambda → Kafka

Jaise hi file S3 me aati hai, S3 event Lambda ko trigger karta hai. Lambda Kafka me video.uploaded event publish karta hai: {videoId, courseId, lessonId, s3Key, fileSize: 2GB}.

5. Video Processing Worker (Kafka consumer)

Worker 6 kaam karta hai, ek ke baad ek:

  1. Download — original.mp4 S3 se download karo
  2. Transcode — 4 resolutions (FFmpeg): 1080p (1920×1080, 5 Mbps), 720p (1280×720, 2.5 Mbps), 480p (854×480, 1 Mbps), 360p (640×360, 500 Kbps)
  3. HLS packaging — master.m3u8 + variant playlists + 6-sec ke .ts segments bante hain:
    HLS packaging — FFmpeg command
    ffmpeg -i 1080p.mp4 -c copy -f hls -hls_time 6 \
  -hls_segment_filename '1080p_segment_%03d.ts' 1080p.m3u8
  4. Thumbnail extraction — 0s, 5s aur 10s par frames nikalo (JPEG)
  5. Upload sab kuch S3 me — s3://course-videos-cdn/.../master.m3u8
  6. Lessons table update — UPDATE lessons SET video_url='https://cdn.example.com/.../master.m3u8', thumbnail_url, processing_status='COMPLETED', variants={1080p,720p,480p,360p}, duration_sec=1200

6. Kitna time lagta hai?

2 GB video ke liye processing ~10–15 min lagti hai (async). Instructor ko turant upload confirmation mil jata hai, aur complete hote hi notify kar diya jata hai. Diagram me S3 "video file chunks in CloudMedia (thumbnail)" ke roop me dikhta hai.

Warning — kabhi sync transcode mat karo
NEVER sync transcode during upload. 2 GB video = 10–15 min processing (FFmpeg 4 resolutions + HLS packaging + thumbnails). MUST async: S3 event → Lambda → Kafka 'video.uploaded' → Worker pool (Spot instances 70% cheaper). Instructor ko turant upload confirmation milta hai, complete hone par notify kiya jata hai.
Spot instances kyun? Transcoding bursty kaam hai — kabhi 100 videos ek saath, kabhi zero. Spot instances 70% saste hote hain; agar ek instance chala bhi jaye to worker dobara try kar lega, kyunki Kafka event dobara consume ho sakta hai. Isiliye worker pool Spot par chalana cost ka best faisla hai.

06.2Deep Dive — LLD

Step 2: Video Streaming & CDN (<2sec startup)

Student video dekhna chahta hai. Target hai <2 sec startup — play dabate hi video shuru. Ye target sirf CDN + adaptive bitrate se milta hai, seedha S3 se nahi.

1. Student video maangta hai

GET /courses/C123/lessons/L456/video

2. Video Playback Service — 4 steps

  1. Enrollment check: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM enrollments WHERE user_id={user_id} AND course_id='C123' — agar 0 hai to 403 'Enroll first' return karo. Bina enroll kiye video nahi milegi.
  2. Video URL nikalo: SELECT video_url FROM lessons WHERE lesson_id='L456' → https://cdn.example.com/.../master.m3u8
  3. CloudFront signed URL banao (24 ghante me expire, unauthorized sharing rokta hai): signedURL = cloudfront.getSignedUrl({url: video_url, expires: now() + 86400})
  4. Response: {videoURL: signedURL, thumbnailURL, duration: 1200, variants: [1080p, 720p, 480p, 360p]}

3. Client-side playback — adaptive bitrate kaise kaam karta hai

  1. Player HLS manifest load karta hai (Video.js, Plyr): fetch(signedURL) → master.m3u8
  2. Adaptive bitrate: Player pehle segment ki download speed se bandwidth naapta hai — tez connection (10 Mbps) → 1080p, slow connection (1 Mbps) → 480p. Beech me bandwidth badle to mid-stream switch hota hai, bina buffering ke, seamlessly.
  3. Segment loading: Player .ts segments ek ke baad ek fetch karta hai — segment_0.ts (6 sec), segment_1.ts, … aur 30 sec aage buffer karta hai taaki playback smooth rahe.

4. CDN optimization (CloudFront)

CheezDetail
Edge locations200+ — user ko nearest edge par route kiya jata hai (<50ms latency)
Cache TTLSegments 30 din (immutable — kabhi badalte nahi), manifests 1 ghanta
Cache hit rate95%+ popular courses ke liye — matlab 95 baar me se origin (S3) tak jaana hi nahi padta

5. Startup time ka hisaab — 850ms (target <2000ms)

StepTime
DNS lookup50ms
TCP/TLS handshake100ms
Manifest download200ms
First segment download (6 sec ≈ 2 MB at 480p)500ms
Total~850ms — <2sec target se bahut neeche

Optimization: Pehla segment intro dikhate hue preload karo, HTTP/2 connection reuse karo — har segment par naya handshake nahi.

Warning — CDN ke bina target fail
CRITICAL: Video <2sec startup ke liye HLS adaptive bitrate + CDN MUST hai. 4 resolutions (1080p/720p/480p/360p), 6-sec segments, CloudFront 200+ edges chahiye. Bina CDN ke S3 latency globally 200–500ms hoti hai — requirement fail ho jayega.
Interview Favorite — HLS adaptive bitrate
HLS adaptive bitrate: master.m3u8 me 4 variant playlists hoti hain, player pehle segment se bandwidth naapta hai, resolution select karta hai (tez → 1080p, slow → 360p), beech me seamlessly switch karta hai. Slow network wale user ko buffering ki jagah kam quality milti hai (kam bandwidth). CDN segments 30 din cache karta hai (95% hit rate).

Step 3: Progress Tracking

Millions of students video dekh rahe hain — har ek ki position track karni hai, resume kaam karna chahiye, aur 95% dekhne par lesson complete hona chahiye. Ye sab Kafka + upsert se hota hai, sync DB write se nahi.

Progress record kaisa dikhta hai

progress_level: course_id, video_id (agar lesson video hai), max_position_sec (sabse door tak dekha hua point), last_position_sec (resume point), status, metadata

1. Video watch events — heartbeat + batching

  1. Heartbeat har 10 sec: Client bhejta hai {courseId: 'C123', lessonId: 'L456', videoId: 'V789', position: 120 (seconds), action: 'WATCHING', timestamp}
  2. Batching: Client 3 events (30 sec total) ikatthe karke bhejta hai: POST /progress/events with {events: [{position: 120}, {position: 130}, {position: 140}]} — API calls 3× kam (1 event/10sec ki jagah 1 batch/30sec)

2. User Progress Service

8. Scaling & Optimization — 12 techniques

Scale par system ko zinda rakhne ke liye 12 techniques hain. Har ek ek alag bottleneck ko solve karti hai — bandwidth, CPU, DB load, cost ya latency. Interview me inme se 3–4 poochna pakka hai.

Technique 1 — CDN video delivery

CloudFront 200+ edge locations, 95% cache hit rate, <2sec startup time (HLS adaptive streaming), S3 egress cost 95% kam hoti hai. Video ka load origin par nahi, edge par jata hai — isiliye millions concurrent viewers possible hain.

Technique 2 — Async video processing

S3 event → Lambda → Kafka → Worker pool (Spot instances 70% saste), transcoding 10–15 min (4 resolutions), instructor ko complete hone par notify kiya jata hai. Upload aur processing alag hain — instructor wait nahi karta.

Technique 3 — Progress event batching

Client 3 events har 30 sec me batch karta hai (1 event/10sec ki jagah), API calls 3× kam. Kafka millions events/day sambhalta hai, 100 partitions ke saath.

Technique 4 — Database read replicas

1 master (writes: enrollments, payments) + 5 replicas (reads: course listing, progress queries). Read/write split se millions of queries scale hote hain — kyunki is system me reads writes se bahut zyada hain.

Technique 5 — Denormalized stats

course_stats table (avg_rating, total_reviews, students_enrolled) Kafka se async update hoti hai. Course listing queries tez hoti hain kyunki COUNT aggregation nahi karni padti.

Technique 6 — Elasticsearch for search

Title/description/tags par full-text search, category/price ke liye aggregations, autocomplete ke liye ngram tokenizer. Complex queries <100ms me handle hoti hain — SQL LIKE se kabhi nahi hota.

Technique 7 — Redis caching

Course details (1 hour TTL), enrollment check (24 hours), user preferences (24 hours). 90% cache hit rate se DB load 10× kam ho jata hai. Enrollment check <100ms target isi se milta hai.

Technique 8 — Payment idempotency

enrollment_request_id (client-generated UUID), Stripe deduplicate karta hai, Kafka consumer enrollment se pehle DB check karta hai. Retry ya duplicate webhook par double enrollment nahi hoti.

Technique 9 — HLS adaptive bitrate

4 resolutions (1080p/720p/480p/360p), 6-sec segments, player bandwidth ke hisaab se beech me switch karta hai. Slow network wale users ko 360p milta hai (kam bandwidth) — buffering ki jagah kam quality.

Technique 10 — Database sharding

Progress table user_id se sharded hai (ek user ki progress ek shard par), Payments created_at se sharded hai (time-based, historical queries ke liye). Billions of records tak scale hota hai.

Technique 11 — S3 lifecycle policies

Raw videos 90 din baad Glacier me archive ho jate hain ($0.004/GB vs $0.023/GB), transcoded videos S3 me rehte hain (frequently accessed). Purane content par storage cost 80% bachta hai.

Technique 12 — Kafka consumer auto-scaling

Lag ke basis par auto-scale — agar lag > 1000 messages ho to instances add karo. 100 partitions se 100 parallel consumers chal sakte hain, throughput linearly scale hota hai.

Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: Har technique ko ek problem se jodo — CDN = bandwidth, batching = API load, replicas = read load, denormalization = aggregation cost, Elasticsearch = search, Redis = DB load, idempotency = correctness, ABR = user experience, sharding = data volume, Glacier = storage cost, auto-scaling = spikes. Problem → solution bolna hi system design hai.

Q3: Payment succeed hone par bhi webhook duplicate ya lost ho to double enrollment kaise rokte ho?

Ye question payment idempotency ka test hai — is lesson ka sabse tricky hissa. Jawab 5 safeguards me do, aur end me bolo: "saare raste exactly-once enrollment par milte hain."

Stripe payment_intent.succeeded webhook bhejta hai. Payment Service: signature verify karta hai (fake webhook rokne ke liye), DB update karta hai (payment status='COMPLETED'), aur Kafka me payment.completed publish karta hai. (3) Enrollment consumer idempotency

Consumer payment.completed consume karta hai, phir DB check karta hai: SELECT COUNT(*) WHERE user_id AND course_id. Agar count > 0 hai → already enrolled → SKIP (duplicate enrollment nahi). Agar count = 0 hai → INSERT enrollment (pehli baar). UNIQUE constraint (user_id, course_id) race conditions rokta hai.

Payment succeed ho gaya (Stripe status='succeeded') lekin webhook delay ho gaya ya kho gaya. Saare raste converge karte hain: DB check duplicate enrollment rokta hai, Stripe idempotency duplicate charge rokti hai, aur Kafka consumer out-of-order/duplicate events handle karta hai.

FAQs — 36 Interview Questions

Interview me is design par sabse zyada poochhe jaane wale 36 questions — lesson ke 3 bade Q&A se shuru karke, har concept par. Har answer me wahi numbers aur logic hai jo lesson me hai.

Q01 HLS kya hai, aur ye DASH se alag kaise hai?
HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) video ko chhote-chhote .ts segments (yahan 6-sec) me tod kar HTTP se deliver karta hai, saath me .m3u8 playlist hoti hai jo batati hai segments kis order me hain. DASH bhi aisa hi karta hai lekin HLS Apple ka standard hai aur har device/browser me native support milta hai — isiliye is lesson me HLS use hua. Dono ka core idea ek hai: segment karo, playlist banao, player bandwidth ke hisaab se quality chuno.
Q02 Adaptive bitrate streaming kaise kaam karta hai?
Player pehle master.m3u8 load karta hai, jisme 4 variant playlists hoti hain (1080p/720p/480p/360p). Pehle segment ki download speed se player bandwidth naapta hai — tez hai to 1080p, slow hai to 480p. Beech me bandwidth badle to player agle segment se doosri quality par switch kar deta hai, bina video roke. User ko buffering ki jagah kam quality milti hai — yehi adaptive bitrate hai.
Q03 6-sec segments kyun? Chhote ya bade segment ka kya trade-off hai?
6 sec ek balance hai. Segment chhota (2 sec) ho to switching tez hoti hai lekin har segment par HTTP overhead badhta hai aur CDN par files zyada hoti hain. Segment bada (10 sec) ho to overhead kam lekin quality switch karne me 10 sec lag jayenge aur startup slow hoga. 6 sec me startup ~850ms rehta hai aur switching bhi smooth hoti hai. Pehle segment ko 4-sec rakh kar startup aur tez kiya ja sakta hai.
Q04 Presigned URL kya hota hai? Video seedha server se upload kyun nahi karte?
Presigned URL ek time-limited signed link hota hai jo client ko seedha S3 me upload karne deta hai — server beech me nahi aata. Server se upload karoge to 5 GB video pehle server par aayegi, phir S3 me jayegi — server ka bandwidth aur time dono waste, aur server crash ka risk. Presigned URL se upload tez hota hai, server free rehta hai, aur URL 1 ghante me expire ho jata hai to misuse nahi hota.
Q05 Chunked aur resumable upload kaise kaam karta hai?
Badi file ko chhote chunks (jaise 5–10 MB) me tod kar ek-ek karke upload kiya jata hai. Har chunk ka alag request hota hai. Agar network beech me toot jaye to sirf wahi chunk dobara bhejna padta hai jo fail hua — poori 5 GB file dobara nahi. S3 multipart upload isi tarah kaam karta hai, aur client ko pata hota hai kaunse chunks ho chuke hain.
Q06 Transcoding async kyun hoti hai? Sync kyun nahi?
2 GB video ko 4 resolutions me transcode + HLS packaging + thumbnails nikalne me 10–15 min lagte hain. Agar ye sync hota to instructor ka upload request 15 min tak atka rehta — timeout ho jata, server thread block hota. Async flow me instructor ko turant confirmation milta hai, kaam peeche Kafka worker par hota hai, aur complete hone par notify kar diya jata hai.
Q07 CloudFront signed URL aur S3 presigned URL me farak kya hai?
Dono ka kaam access control hai, lekin jagah alag hai. S3 presigned URL upload ke liye hota hai — client ko S3 bucket me likhne ki temporary permission deta hai (1 ghanta). CloudFront signed URL playback ke liye hota hai — student ko CDN se video dekhne ki permission deta hai (24 ghante). Ek likhne ke liye, doosra padhne ke liye — dono expire hote hain taaki link share karke misuse na ho.
Q08 Segments 30 din cache hote hain, manifests 1 ghanta — kyun alag TTL?
Segments immutable hote hain — ek baar bana to kabhi badalta nahi, isiliye 30 din cache karna safe hai. Manifest (.m3u8) me video ki list hoti hai; agar video dobara process ho ya naya variant add ho to manifest badal sakta hai, isiliye uska TTL sirf 1 ghanta hai. Immutable cheez ko lamba cache karo, badalne wali cheez ko chhota — ye caching ka basic rule hai.
Q09 last_position_sec aur max_position_sec me farak kya hai? Dono kyun chahiye?
last_position_sec batata hai user ne video aakhri baar kahan chhodi thi — resume isi se hota hai. max_position_sec batata hai user sabse door tak kahan pahuncha tha — completion isi se check hoti hai (95% par). Agar sirf last hota to user rewind karke wapas aaye to system samajhta wo peeche hai, aur completion galat hoti. Dono alag isiliye hain kyunki resume aur completion do alag sawaal hain.
Q10 Lesson 95% dekhne par complete kyun hota hai, 100% par kyun nahi?
Video ke aakhri kuch seconds me aksar credits ya outro hota hai, aur network glitch se aakhri segment kabhi miss bhi ho sakta hai. 100% maangoge to bahut se students kabhi complete nahi honge. 95% ek practical threshold hai — itna dekh liya matlab content samajh liya. Isiliye completion check hai: max_position_sec >= duration_sec × 0.95.
Q11 Heartbeat har 10 sec aur batching har 30 sec — ye numbers kyun?
Heartbeat 1 sec ka hota to 1M students se 60M events/min aate — system doob jata. 10 sec par resume accuracy kaafi hai (10 sec peeche se resume karna acceptable hai). 3 events batch karke 30 sec me bhejne se API calls 3× kam ho jati hain. Ye numbers accuracy aur cost ke beech ka balance hain — zyada accuracy chahiye to cost badhegi, kam accuracy se resume kharab hoga.
Q12 Kafka topic video.progress ke 100 partitions kyun hain?
Partitions parallelism decide karte hain. 100 partitions ka matlab 100 consumers ek saath kaam kar sakte hain — throughput linearly badhta hai. Partitioning user_id hash se hoti hai, taaki ek user ke saare events ek hi partition me jayein aur order bana rahe. Yahan 10 consumer instances chal rahe hain (har ek 10 partitions sambhalta hai); load badhe to 100 tak badha sakte ho.
Q13 Upsert idempotent kaise hai? GREATEST() kyun use hota hai?
Upsert INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE hai — wahi event do baar aaye to bhi final state ek jaisi rehti hai, isiliye idempotent hai. max_position_sec ke liye GREATEST(existing, new) use hota hai taaki purana bada value naye chhote value se overwrite na ho — jaise user 500s se rewind karke 140s par aaye to max 500 hi rehna chahiye. Seedha overwrite karte to rewind par progress peeche chala jata.
Q14 Consumer restart ho jaye to events dobara process hone par kya hota hai?
Kuch nahi bigadta — upsert idempotent hai, wahi event dobara aaye to wahi final state banti hai. last_position_sec latest value se update hota hai, max_position_sec GREATEST se safe rehta hai, aur completion check 95% par wahi result deta hai. Isiliye Kafka consumer ko restart ya lag se darne ki zaroorat nahi — at-least-once delivery + idempotent processing = exactly-once effect.
Q15 Enrollment check <100ms kaise milta hai?
Enrollment check Redis me cache hota hai — 24 hours TTL ke saath. Student ne enroll kiya hai ya nahi, ye sawaal har video play par poochha jata hai; har baar DB query karoge to 100ms target kabhi nahi milega. Redis se jawab microseconds me milta hai, aur 90% cache hit rate se DB load 10× kam ho jata hai. Enrollment badalte hi cache invalidate kar diya jata hai.
Q16 Course listing <200ms kaise milti hai?
Teen cheezein mil kar deti hain: (1) course_stats denormalized table — rating aur enrolled count ke liye COUNT/AVG aggregation nahi karni padti, (2) read replicas — listing queries 5 replicas par baant di jati hain, master par load nahi, (3) Redis caching — course details 1 ghante ke liye cache hote hain. Search ke liye Elasticsearch alag se hai, jo <100ms me complex queries handle karta hai.
Q17 Stripe payment intent kya hai? client_secret ka kya use hai?
Payment intent Stripe par ek payment ka record hota hai — amount (cents me, jaise 4999 = $49.99), currency, aur status (requires_payment_method → succeeded). client_secret ek temporary key hai jo browser me Stripe.js ko di jati hai — isse card details Stripe tak securely pahunchte hain, card number kabhi apne server par nahi aata (PCI compliance ka fayda). Payment confirm hone par Stripe webhook bhejta hai.
Q18 enrollment_request_id double charge kaise rokta hai?
Ye client-generated UUID hai jo payment intent ke metadata me jata hai — Stripe ke liye idempotency key ka kaam karta hai. User Enroll do baar dabaye to dono baar wahi ID jati hai, aur Stripe pehchan leta hai ki ye wahi request hai — naya charge karne ki jagah existing payment_intent return kar deta hai. Isiliye double-click se double charge nahi hota.
Q19 Webhook signature verify karna kyun zaroori hai?
Webhook ek HTTP POST hai — koi bhi fake webhook bhej kar keh sakta hai 'payment ho gaya'. Signature verify karne se pata chalta hai ki webhook asal me Stripe se aaya hai (Stripe apne secret se sign karta hai). Bina verify kiye enrollment de doge to koi bhi bina paise diye course access le lega. Ye payment security ka pehla rule hai.
Q20 Webhook kho jaye to polling backup kaise kaam karta hai?
Payment Service har 5 min me un payments ko check karta hai jo PENDING me atki hain — Stripe API se GET /v1/payment_intents/{id} karke. Agar Stripe par status 'succeeded' hai lekin local DB me 'PENDING' hai, to service manually DB update karti hai aur Kafka me payment.completed publish karti hai — bilkul wahi flow jo webhook se hota. Isse stuck payments 5 min ke andar pakde jate hain.
Q21 UNIQUE (user_id, course_id) constraint kyun zaroori hai? Race condition kya hoti hai?
Do webhook events ek hi waqt par aaye to dono consumer SELECT karenge, dono ko count 0 milega, aur dono INSERT karne chal padenge — DB check akela race condition nahi rok sakta. UNIQUE constraint database level par rokta hai: pehla INSERT pass hoga, doosra fail hoga. Application-level check + database constraint dono mil kar hi double enrollment ko poori tarah rokte hain.
Q22 Payment ke liye ACID aur video ke liye availability — ye CAP trade-off samjhao.
Ek hi system me do alag trade-off hain. Payment me consistency sabse important hai — double charge ya double enrollment kabhi acceptable nahi, isiliye ACID transactions aur idempotency use hoti hai, thodi latency chalegi. Video streaming me availability sabse important hai — video rukna nahi chahiye, thoda stale data (jaise purana thumbnail) chalega, isiliye CDN + cache + eventual consistency. Har component ka CAP choice uske use-case se nikalta hai, ek jaisa nahi hota.
Q23 70/30 revenue split kaise implement hota hai? Stripe Connect kya hai?
Instructor Stripe Connect se apna bank account jodta hai. Jab student $49.99 pay karta hai, to platform 30% ($15) rakhta hai aur 70% ($34.99) instructor ke Stripe Connect account me transfer hota hai. Stripe Connect hi payouts, tax forms aur fraud checks sambhalta hai — platform ko khud paise baantne ka system nahi banana padta.
Q24 Refund 30-day window kaise handle hoti hai?
Student 30 din ke andar refund maang sakta hai. Payment Service Stripe par refund initiate karta hai, payment ka status REFUNDED mark hota hai, aur enrollment revoke ho jati hai (course access band). 30 din ke baad refund nahi milta — ye business rule hai, aur payment status enum me REFUNDED isiliye hai.
Q25 Quiz auto-grading ka formula kya hai? Pass 70% kyun hai?
Har sahi MCQ answer ke points judte hain: total_score = SUM(scores). Phir percentage = (total_score / total_points) × 100, aur passed = percentage >= pass_score (70%). 70% ek standard threshold hai — itna score matlab student ne concept samjha. Example: 100 points me se 85 mile to percentage 85.0, passed = true.
Q26 Retake ke beech 24 ghante ka cooldown kyun hai?
Cooldown brute force rokta hai. Agar turant retake mile to student har baar guess karke answers try karega aur aakhir me pass ho jayega — quiz ka matlab khatm. 24 ghante ka gap force karta hai ki student dobara padhe, phir aaye. Saath me attempts bhi 3 hi hain — dono mil kar quiz ki credibility bachate hain.
Q27 TEXT aur FILL type ke questions auto-grade kyun nahi hote?
MCQ ka answer fixed hota hai — A sahi hai ya B, machine check kar sakti hai. TEXT/FILL me student apne shabdon me jawab likhta hai — uski correctness judge karne ke liye samajh chahiye, jo sirf instructor (ya ek LLM grader) kar sakta hai. Isiliye in questions ke attempts ka status manual grading tak PENDING rehta hai.
Q28 Search ke liye Elasticsearch kyun, SQL LIKE kyun nahi?
SQL LIKE '%python%' har row ko scan karta hai — 100K courses par slow hoga, aur typo, ranking, autocomplete kuch nahi dega. Elasticsearch inverted index par kaam karta hai: title/description/tags par full-text search, relevance ranking, category/price ke liye aggregations, aur ngram tokenizer se autocomplete — sab <100ms me. Search ek alag problem hai, isiliye alag engine chahiye.
Q29 Read replicas kaise kaam karte hain? 1 master + 5 replicas ka matlab?
Saare writes (enrollments, payments) master par jate hain, aur master unhe 5 replicas par copy karta hai. Saare reads (course listing, progress queries) replicas par baant diye jate hain — isiliye read load 5× baant jata hai. Ek caveat hai: replication lag — master par likha data replica par aane me milliseconds lag sakte hain, isiliye enrollment ke turant baad ki listing me thoda stale data dikh sakta hai. Ye eventual consistency acceptable hai.
Q30 Progress user_id se shard hoti hai, Payments created_at se — alag kyun?
Sharding key query pattern se nikalti hai. Progress ki queries hamesha 'ek user ki progress' hoti hain — user_id se shard karoge to ek user ka saara data ek shard par milega, ek query me kaam ho jayega. Payments ki queries zyada tar time-based hoti hain ('is mahine ke payments', 'pichhle saal ka revenue') — created_at se shard karoge to purana data archive karna aur time-range queries dono aasan hote hain.
Q31 S3 lifecycle: raw video Glacier me kyun, transcoded video kyun nahi?
Raw (original) video 90 din ke baad rarely access hoti hai — transcoding ho chuki, ab sirf backup ke liye chahiye. Glacier $0.004/GB hai vs S3 $0.023/GB — 80% bachat. Transcoded videos (master.m3u8 + segments) roz access hote hain — unhe Glacier me daaloge to har play par retrieval time aur cost badhegi. Access pattern ke hisaab se storage tier chuno.
Q32 Transcoding ke liye Spot instances safe kyun hain?
Spot instances 70% saste hote hain kyunki cloud provider unhe kabhi bhi wapas le sakta hai. Transcoding ke liye ye safe hai kyunki kaam idempotent hai — worker beech me mare to Kafka event dobara consume hoga aur doosra worker wahi kaam phir se karega. Koi data loss nahi, bas thoda delay. Isiliye bursty, retry-safe kaam Spot par chalana cost ka best faisla hai.
Q33 Kafka consumer lag ke basis par auto-scale kaise karta hai?
Lag matlab: topic me kitne messages abhi tak process nahi hue. Agar lag 1000 se zyada ho jaye to auto-scaler naye consumer instances add kar deta hai — 100 partitions hain to 100 tak consumers chal sakte hain. Lag kam ho jaye to instances hata diye jate hain. Isse peak time (jaise shaam ko sab video dekhte hain) par bhi progress updates time par hote hain, aur off-peak me cost bachti hai.
Q34 Instructor verification kyun zaroori hai? Bina iske kya problem hai?
Bina verification ke koi bhi fake instructor ban kar low-quality ya copied course bech sakta hai, paise lekar gayab ho sakta hai. Verification (identity check + Stripe Connect payout setup) se pata chalta hai ki instructor asal vyakti hai aur paise sahi account me jayenge. Ye students ka trust aur platform ki reputation dono bachata hai.
Q35 Pehle course par admin approval kyun chahiye?
Pehla course ek test hai — admin check karta hai ki content quality theek hai, copyrighted material to nahi, aur instructor genuine hai. Ek baar instructor verify ho jaye aur pehla course approve ho jaye, to aage ke courses seedha publish ho sakte hain. Ye fraud aur spam courses ko platform par aane se pehle rokta hai.
Q36 Certificate kab milta hai? 100% completion kaise pata chalta hai?
Jab course ke saare lessons complete ho jayein — har video 95%+ dekhi ho aur saare quizzes pass hon — to enrollment ka completion_status 100.00% ho jata hai. Tab system certificate generate karta hai. Completion ka hisaab progress table se aata hai: completed lessons / total lessons. Isiliye progress tracking sahi hona certificate ke liye bhi zaroori hai.
