01Payment Gateway

Functional Requirements

Payment gateway ka kaam simple lagta hai — user card se paise de, merchant ko paise mil jayein — lekin beech me jo kuch hota hai, wo ek poora orchestration layer hai. Lesson ka one-liner yaad rakhne layak hai: PCI-DSS compliant orchestration layer → Tokenization + Idempotency → Multi-processor routing with reconciliation. Isi ek line me poora system chhupa hai: security, duplicate-charge se bachav, aur kayi processors ke beech routing.

Functional requirements wo 6 kaam hain jo system ko karna hi chahiye. Interview me FR bolte waqt sirf list mat ginao — har feature ke peeche ka "kyu" bhi bolo.

Feature 1 — Payment requests initiate karna

Merchant ya client payment shuru karta hai — amount, currency aur merchant details ke saath. Ye entry point hai; yahin se PaymentIntent banta hai.

Feature 2 — Temporary checkout sessions

Payment details ke saath ek temporary checkout session banta hai. Session ka matlab hai: user ko ek checkout page/URL milta hai jahan wo card details bharta hai. Session temporary hota hai — 30 minute me expire ho jata hai (aage Section 02 aur 09 me detail).

Feature 3 — PCI-DSS compliant card handling (tokenization)

Card data kabhi raw form me application database me store nahi hota. Card number ko token me badal diya jata hai — ye poore system ki sabse important security requirement hai. Tokenization ka poora flow Section 06, Step 3 me hai.

Feature 4 — Transaction status tracking

Har transaction ki status track hoti hai: pending processing success failed. User aur merchant dono ko har waqt pata hona chahiye ki payment kahan atka hai.

Feature 5 — Partial payments aur refunds

Poora refund hi nahi — partial refund bhi support karna hota hai. Example: ₹100 ke order me se ₹30 refund, phir ₹20 refund — bacha hua ₹50 ab bhi refundable hai. Refund ka poora workflow Section 11 ke Q&A me hai.

Feature 6 — Webhook notifications

Payment ki status badalte hi merchant ko async notification jati hai — webhook ke through. Ye polling ka replacement hai: merchant ko baar-baar status poochhne ki zaroorat nahi.

Interview tip: FR likhte waqt "user kya kar sakta hai" se socho — initiate, checkout, track, refund, notify. Yehi 6 features har payment gateway (Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal) me common hain.

02Payment Gateway

Non-Functional Requirements

Non-functional requirements batate hain ki system kis scale aur kis quality par chalega. Payment system me NFR sirf "fast hona chahiye" nahi hota — yahan har number ke peeche paisa aur trust juda hai. Ek galat charge ya ek ghante ka downtime seedha revenue aur reputation par chot karta hai.

Scale

MetricTargetMatlab kya hai
Transaction Volume10,000+ TPSHar second 10,000 se zyada transactions — ye sustained load hai, peak par 50,000 TPS tak jata hai (Section 12).
MerchantsLakhs me accountsHar merchant ka alag account, alag API key, alag config — system multi-tenant hai.
Daily TransactionsCrores per day10,000 TPS × 86,400 seconds = din bhar me ~86 crore transactions ki capacity — isliye sharding aur Kafka zaroori hain.

Performance

MetricTargetMatlab kya hai
Latency< 200msPayment authorization ka response 200ms ke andar — iska hisaab Section 12 me step-by-step diya hai (20 + 50 + 100 + 30 = 200ms).
ThroughputHigh concurrencyHazaaron payments ek saath process honi chahiye — stateless services aur connection pooling se milta hai.
Availability99.99% uptimeSaal me sirf 52 minute downtime allowed. Hisaab: 0.01% × 365 din × 24 ghante × 60 minute = 52.56 minute.

Reliability & Security

RequirementDecisionKyu
ConsistencyStrong consistency (CP)Paise ke mamle me "eventually consistent" nahi chalega — ek payment ya to hua ya nahi hua; double-charge ya lost payment ka risk nahi le sakte. Isliye PostgreSQL synchronous replication.
SecurityEnd-to-end encryption, PCI-DSS Level 1Card data ki security par koi compromise nahi — tokenization + HSM + encrypted vault.
IdempotencyDuplicate charge roknaNetwork fail ho ya user double-click kare — ek hi payment ek hi baar charge honi chahiye. Poora mechanism Section 09 me hai.
ReconciliationDaily settlement matchingRoz processor ki files se apne records milaye jate hain — paisa kahin leak na ho. 99.8% auto-match rate target hai.
Interview Favorite

Interviewer zaroor poochhega: "Strong consistency kyu, eventual kyu nahi?" Jawab: payment transactions ke liye CP chahiye — paise ka record galat ya duplicate nahi ho sakta. Lekin webhooks ke liye eventual consistency theek hai (Kafka + async retry), kyunki notification thodi der baad bhi chalegi. Ye CAP theorem ka trade-off dikhata hai — aur interviewer yehi sunna chahta hai.

Core Entity

Core entities wo 7 cheezein hain jinke aas-paas poora system ghumta hai. Interview me jab tumhe "entities batao" kaha jaye, to in 7 ko inke relationships ke saath bolo — kaun kisko point karta hai, ye bhi.

EntityKya haiKey baatein
Merchant / ClientsBusiness accounts jo payment initiate karte hainAPI keys se authenticate hote hain; har merchant ki apni config, rate limit aur settlement settings hoti hain.
TransactionCore payment recordAmount, status, timestamps — yehi wo record hai jo processor se reconcile hota hai. Status: pending, authorized, captured, failed, refunded.
PaymentMethodCard details aur billing infoRaw card kabhi yahan nahi rehta — sirf tokenized reference (token + last4 + brand) store hota hai.

04Payment Gateway

API Designing

API design me 7 endpoints hain — 4 payment operations aur 3 management operations. Saare endpoints /v1/ prefix ke saath versioned hain, taaki aage breaking changes bina purane merchants ko tode laaye ja sakein.

Payment Operations

EndpointKaam
POST /v1/payment-intentsPayment intent banao — amount, currency, merchant details ke saath. Response me intent_id aur client_secret milta hai.
POST /v1/payment-sessionsCheckout session banao — cart details, success_url aur cancel_url ke saath. Response me checkout URL milta hai.
POST /v1/checkout/{pay}Checkout process karo — payment method aur session ID ke saath. Yahi wo call hai jo asal charge trigger karti hai.
GET /v1/payments/{payment_id}Payment ki status aur details nikalo — polling aur webhook fallback dono ke liye use hota hai.

Management Operations

EndpointKaam
POST /v1/refundsCompleted payment ka refund shuru karo — full ya partial, idempotency key ke saath.
POST /v1/webhooksWebhook endpoint register karo — merchant batata hai ki status updates kahan bhejne hain.
GET /v1/reconciliation/reportSettlement aur reconciliation report nikalo — matched, unmatched aur discrepancies ke saath.

Design notes (interview me bolo): (1) Har mutating request ke saath Idempotency-Key header bhejta hai client — retry par duplicate charge nahi hota. (2) Amount hamesha cents/paise me integer hota hai (10000 = $100.00) — float use karne se rounding errors aate hain, paise ke mamle me ye fatal hai. (3) Currency ISO 4217 code me hoti hai — USD, EUR, INR. (4) client_secret frontend ko diya jata hai taaki browser se safe tareeke se payment confirm ho sake, bina secret API key expose kiye.

High level design me 8 bade components hain. Flow ko left-to-right samjho: user/merchant → API Gateway → alag-alag services → processors aur databases. Neeche wala design diagram isi HLD ko whiteboard par dikhata hai — diagram me kuch detail aisi bhi hai jo text me nahi likhi (connector services, PCI Zone ka andar ka flow, sample payloads). Wo detail Section 06 me "diagram se" mark karke di gayi hai.

ComponentKya karta hai
Clients / Merchants → API Gateway + Load BalancerEntry point — rate limiting aur authentication yahin hoti hai. Har request pehle yahan check hoti hai, phir andar jati hai.
PaymentIntent Service → PaymentIntent DBPayment intentions manage karta hai aur Payment Intent Queue me event daalta hai. Data PostgreSQL me rehta hai (strong consistency).
PaymentSession Service → RedisCheckout sessions banata aur validate karta hai — expiry ke saath. Redis isliye, kyunki session temporary hai aur TTL se auto-cleanup hota hai.
Checkout Backend Service → PostgreSQLSession, intent aur merchant match validate karta hai; state transitions manage karta hai (requires_payment_method → processing → succeeded/failed).
Payment Processor Service → External ProcessorsAsal processors (Visa, Razorpay, Mastercard) se baat karta hai — connector ke through.
Orchestrator → Multiple ServicesPayment flow coordinate karta hai aur processor select karta hai — card brand, amount aur merchant config dekh kar.
Reconciliation Service → Merchant Register + KafkaRoz settlement matching karta hai aur discrepancy detect karta hai.

Ye saari detail sirf diagram se li gayi hai — text me iska zikr nahi hai.

06Payment Gateway

Deep Dive Design (Low Level)

Deep dive me payment ka poora safar 7 steps me bataya gaya hai — intent creation se lekar daily reconciliation tak. Har step me kaun kisko kya bhejta hai, kya validate hota hai, aur kya store hota hai — yehi LLD hai. Interview me isi flow ko whiteboard par step-by-step banao.

Step 1 — Payment Intent Creation

  1. Client bhejta hai: POST /v1/payment-intents with {amount, currency, merchant_id, metadata}
  2. PaymentIntent Service validate karta hai: merchant exists, amount > 0, currency valid
  3. Service banata hai: PaymentIntent record PostgreSQL me, status = requires_payment_method
  4. Service publish karta hai: intent-created event Payment Intent Queue me
  5. Service return karta hai: {intent_id, client_secret, status, amount, currency}

Step 2 — Checkout Session Creation

  1. Client bhejta hai: POST /v1/payment-sessions with {intent_id, success_url, cancel_url}
  2. PaymentSession Service nikalta hai: intent details PaymentIntent DB se
  3. Service banata hai: session object Redis me, TTL 30 min — {session_id, intent_id, merchant_id, expiry}
  4. Service generate karta hai: checkout URL — https://pay.gateway.com/checkout/sess_{32-char-id}
  5. Service return karta hai: {session_id, checkout_url, expires_at}

Diagram se (sample payload): Redis me session kuch aisi dikhti hai — {intent_id: "pi_123", merchant_id: "m_42", amount: 50000, currency: "INR", theme: "dark", allowed_methods: ["card", "upi"], expires_at: 2025-12-03T10:15:00Z}. Amount paise me hai — 50000 paise = ₹500.

Step 3 — Tokenization (PCI-DSS Zone)

  1. User submit karta hai: card details {card_number: '4111 1111 1111 1111', expiry_month: '12', cvv: '123', billing_address}
  2. Tokenization Service bhejta hai: card data HSM (Hardware Security Module) ko encryption ke liye
  3. HSM generate karta hai: token jaise tok_1A2B3C aur encrypted card Card Vault me store karta hai
  4. Service store karta hai: mapping {token → card data ka hash} secure database me
  5. Service return karta hai: {card_token: 'tok_1A2B3C', last4: '1111', brand: 'visa'} — koi raw card data PCI zone se bahar nahi jata
Warning — PCI-DSS

Kabhi raw card numbers application database me store mat karo. Hamesha HSM ke through tokenization use karo. Ye PCI-DSS compliance ke liye critical hai — violation par $5,000–$100,000/month tak ke fines lag sakte hain.

Step 4 — Checkout Processing

  1. Client call karta hai: POST /v1/checkout/{pay} with {session_id, card_token, save_card: true/false}
  2. Checkout Backend validate karta hai: (1) session Redis me hai aur expire nahi hui, (2) intent valid hai aur merchant match karta hai, (3) session state ACTIVE hai
  3. Service update karta hai: intent status processing PostgreSQL me — idempotency key check ke saath
  4. Service call karta hai: Orchestrator ko {intent_id, card_token, amount, merchant_id} ke saath
  5. Orchestrator select karta hai: sahi processor — card brand, amount aur merchant config dekh kar

Diagram se (sample payload): CheckoutBackend → Orchestrator call POST /v1/payments {intent_id: "pi_123", session_id: "sess_ABC", payment_method: "CARD", card_token: "tok_123", save_card: true/false} aur response {txn_id: "txn_242", intent_id: "pi_123", status: "SUCCESS", processor_ref: "pay_44ff12", amount: 50000, card_brand: "VISA", auth_code: "A12345"}.

Step 5 — Payment Processing

  1. Orchestrator route karta hai: request Payment Processor Service ko (jaise Visa Connector)
  2. Processor Service call karta hai: external processor API — HSM se detokenized card data ke saath
  3. External processor respond karta hai: authorization response {transaction_id, status, auth_code}
  4. Processor Service store karta hai: response database me aur publish karta hai Kafka topic payment.processor.callback par
  5. Response wapas aata hai: Orchestrator → Checkout Backend → intent status update

Step 6 — Status Update & Webhook

  1. Checkout Backend update karta hai: transaction status succeeded ya failed PostgreSQL me
  2. Backend publish karta hai: event Kafka topic par async processing ke liye
  3. Webhook Service consume karta hai: event aur merchant webhook URL nikalta hai config se
  4. Service bhejta hai: POST merchant webhook par {event: 'payment.succeeded', payment_id, amount, status, metadata}
  5. Service implement karta hai: retry logic with exponential backoff (3 attempts: 0s, 30s, 5min)

Step 7 — Reconciliation (Daily)

  1. Reconciliation Service poll karta hai: Checkout Backend DB — pichhle din ki transactions
  2. Service fetch karta hai: processor settlement files connector APIs se
  3. Service match karta hai: internal records ko processor records se transaction_id par
  4. Service detect karta hai: discrepancies (missing, amount mismatch) aur log karta hai Merchant Register DB me
  5. Service generate karta hai: daily reconciliation report {matched, unmatched, discrepancies}
  6. Service trigger karta hai: alerts — unreconciled transactions threshold se zyada hon to
Tip — Reconciliation optional nahi hai

Financial regulations ke hisaab se daily settlement matching zaroori hai. Automated reconciliation + alert system banao jo discrepancies > 0.1% par alert kare — warna revenue leakage hoti hai aur pata bhi nahi chalta.

07Payment Gateway

Client-Side Components

Client side wo 6 components hain jo user ke browser ya merchant ke backend me chalte hain. Payment gateway sirf server ka khel nahi hai — checkout ka aadha experience client par banta hai, aur security ka ek hissa bhi (PCI-compliant form, 3D Secure).

Component 1 — Checkout UI

Embedded iframe ya redirect to hosted payment page. Hosted page ka fayda: card data kabhi merchant ke page par aata hi nahi — PCI scope aur chhota ho jata hai.

Component 2 — Card Input Form

PCI-compliant form with client-side validation — card number ka format (Luhn check), expiry date, CVV length. Galat input server tak jaane se pehle hi pakda jata hai.

Component 3 — Payment Status Poller

GET /v1/payments/{id} ko poll karta hai status updates ke liye. Webhook merchant ke liye hai; user ke browser ke liye polling hi tareeka hai — jab tak status succeeded ya failed na ho jaye.

Component 4 — Webhook Handler

Merchant backend ka endpoint jo async notifications receive karta hai. Ise idempotent hona chahiye — ek hi event do baar aaye to order do baar fulfill na ho.

Component 5 — 3D Secure Handler

Jab additional authentication chahiye (OTP / bank page) to redirect karta hai. High-value ya risky transactions par 3DS mandatory hota hai — isse fraud aur chargebacks dono kam hote hain.

Component 6 — Error Display

User-friendly error messages aur retry options. Lekin dhyaan do — error me processor ki internal detail kabhi mat dikhao (neeche warning dekho).

Warning — Error messages

Client ko processor failures ke baare me detailed error kabhi mat do (jaise "Visa API timeout"). Hamesha generic message do — "Payment failed, please try again". Detailed errors se information leakage hota hai aur attackers ko system ke baare me pata chalta hai.

09Payment Gateway

Idempotency & Retry Mechanism

Ye section poore lesson ka sabse interview-heavy hissa hai. Do mechanisms hain — ek duplicate charge rokta hai (idempotency), doosra failed notifications ko dobara bhejta hai (webhook retry). Dono ko alag-alag samjho, kyunki dono ka purpose alag hai.

Idempotency Implementation

  1. Trigger: Client har request ke saath Idempotency-Key header bhejta hai (ek UUID).
  2. Processing: Backend check karta hai ki key idempotency cache (Redis, TTL 24h) me hai ya nahi.
  3. Cache Hit: Cached response turant return kar do — payment dobara process mat karo.
  4. Cache Miss: Payment normal process karo, aur return karne se pehle {key → response} Redis me store kar do.
  5. Resolution: Network failure ya retry par duplicate charge nahi hota — payment integrity ke liye critical hai.

Database bhi ek aur layer deta hai: idempotency_key column par UNIQUE constraint. Agar do requests ek saath aayein, to pehli row lock leti hai aur process karti hai; doosri ya to wait karti hai ya cached response paati hai. User "Pay" button 5 baar dabaye, tab bhi ek hi charge.

Webhook Retry Logic

  1. Trigger: Payment status change event Kafka par publish hota hai.
  2. Initial Attempt: Webhook service merchant URL par POST bhejta hai, 5s timeout ke saath.
  3. Retry Schedule: Fail ho to retry — [30s, 5min, 1 hour] exponential backoff ke saath.
  4. Handling: 3 failures ke baad webhook ko failed mark karo aur merchant ko alert bhejo.
  5. Offline fallback: Merchant GET /v1/payments/{id} se status khud poochh sakta hai.

Farq samjho: Idempotency request ko duplicate hone se bachata hai; webhook retry notification ko deliver karne ki koshish karta hai. Ek payment ki taraf hai, doosra merchant-notification ki taraf. Dono me retry hai, lekin retry ka matlab alag hai — pehle me retry = wahi response do, doosre me retry = dobara bhejo.

Interview Favorite

Interviewers ALWAYS poochhte hain: 'Duplicate charges kaise rokte ho?' Jawab: Idempotency keys (client-generated UUID) + database UNIQUE constraint + Redis caching (24h TTL). Same key = cached response return karo, dobara process mat karo.

Tip — Redis sessions

Session storage ke liye database ki bajaye Redis use karo. Sessions temporary hain (30 min TTL), read/write frequency bahut high hai (1M+ sessions/hour). Redis TTL auto-cleanup karta hai — manual garbage collection ki zaroorat nahi.

10Payment Gateway

Scaling & Optimization

10,000 TPS tak scale karna koi ek trick nahi hai — 10 techniques ka combination hai. Interview me jab "scale kaise karoge" poochha jaye, to in 10 me se kam se kam 5–6 ka naam aur unka asar (numbers ke saath) bolo.

TechniqueKya hai aur kyu kaam karta hai
1. Database Shardingmerchant_id se partition — har shard 10,000+ merchants handle karta hai. Ek merchant ka saara data ek shard par, to queries fast rehti hain.
2. Redis CachingSession data 30 min TTL ke saath cache — DB reads 85% kam ho jate hain. Sessions aur idempotency keys dono Redis me.
3. Kafka Event StreamingAsync webhook delivery — payment flow notification delivery se decoupled. Payment rukti nahi agar webhook slow ho.
4. Read ReplicasStatus queries read replicas par, writes primary par — 95% traffic read hai, isliye primary par load kam hota hai.
5. Connection PoolingProcessor connector services persistent connections rakhte hain — har request par naya TLS handshake nahi, latency bachti hai.
6. Rate LimitingAPI Gateway 1000 req/min per merchant enforce karta hai — abuse aur accidental flood dono rokte hain.
7. Circuit BreakerProcessor fail ho to backup processor par fallback ya graceful degradation — cascade failure nahi hoti.
8. HSM ScalingMultiple HSM instances load balancing ke saath — 5,000 tokenizations/sec. Tokenization bottleneck na bane, isliye HSM bhi scale hota hai.
9. Dead Letter QueueFailed Kafka messages DLQ me jate hain — manual investigation ke liye. Koi payment event chupchaap kho nahi jata.
10. Database IndexingComposite index (merchant_id, created_at, status) — reconciliation queries fast hoti hain.
Interview Favorite

Interviewers ko ye sawaal bahut pasand hai: '10,000 TPS tak kaise scale karoge?' Jawab: (1) Horizontal scaling — stateless services load balancer ke peeche, (2) Database sharding by merchant_id, (3) Redis sessions/idempotency ke liye, (4) Kafka async processing ke liye, (5) Processors ke liye connection pooling.

Tip — Circuit breaker

Payment processors ke liye circuit breaker pattern critical hai. Agar Visa API fail ho rahi hai, to use retries se hammer mat karo. 5 failures ke baad circuit open → backup processor par route karo ya queue me daalo. Cascade failures rukti hain.

Common Interview Questions

Lesson ke 10 interview Q&A — ye wo sawaal hain jo interviewer is design par sabse zyada poochhta hai. Har jawab ko poora padho; inme se kai concepts (tokenization, idempotency, reconciliation, failover) alag-alag sections me detail me bhi hain. FAQ tab me inke alawa 26 aur interview sawaal hain.

Q1. Card numbers store karne ki bajaye tokenization kyu use karte hain?

PCI-DSS compliance ki requirement hai. Raw card data store karne ke liye Level 1 PCI compliance chahiye, jo bahut expensive aur complex hai. Tokenization ke 3 fayde: (1) PCI scope kam ho jata hai — sirf tokenization service ko full compliance chahiye, (2) Security — token leak bhi ho jaye to bekaar hai, asal card data HSM-protected vault me rehta hai, (3) Flexibility — ek hi token kayi processors par chal jata hai. Tokens one-way mapped hote hain, HSM access ke bina reverse nahi ho sakte.

Q2. Ek hi card se concurrent payment attempts ko kaise handle karte ho?

Database-level locking + idempotency keys. Flow: (1) Client unique idempotency key (UUID) generate karta hai, (2) Backend idempotency_key column par UNIQUE constraint check karta hai, (3) Pehli request row lock leti hai aur process karti hai, (4) Same key wali concurrent requests ya to wait karti hain ya cached response paati hain, (5) Processing ke baad response 24h ke liye Redis me cache hota hai. User "Pay" button kitni bhi baar dabaye — double-charge nahi hota.

Q3. Payment processor API beech me fail ho jaye to kya hota hai?

Multi-layer failure handling: (1) Detection — timeout (30s) ya HTTP 5xx failure trigger karta hai, (2) Circuit Breaker — 5 consecutive failures ke baad 1 minute ke liye backup processor par route karo, (3) Transaction State — failed ki bajaye pending_verification mark karo, (4) Reconciliation — daily job processor se transaction status poochhta hai, (5) Fallback — processor down ho to payment Kafka me queue kar do, baad me process karo, (6) User Experience — hard failure ki bajaye "Payment pending, will update via email" dikhao. Recovery SLA: 99% cases 1 ghante me automated reconciliation se resolve.

Q4. Payment data ki durability kaise ensure karte ho — data loss kaise rokte ho?

Multiple durability layers: (1) Database — PostgreSQL synchronous replication 2 standby replicas ke saath (RPO = 0), (2) WAL Archival — continuous archiving S3 par, point-in-time recovery ke saath, (3) Kafka — payment events replication factor 3, retention 7 din, (4) Processor Records — external processors ke paas apne independent records hain reconciliation ke liye, (5) Backup — roz encrypted snapshots cold storage me (30 din retention). Poora primary datacenter bhi fail ho jaye to zero transaction data loss.

Q5. Reconciliation process kaise kaam karta hai?

Daily batch reconciliation: (1) Fetch — pichhle din ki transactions internal DB se (status = succeeded), (2) Download — har processor (Visa, Mastercard, Razorpay) se settlement files lao, (3) Match — processor_txn_id par join karo, (4) Compare — amount, status, timestamp ka difference check karo, (5) Detect — discrepancies flag karo: processor file me missing, amount mismatch (>1%), status conflict, (6) Alert — finance team ko manual review ke liye notify karo, (7) Resolve — missing transactions ke liye 24h baad automatic retry. Typical match rate: 99.8%, discrepancies 48h me resolve.

Q6. Payment processing ke beech database down ho jaye to?

High availability setup: (1) Primary-Standby — PostgreSQL streaming replication, automatic failover Patroni se <30s me, (2) Health Checks — load balancer har 2s me DB ping karta hai, primary unhealthy ho to standby par route karta hai, (3) In-Flight Transactions — processing state wale payments idempotent hain, safe retry ho sakta hai, (4) Kafka Buffer — DB poori tarah down ho to payment requests Kafka me queue kar do (retention 7 din), DB recover hote hi process karo, (5) Graceful Degradation — hard error ki bajaye "Payment queued, will process shortly" return karo. RTO: 30 seconds, RPO: 0 seconds (koi data loss nahi).

Q7. Per-merchant quota / rate limits kaise handle karte ho?

Multi-tier rate limiting: (1) API Gateway — token bucket algorithm, 1000 req/min per merchant API key, Redis me stored, (2) Circuit Breaker — limit cross ho to 429 + Retry-After header return karo, (3) Quota Enforcement — monthly transaction volume limits processing se pehle check karo (merchant config table me stored), (4) Soft Limits — 80% quota par email warning, 100% par hard stop, (5) Sliding Window — Redis sorted sets me timestamp scoring se accurate window-based counting. Implementation: ZADD merchant:{id}:requests {timestamp} {request_id}, ZREMRANGEBYSCORE se purani entries hatao, ZCARD se current window count karo.

Q8. Optimal session timeout kya hai, aur kyu?

30 minutes, aur reasoning ye hai: (1) User Behavior — 95% checkouts 5 minute me complete ho jate hain, 30 min edge cases cover karta hai (phone interruptions, OTP delays), (2) Security — session hijacking ka window limited rehta hai, (3) Memory — Redis eviction policy (LRU) unbounded growth rokti hai, (4) UX Trade-off — bahut chhota (5 min) = slow forms par user frustrate, bahut lamba (2 ghante) = stale cart data + security risk. Implementation: session creation par Redis TTL set karo, har activity par 10 min auto-extend (payment page view, card enter), maximum 30 min first creation se. Alternative: high-value transactions (>$1000) ke liye chhota timeout (10 min).

Q9. Payment APIs par replay attacks kaise rokte ho?

Multiple layers: (1) Idempotency Keys — har payment request me unique key honi chahiye, TTL 24h ke saath stored; replayed request bina charge kiye cached response deti hai, (2) Timestamp Validation — request me timestamp hona chahiye, 5 minute se purana ho to reject, (3) HMAC Signature — client request ko secret key se sign karta hai, server signature validate karta hai integrity ke liye, (4) Nonce — one-time random value signature me shamil, Redis me stored (TTL 10 min) taaki reuse na ho, (5) HTTPS — TLS 1.3 MITM attacks rokta hai. Full signature scheme: HMAC-SHA256(api_key + timestamp + nonce + request_body), X-Signature header me bheja jata hai.

Q10. Refunds aur partial refunds kaise handle karte ho?

Refund workflow: (1) Validation — original payment status succeeded ho, refund amount remaining balance se zyada na ho, (2) Idempotency — refund requests bhi idempotency keys use karti hain taaki double-refund na ho, (3) Processor Call — processor ki refund API call karo original transaction_id ke saath, (4) State Update — Transaction se linked Refund record banao, remaining_refundable_amount update karo, (5) Settlement — refunds agle din ke settlement cycle me process hote hain, instant nahi, (6) Partial Support — refund history track karo: original_amount = 100, refunded = [30, 20] to remaining = 50. Typical refund processing time: 5–10 business days (processor par depend karta hai).

Interview FAQs — Payment Gateway

Current-era interviews me payment gateway se poochhe jaane wale 36 sawaal — lesson ke 10 Q&A ke alawa 26 aur, jo isi lesson ke concepts par based hain. Kisi bhi sawaal par click karke jawab kholo.

Q01 Payment gateway kya hota hai? Stripe / Razorpay asal me kya karte hain?
Payment gateway ek orchestration layer hai jo merchant, user aur banks/processors ke beech baithta hai. User card details gateway ko deta hai, gateway unhe tokenize karke sahi processor (Visa, Mastercard, Razorpay) tak pahunchata hai, authorization leta hai, aur status merchant ko webhook se batata hai. Merchant ko alag-alag banks se integrate nahi karna padta — ek gateway sab sambhalta hai. Stripe, Razorpay aur PayPal isi category ke products hain.
Q02 Payment gateway aur payment processor me kya farq hai?
Gateway wo layer hai jo merchant aur user se baat karta hai — checkout, tokenization, routing, webhooks. Processor wo entity hai jo asal me bank/card network se authorization leti hai (jaise Visa ya Mastercard ka network, ya Razorpay ka processing backend). Simple: gateway traffic police hai, processor wo raasta hai jahan se paise authorize hote hain. Ek gateway kayi processors se jud sakta hai aur Orchestrator decide karta hai kaunsa processor use ho.
Q03 PaymentIntent kya hai? Sirf Transaction se kaam kyu nahi chalta?
PaymentIntent user ki 'payment karne ki intention' hai — wo object jo poore flow me state track karta hai: requires_payment_method → processing → succeeded / failed / canceled. Transaction us intent ka ek attempt ya record hai. Intent isliye chahiye kyunki ek payment me kayi steps hote hain (session, tokenization, 3DS, processor call) aur beech me user ruk sakta hai ya fail ho sakta hai — intent hi batata hai ki payment abhi kis stage par hai.
Q04 Tokenization kya hai? Ye encryption se kaise alag hai?
Tokenization me card number ko ek random token (jaise tok_1A2B3C) se replace kar diya jata hai, aur asal card sirf HSM-protected vault me rehta hai. Token ka card se koi mathematical relation nahi hota — use reverse nahi kiya ja sakta. Encryption me data ko key se encode kiya jata hai aur key se decode bhi ho sakta hai. Tokenization zyada safe hai kyunki token leak ho bhi jaye to bekaar hai, aur PCI scope bhi chhota ho jata hai — sirf tokenization service ko full compliance chahiye.
Q05 HSM (Hardware Security Module) kya hai? Tokenization me iski kya bhoomika hai?
HSM ek dedicated hardware device hai jo cryptographic keys ko secure tareeke se store karta hai aur encryption/decryption usi ke andar hota hai — keys kabhi HSM se bahar nahi aati. Tokenization flow me card data HSM ko jata hai, HSM token generate karta hai aur card ko AES-256 se encrypt karke Card Vault me bhejta hai. Agar attacker database bhi chura le, to bina HSM ke encrypted data bekaar hai. HSM 5,000 tokenizations/sec handle karta hai aur load balancing se scale hota hai.
Q06 PCI-DSS kya hai? Level 1 compliance ka matlab kya hai?
PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) card data handle karne ke rules ka set hai — encryption, access control, network security, auditing. Level 1 sabse strict level hai, jo saal me 60 lakh se zyada transactions process karne wale merchants/processors ke liye mandatory hai. Raw card store karne par Level 1 chahiye hota hai, jo bahut expensive hai — isiliye gateway tokenization use karte hain taaki unka PCI scope chhota rahe.
Q07 Idempotency key kya hai? Ise client generate karta hai ya server?
Idempotency key ek unique value (UUID) hai jo client har payment request ke saath Idempotency-Key header me bhejta hai. Client generate karta hai — kyunki retry bhi client hi karta hai, to wahi key dobara bhejkar server ko bata sakta hai ki ye wahi request hai. Server key ko Redis me 24h TTL ke saath store karta hai aur idempotency_key column par UNIQUE constraint bhi lagata hai. Same key dobara aaye to cached response return hoti hai, dobara charge nahi hota.
Q08 Double charge ho jaye to asal me kya hota hai? User ko paise kaise wapas milte hain?
Pehli baat — sahi idempotency implementation me double charge hota hi nahi. Lekin agar processor level par duplicate authorization ho jaye, to reconciliation use pakadti hai: daily settlement matching me ek hi intent ke do processor records dikhenge. Ek ko void/refund kiya jata hai aur user ko paise wapas milte hain. Isiliye transaction state ko failed ki bajaye pending_verification mark kiya jata hai — taaki reconciliation use resolve kar sake.
Q09 Authorization aur Capture me kya farq hai?
Authorization ka matlab hai bank ne paise 'hold' kar diye hain — user ke account me amount block ho gaya, lekin merchant ko abhi paise mile nahi. Capture ka matlab hai hold kiye hue paise asal me merchant ke account me transfer ho gaye. Hotels aur cab apps pehle authorize karte hain, phir service ke baad capture karte hain. Lesson ke Transaction status me authorized aur captured alag-alag states isi liye hain.
Q10 Webhook kya hai? Status poochhne (polling) se behtar kyu hai?
Webhook ek HTTP callback hai — payment ki status badalte hi gateway merchant ke registered URL par POST bhejta hai (jaise payment.succeeded event). Polling me merchant ko baar-baar GET /v1/payments/{id} poochhna padta hai, jo wasteful hai aur status late milta hai. Webhook event-driven hai: status badla, turant pata chala. Lekin webhook kabhi 100% reliable nahi hota — isiliye retry logic (30s, 5min, 1h) aur GET fallback dono hote hain.
Q11 Webhook ko secure kaise karte hain? Koi fake webhook bhej de to?
Webhook requests ko sign kiya jata hai — gateway payload ko secret key se HMAC-SHA256 sign karke signature header me bhejta hai. Merchant ka webhook handler signature verify karta hai; galat signature ho to request reject kar deta hai. Iske alawa webhook endpoint HTTPS hona chahiye aur merchant ko event idempotency bhi handle karni chahiye — ek hi event do baar aaye to order do baar fulfill na ho.
Q12 Ek hi webhook event do baar deliver ho jaye to kya hota hai?
Webhook delivery at-least-once hoti hai — retry ki wajah se duplicate delivery normal hai. Isiliye merchant ka webhook handler idempotent hona chahiye: har event ka event_id store karo, aur agar wahi id dobara aaye to use ignore karo. Payment gateway ki taraf se bhi event me payment_id hota hai, jisse merchant check kar sakta hai ki ye payment pehle process ho chuki hai ya nahi.
Q13 Webhook events hamesha order me aate hain kya?
Nahi — events out-of-order aa sakte hain, khaas taur par retry ki wajah se. Example: payment.succeeded se pehle payment.processing ka retry aa sakta hai. Isiliye merchant ko event ke timestamp ya version dekh kar handle karna chahiye — purana event naye state ko overwrite na kare. Har event me payment_id aur status hota hai; handler ko current state check karke hi update karna chahiye.
Q14 3D Secure (3DS) kya hai aur kab trigger hota hai?
3D Secure ek extra authentication layer hai — payment ke beech user ko bank ke page ya OTP par redirect kiya jata hai. Ye high-value transactions, naye devices, ya risky patterns (unusual location, badi amount) par trigger hota hai. 3DS se fraud aur chargebacks dono kam hote hain, kyunki bank khud verify karta hai ki cardholder hi payment kar raha hai. Lesson ke client components me 3D Secure Handler isi kaam ke liye hai.
Q15 Checkout session 30 minute ka hi kyu hota hai?
30 minute ek balance hai: 95% checkouts 5 minute me complete ho jate hain, aur 30 minute phone interruptions ya OTP delays jaise edge cases cover karta hai. Chhota timeout (5 min) slow forms par user ko frustrate karta hai; lamba timeout (2 ghante) stale cart data aur session hijacking ka risk badhata hai. Redis TTL auto-extend bhi hota hai — har activity par 10 minute, maximum 30 minute first creation se. High-value transactions (>$1000) ke liye 10 minute ka timeout use hota hai.
Q16 Session storage ke liye Redis kyu, PostgreSQL kyu nahi?
Sessions temporary hain (30 min TTL), read/write frequency bahut high hai (1M+ sessions/hour), aur unhe strong consistency nahi chahiye — ye teeno cheezein Redis ke liye perfect hain. PostgreSQL me har session ke liye row insert + delete karna expensive hota aur manual garbage collection chahiye hoti. Redis TTL se auto-cleanup karta hai. PaymentIntent aur Transaction jaise permanent records PostgreSQL me rehte hain — wahan strong consistency chahiye.
Q17 Reconciliation kya hai aur ye roz kyu hoti hai?
Reconciliation ka matlab hai apne internal transaction records ko processor ki settlement files se milana — transaction_id par join karke amount, status aur timestamp compare karna. Roz hoti hai kyunki paise ka hisaab roz band hona chahiye: koi transaction processor file me missing to nahi, amount mismatch to nahi. Typical match rate 99.8% hota hai; 0.2% manual review me jata hai aur 48h me resolve hota hai. Financial regulations bhi daily settlement matching maangti hain.
Q18 Settlement aur reconciliation me kya farq hai?
Settlement ka matlab hai paise ka asal movement — processor merchant ke account me paise bhejta hai (usually agle din ke cycle me). Reconciliation ka matlab hai hisaab milana — internal records vs processor records compare karke verify karna ki settlement sahi hua. Settlement paise bhejta hai, reconciliation check karta hai ki sahi paise bheje gaye. Refunds bhi settlement cycle me process hote hain, instant nahi.
Q19 Amount cents/paise me integer kyu store karte hain, float kyu nahi?
Float (decimal) numbers binary me exactly represent nahi hote — 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004 jaisi errors aati hain. Paise ke mamle me 1 paise ki error bhi fatal hai, aur lakhon transactions par ye errors badi rakam ban jati hain. Isiliye amount hamesha smallest unit me integer hota hai — 10000 cents = $100.00, 50000 paise = ₹500. Saari calculations integers par hoti hain, display ke waqt hi divide kiya jata hai.
Q20 Payments me strong consistency kyu chahiye? Eventual consistency kyu nahi chalegi?
Paise ke record me 'eventually correct' nahi chalega — ek payment ya to hui ya nahi hui; beech ki state me double-charge ya lost payment ka risk hai. Isiliye PaymentIntent aur Transaction PostgreSQL me synchronous replication ke saath store hote hain (CP in CAP theorem). Lekin webhooks ke liye eventual consistency theek hai — notification thodi der baad bhi chalegi, isliye Kafka + async retry use hota hai. Ye trade-off interviewer ko dikhana zaroori hai.
Q21 Database sharding merchant_id se kyu karte hain? Koi aur key kyu nahi?
merchant_id se sharding isliye kyunki ek merchant ke saare queries (uske payments, uski reconciliation, uski reports) ek hi shard par rehte hain — cross-shard query nahi chahiye hoti. Har shard 10,000+ merchants handle karta hai. Agar transaction_id se shard karte to ek merchant ke payments kayi shards par bikhre hote aur reporting slow ho jati. Shard key hamesha wo chuno jisse queries ek shard me simat jayein.
Q22 Circuit breaker kaise kaam karta hai? 5 failures ke baad kya hota hai?
Circuit breaker processor calls ko monitor karta hai. 5 consecutive failures ke baad circuit OPEN ho jata hai — 1 minute tak us processor par koi request nahi jati, traffic backup processor par route hota hai ya Kafka me queue hota hai. 1 minute baad circuit HALF-OPEN hota hai aur ek trial request bhejta hai; success hua to CLOSED (normal), fail hua to wapas OPEN. Isse fail ho rahe processor ko retries se hammer nahi karte aur cascade failure rukti hai.
Q23 Processor poori tarah down ho jaye to payment ka kya hota hai?
Payment rukti nahi hai — multi-layer fallback hai. Pehle circuit breaker backup processor par route karta hai. Backup bhi down ho to payment Kafka me queue ho jati hai aur processor wapas aate hi process hoti hai. Transaction ko failed ki bajaye pending_verification mark kiya jata hai, aur daily reconciliation processor se status poochh kar use resolve karti hai. User ko 'Payment pending, will update via email' dikhaya jata hai — hard failure nahi. 99% cases 1 ghante me resolve ho jate hain.
Q24 Rate limiting kaise implement karte hain — token bucket vs sliding window?
Dono Redis par implement hote hain. Token bucket: har merchant ke paas bucket hoti hai jo fixed rate se bhar ti hai (1000 req/min); request aaye to ek token kharch hota hai, bucket khali ho to 429 milta hai. Sliding window: Redis sorted set me har request ka timestamp store hota hai (ZADD), purani entries hatayi jati hain (ZREMRANGEBYSCORE) aur current count (ZCARD) se decide hota hai. Token bucket bursts allow karta hai, sliding window zyada accurate hota hai. Gateway API Gateway level par token bucket aur quota ke liye sliding window dono use karta hai.
Q25 Refund kitne din me aata hai? Partial refund kaise track hota hai?
Refund 5–10 business days me aata hai, kyunki refunds agle din ke settlement cycle me process hote hain — instant nahi. Partial refund track karne ke liye har refund ka alag record banta hai jo Transaction se linked hota hai, aur remaining_refundable_amount update hota hai. Example: original ₹100, refunded [₹30, ₹20] to remaining ₹50. Refund request bhi idempotency key use karti hai taaki double-refund na ho.
Q26 Chargeback kya hai? Isme gateway ka kya role hota hai?
Chargeback tab hota hai jab cardholder apne bank ko bolkar payment reverse karwa deta hai — fraud ya product na milne ki wajah se. Bank paise wapas le leta hai aur merchant par fee bhi lagti hai. Gateway ka role hai evidence dena (transaction records, webhook logs, 3DS proof) aur merchant ko chargeback notification bhejna. Note: ye concept lesson ke bahar ka hai — lesson me chargeback handling cover nahi hai — lekin interview me aksar poochha jata hai, isliye yahan diya gaya hai.
Q27 Replay attack kya hai? HMAC signature kaise banta hai?
Replay attack me attacker ek purani valid request ko pakad kar dobara bhejta hai — jaise wahi payment request dobara, taaki dobara charge ho. Rokne ke liye 5 layers hain: idempotency keys (dobara bhejne par cached response), timestamp validation (5 minute se purani request reject), HMAC signature, nonce (ek baar use hone wali random value), aur TLS 1.3. Signature scheme: HMAC-SHA256(api_key + timestamp + nonce + request_body), X-Signature header me bheja jata hai.
Q28 Nonce kya hai aur iska TTL 10 minute kyu hota hai?
Nonce ek one-time random value hai jo har request ki signature me shamil hoti hai. Server use Redis me 10 minute ke liye store karta hai — agar wahi nonce dobara aaye to request reject ho jati hai. 10 minute isliye kyunki timestamp validation bhi 5 minute ki hai; nonce ka window usse thoda bada rakha jata hai taaki clock skew (client aur server ki ghadi ka farq) se valid requests reject na hon. Nonce ke bina attacker signature wali request ko bhi replay kar sakta tha.
Q29 Kafka payment system me kahan-kahan use hota hai?
Kafka teen jagah use hota hai. (1) Payment Intent Queue — intent create hote hi event publish hota hai. (2) payment.processor.callback topic — processor ka response yahan publish hota hai taaki kayi services use consume kar sakein. (3) Webhook events — status change Kafka par publish hota hai aur Webhook Service use consume karke merchant ko bhejta hai. Kafka se payment flow aur notifications decoupled rehte hain — webhook slow ho to bhi payment nahi rukti. Retention 7 din hai, replication factor 3.
Q30 Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) kya hai?
DLQ wo queue hai jahan wo Kafka messages jate hain jo baar-baar process karne par bhi fail hote hain. Unhe delete karne ki bajaye DLQ me daal diya jata hai taaki koi payment event chupchaap kho na jaye — team manually investigate karke use fix karke dobara process kar sakti hai. Payment system me har event paise se juda hai, isliye 'fail hua to bhool jao' allowed nahi hai.
Q31 Read replicas se kya fayda hai? Replication lag ka kya risk hai?
Payment status queries (GET /v1/payments/{id}) 95% traffic hain — ye read replicas par chalti hain, writes primary par. Isse primary par load kam hota hai aur 10,000 TPS handle hota hai. Risk hai replication lag — replica par purana data dikh sakta hai. Isiliye payment karne ke turant baad wali status query primary se bhi serve ki ja sakti hai, ya UI me 'processing' state dikhayi jati hai jab tak replica sync na ho jaye.
Q32 HSM scale kaise hota hai? 5,000 tokenizations/sec ka matlab kya hai?
Ek HSM ki capacity limited hoti hai, isliye multiple HSM instances load balancer ke peeche chalaye jate hain — 5,000 tokenizations/sec matlab poora cluster har second 5,000 card tokenize kar sakta hai. Tokenization payment flow ka pehla step hai, isliye ye bottleneck nahi ban sakta. HSM instances stateless tareeke se kaam karte hain — koi bhi HSM kisi bhi card ko tokenize kar sakta hai, kyunki keys aur vault shared hain.
Q33 Saved card (card-on-file) kaise kaam karta hai?
User 'save card' choose kare to card tokenize hokar TokenizedCard table me customer_id ke saath link ho jata hai. Agli baar user ko card number dobara nahi bharna padta — gateway saved token use karke payment karta hai, sirf CVV ya 3DS maangta hai. Token customer ke account se linked hota hai, isliye ek user ka token doosre user ke liye kaam nahi karta. Card Vault me encrypted data rehta hai, token hi bahar ghumta hai.
Q34 UPI ya wallet payments card flow se kaise alag hain?
Flow ka structure wahi rehta hai — PaymentIntent, session, orchestrator, processor, webhook — lekin PaymentMethod alag hota hai. Card me tokenization HSM se hoti hai; UPI me VPA (jaise user@okbank) aur collect request hoti hai; wallet me pre-authorized balance hota hai. payment_method_type field (card, upi, wallet) isi liye hai — Orchestrator method dekh kar sahi processor/connector select karta hai. Diagram ke sample session me allowed_methods: ['card', 'upi'] isi ko dikhata hai.
Q35 Multi-processor routing kaise decide hoti hai? Orchestrator kya dekhta hai?
Orchestrator 3 cheezein dekhta hai: (1) Card brand — Visa card Visa connector par, Mastercard apne connector par. (2) Amount — badi amount par zyada reliable ya sasta processor. (3) Merchant config / MerchantPref DB — merchant ne kaunsa processor prefer kiya hai, aur uska us processor par success rate kaisa raha hai. Agar primary processor fail ho to circuit breaker backup par bhejta hai. Diagram me Razorpay, PayU aur Fiserv ke alag-alag connectors isi routing ko dikhate hain.
Q36 Payment gateway design karne ko kaha jaye to interview me kahan se shuru karun?
Isi order me jao: (1) Requirements — functional (6 features) aur non-functional (10k TPS, 200ms, 99.99%, strong consistency). (2) Entities — 7 core entities aur unke relations. (3) APIs — 7 endpoints. (4) HLD — 8 components ka diagram. (5) Deep dive — 7 steps ka flow, tokenization aur idempotency par zyada waqt do. (6) Database schema — 5 tables, sharding key aur indexes. (7) Scale — 10 techniques me se 5-6 numbers ke saath. End me key numbers (200ms budget, 99.8% reconciliation, 5-failure circuit breaker) bolkar khatam karo.
