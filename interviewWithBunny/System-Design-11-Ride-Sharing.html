Ride Sharing System — Uber, Ola, Rapido, Lyft jaisi app ka design

Is lesson me hum ek ride-sharing service ka poora system design karenge — functional requirements se lekar high-level design (HLD) aur phir low-level deep dive (LLD) tak. Socho: rider app kholta hai, cab book karta hai, paas wala driver milta hai, live tracking chalti hai, trip khatm hote hi payment aur rating ho jati hai. In sab ke peeche jo machinery hai — geo-search, distributed locking, WebSocket tracking, surge pricing — wahi is lesson ka asli masala hai.

▶ Video dekho — Ride Sharing System Design (Lesson 11)YouTube par poora lesson video: https://youtu.be/twUz_hWw2T8

Ek line me poori kahani (yaad kar lo)

Rider requests → Geo-search nearby drivers → Zookeeper lock (double assignment roko) → Driver accepts → WebSocket location tracking → Trip completion → Payment & Rating

Ye one-liner hi interview ka skeleton hai. Agar tumhe sirf ek cheez ratni hai to yahi rat lo — har section is pipeline ke ek hisse ko kholta hai. Pehle rider ride request karta hai, phir system geo-search se paas ke drivers dhoondhta hai, phir Zookeeper lock se ensure karta hai ki ek driver do riders ko na mil jaye, driver accept karta hai, WebSocket se live location track hoti hai, aur aakhir me trip complete hokar payment aur rating ke saath kahani khatm hoti hai.

01Requirements

1. Functional Requirements — system ko karna kya hai?

Sabse pehle ye clear karo ki system kya-kya karega. Interview me yahi pehli cheez poochhi jati hai, aur yahi baaki design ki neev hai. Ride sharing ke liye lesson 8 features deta hai:

  • Feature 1 — Fare estimation: Rider ko start location aur destination ke basis par pehle se fare ka estimate milna chahiye, taaki wo book karne se pehle decide kar sake ki ride leni hai ya nahi.
  • Feature 2 — Ride request: Estimated fare dekhne ke baad rider usi fare ke basis par ride request kar sake.
  • Feature 3 — Vehicle categories: Rider alag-alag categories maang sake — Sedan, SUV, Bike, Auto. Har category ka apna rate aur apna driver pool hota hai.
  • Feature 4 — Nearby drivers on map: Rider ko map par real-time me paas ke available drivers dikhne chahiye, unke current pickup/drop-off ke saath.
  • Feature 5 — Geo-proximity matching: Request aate hi rider ko sabse paas wale driver se match kiya jaye — yahi is system ka dil hai.
  • Feature 6 — Real-time tracking: Trip ke dauran driver aur user dono ki location live track ho.
  • Feature 7 — Payment & rating: Trip khatm hone ke baad rider payment kar sake aur ride ko rate kar sake.
  • Feature 8 — Driver controls: Driver ride requests accept/deny kar sake aur apna status badal sake — available, busy, offline.

Notice karo: features dono taraf ke hain — rider side (estimate, request, tracking, payment) aur driver side (accept/deny, status). Interview me aksar log sirf rider side bolke ruk jate hain; driver side ke features bhi utne hi zaroori hain kyunki matching aur availability wahi se control hoti hai.

02Requirements

2. Non-Functional Requirements — system kaisa behave kare?

Functional requirements batate hain system kya karega; non-functional batate hain wo kis scale aur kis quality par karega. Ride sharing ke NFRs hi is design ko mushkil banate hain.

Scale

Users & Drivers
Millions of users aur drivers, globally faila hua system. Matlab koi single-server wala design chalega hi nahi.
Concurrent Rides
Peak hours me hundreds of thousands (lakhon) rides ek saath chal rahi hoti hain. Har active ride ka matlab hai live location updates, live state, live ETA.

Performance & Consistency — CAP ka asli khel

CAP Theorem
Yahan ek bahut important nuance hai: user-facing cheezon ke liye Availability >> Consistency (map thoda purana chalega, app band nahi hona chahiye), lekin driver assignment ke liye Consistency >> Availability — kyunki double assignment kisi bhi haal me nahi hona chahiye. Ek hi system ke andar do alag choices!
Ride Matching
Koi bhi driver ek saath multiple rides ko assign nahi hona chahiye — ye strong consistency wali requirement hai, aur yahi aage chalke Zookeeper lock ki wajah banti hai.
Latency
Driver assignment 1 second se kam me hona chahiye (ride request → driver assigned). Aur real-time location updates har 3–5 seconds me aane chahiye.

Interview tip ke taur par yaad rakho: jab bhi tum "availability vs consistency" ka split kisi specific operation ke hisaab se bata sakte ho, interviewer impress hota hai. "System eventually consistent hai" kehna kamzor jawab hai; "assignment strongly consistent hai, baaki sab available-first hai" — ye mazboot jawab hai.

03Design Foundations

3. Core Entities — data ki duniya

Ye section lesson ke design diagram se liya gaya hai — entities ki detail diagram me hi likhi hai, isliye yahan hum prose me wahi bata rahe hain jo diagram dikhata hai. System me 7 core entities hain:

  • Rider: user_id, name, email, phone, payment_methods, aur current_location (lat/lon). Rider woh hai jo ride maangta hai.
  • Driver: driver_id, name, vehicle_details (type, model, license_plate), rating, status (available / busy / offline), aur current_location. Driver ki status aur location hi matching ki buniyaad hai.
  • Location: Geospatial coordinates timestamp ke saath — real-time tracking ke liye har location ping ke saath time bhi store hota hai.
  • Fare: fare_id, base_fare, distance_fare, time_fare, surge_multiplier, total_amount. Fare ek single number nahi hai — uske tukde hain, aur har tukda alag rule se banta hai.
  • Ride/Trip: ride_id, rider_id, driver_id, pickup_location, drop_location, status (requested / matched / started / completed / cancelled), estimated_fare, actual_fare, start_time, end_time. Ye sabse central entity hai — baaki sab isse judte hain.
  • Rating: rating_id, ride_id, rider_rating (1–5 stars), driver_rating, feedback_text.
  • Payment: payment_id, ride_id, amount, payment_method, status (pending / completed / failed).

Ek baat notice karo: estimated_fare aur actual_fare dono alag-alag rakhe gaye hain. Estimate booking ke time ka vaada hai; actual trip ke asal distance/time se banta hai. Dono ka farak hi baad me disputes aur refunds ki kahani hai.

04Design Foundations

4. API Designing — bahar se system kaisa dikhta hai

Ye API list bhi design diagram se aayi hai — diagram me APIs do samoohon me bate hain: rider APIs aur driver APIs. REST APIs normal request-response ke liye hain, lekin location ke liye WebSocket hai kyunki wahan continuous stream chahiye.

Rider / User APIs

POST /v1/api/fare/estimate
Fare estimation lene ke liye. Request ID ke saath response milta hai, aur alag-alag vehicle types (Sedan, SUV, Bike, Pool) ke options bhi.
POST /v1/api/ride/request
Body me {pickup, drop, vehicleType} bhejo → response me rideId ke saath driver details (agar assignment success ho), ya "driver se match ho gaya" wala message ride par bheja jata hai.
GET /v1/api/ride/history
User ki purani rides ki history.
POST /v1/api/ride/{rideId}/cancel
Ride request cancel karne ke liye.
POST /v1/api/ride/{rideId}/rate
Trip complete hone ke baad driver ko rate karne ke liye.

Driver APIs

WS /v1/driver/location
WebSocket connection — body me {Lat, Long}. Driver ki geolocation har 3–5 seconds me continuously update hoti hai.
POST /v1/api/ride/rides
Body me {requestId, accept/deny} — driver ride request accept ya deny karta hai, response me rideId milta hai.
POST /v1/api/ride/{ride_id}/start
Driver rider ko pickup kar le to trip ko "started" mark karo.
POST /v1/api/ride/{ride_id}/complete
Destination pahunchne par trip ko "completed" mark karo.

Design ki khoobsurati dekho: trip ka lifecycle API names me hi padh sakte ho — estimate → request → (accept) → start → complete → rate, aur beech me cancel ka escape hatch. Interview me APIs aise lifecycle order me sunana hamesha saaf lagta hai.

05Design Foundations

5. High Level Design — bade tukde, unke kaam

Ye bhi design diagram par based hai — diagram jo components dikhata hai, unhi ko yahan prose me samjhaya gaya hai. Har component ka ek saaf, single kaam hai:

  • Users/Clients → LB + API Gateway: Riders aur drivers sabse pehle Load Balancer aur API Gateway se takrate hain. Gateway ka kaam hai — load balancing, authentication, authorization, rate limiting, aur traffic distribution.
  • Ride Service (fare calculation): Pickup/drop ke distance, surge pricing aur vehicle type ke basis par estimated fare calculate karta hai.
  • Driver Matching Service: Geo-proximity search se paas ke available drivers dhoondhta hai aur ride request ko driver assign karta hai. Yahi Zookeeper lock wala critical hissa hai.
  • Location Map Service (GMaps, Apple Map): Mapping, routing, ETA calculations aur distance matrix deta hai. Khud map nahi banate — bahari map service ka istemal karte hain.
  • Rating Service → Rating DB: Rider/driver ratings aur feedback store aur retrieve karta hai.
  • Payment Service → Payment DB: Payment gateway (Stripe/Razorpay) ke through payments process karta hai, refunds bhi handle karta hai.
  • Location Update Service (WebSocket server): Har 3–5 seconds me aane wale driver location pings ingest karta hai aur Redis update karta hai.
  • Redis: Driver availability status (TTL ke saath, ephemeral data), driver locations (geospatial index), aur ride request status — sab garam (hot) data yahan rehta hai.
  • Zookeeper: Driver assignment ke liye distributed lock — double assignment rokta hai, driver locks ke liye ephemeral nodes use karta hai.
  • Notification Service: FCM (Firebase Cloud Messaging) + APN (Apple Push Notification) se real-time notifications bhejta hai.
  • Kafka: Trip updates, location history, analytics aur notifications ke liye event streaming ki reedh (backbone).
  • Drivers DB: Driver profiles, vehicle details, availability history ka persistent storage.
  • Ride DB: Saare ride records, trip details, routes, fare breakdowns ka persistent storage.
  • Trip Update Consumer: Kafka events consume karke location history persist karta hai aur analytics banata hai (fare bonus tracking, operations).
Tip — Redis Geospatial

Redis Geospatial optimization: GEORADIUS use karo — 1M drivers par bhi sub-10ms proximity search milti hai. Locations sorted set me geohash encoding ke saath store hoti hain. Vehicle type ke liye alag indexes rakho (drivers:available:sedan, drivers:available:suv) taaki filtering tez ho.

Warning — Redis par andha bharosa mat karo

Driver availability ke liye kabhi sirf Redis par trust mat karo. Redis ek cache hai TTL ke saath — ephemeral hai. Final assignment se pehle hamesha DB me driver status validate karo: SELECT status FROM drivers WHERE driver_id = {id}. Redis speed deta hai, DB sach deta hai.

06.1Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 1: Fare Estimation — pehle daam, phir sawaari

Rider sabse pehle pickup aur drop daalta hai — example me pickup hai '123 Main St, SF' aur drop hai '456 Market St, SF'. Client POST /v1/api/fare/estimate par ye bhejta hai:

{"pickup_lat": 37.7749, "pickup_lon": -122.4194,
 "drop_lat": 37.7849, "drop_lon": -122.4094,
 "vehicle_type": "sedan"}

Distance kahan se aata hai?

Ride Service Location Map Service ko query karta hai (Google Maps Distance Matrix API) — pickup aur drop ke beech ka distance aur duration. Response: {distance: 5.2 km, duration: 12 min}. Dhyan do, seedha "crow-fly" distance nahi — sadak wala asal route distance, kyunki paisa sadak par chalne ka lagta hai.

Fare ka hisaab — worked example

Base rate: $2.50 (flat charge — gaadi bulane ka fixed paisa)

Distance rate: 5.2 km × $1.50/km = $7.80

Time rate: 12 min × $0.30/min = $3.60

Subtotal: $2.50 + $7.80 + $3.60 = $13.90

Surge lagao (Surge Calculator)

Diagram me ek alag "Surge Calculator" dikhta hai. Ride Service Redis se pickup area ka surge multiplier nikaalta hai: GET surge_multiplier:{area_hash}. Agar demand zyada hai (>20 requests, aur 2 km radius me <5 available drivers) to surge_multiplier = 1.5x. Final fare: $13.90 × 1.5 = $20.85.

Fare breakdown aisa banta hai:

{"base_fare": 2.50, "distance_fare": 7.80, "time_fare": 3.60,
 "surge_multiplier": 1.5, "estimated_total": 20.85, "currency": "USD"}

Client ko response me request_id, estimated fare, breakdown, eta (12 min), distance (5.2 km) aur vehicle options milte hain — sedan $20.85, SUV $28.50, bike $12.00. Aur estimate Redis me cache ho jata hai: SET fare_estimate:{request_id} {fare_details} EX 300 — 5 minute TTL, matlab estimate 5 minute ke liye valid hai. Uske baad prices badal sakte hain, isliye purana estimate chalega nahi.

Tip — Surge smoothing

Surge pricing me smoothing lagao: exponential moving average — new_surge = 0.7 × old + 0.3 × calculated. Isse achanak jump nahi aata. Rider ko dheere-dheere badhta surge (1.2× → 1.35× → 1.5×) fair lagta hai, ekdum 1.0× → 2.0× wala jhatka nahi. Aur max cap 3.0× rakho taaki price gouging na ho.

06.2Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 2: Ride Request & Driver Matching — sabse paas wala driver

Rider "Request Sedan" button dabata hai. Client POST /v1/api/ride/request bhejta hai — request_id, pickup/drop coordinates, vehicle_type 'sedan', payment_method_id ke saath.

Pehle validation

  1. Ride Service check karta hai ki fare estimate abhi bhi hai: Redis se GET fare_estimate:{request_id}.
  2. Payment Service se payment_method_id validate hota hai — bina valid payment ke ride shuru karna matlab baad me paisa atke.

Request banti hai

DB me ride request insert hoti hai status 'PENDING' ke saath, aur Redis me bhi rakhte hain: SET ride_request:{request_id} {details} EX 600 — 10 minute TTL. Agar 10 minute me driver nahi mila, request khud expire ho jayegi. Phir Kafka par 'ride.requested' event publish hota hai — yahi matching ko trigger karta hai.

Geo-proximity search — dil ki dhadkan

Driver Matching Service Kafka event consume karke Redis Geospatial query chalata hai:

GEORADIUS drivers:available {pickup_lon} {pickup_lat} 5 km WITHDIST ASC

Matlab: pickup ke 5 km ke andar ke available drivers, distance ke hisaab se sorted (sabse paas pehle). Phir vehicle type se filter: har result ke liye SISMEMBER drivers:sedan {driver_id}. Result kuch aisa:

[{driver_id: D1, distance: 0.8 km},
 {driver_id: D2, distance: 1.2 km},
 {driver_id: D3, distance: 2.5 km}]

Diagram me ek pricing note bhi hai — 'Ride = Rs 70/km, waiting: Rs 1/min, Cab = Rs 50/km' — pricing vehicle type ke hisaab se alag hoti hai, isliye matching bhi type-wise hoti hai.

Candidates par iteration

Sorted list me sabse paas wale driver se shuru karo: har driver ke liye Zookeeper lock try karo (agla step). Lock mila → assign karke ruk jao. Lock fail (driver pehle se kisi aur ko mil gaya) → agla driver. Sab try ho gaye aur koi nahi mila → rider ko 'No drivers available, please try again'.

06.3Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 3: Driver Locking with Zookeeper — CRITICAL step

Diagram me Zookeeper wala hissa alag se dikhta hai — 'Driver Lock' ke neeche ephemeral nodes driver1_001, driver1_002, driver1_003. Is step ka ek hi maksad hai: double assignment rokna — ek driver ek time par sirf ek hi ride ko mile.

Lock kaise lagta hai?

Zookeeper structure: /locks/drivers/{driver_id}/ephemeral_node — har lock attempt ke liye ek ephemeral node banta hai. Driver Matching Service driver D1 ko lock karna chahta hai:

  1. Ephemeral node banao: CREATE /locks/drivers/D1/{request_id} (ephemeral, sequence ke saath).
  2. Check karo ki tumhara node sabse chhote sequence number par hai ya nahi: GET_CHILDREN /locks/drivers/D1 ORDER BY sequence.
  3. Tumhara node pehla hai → lock mil gaya.
  4. Kisi aur ka node pehle se hai → lock fail — driver pehle hi kisi aur request ke paas hai.

Diagram ke apne shabdon me: "when the driver matching service want to create a lock it creates a ephemeral node. if it is successfully, means it got the lock. Incase it got the NumberAlreadyExistException, then the driver is already assigned to someone (another) or try after sometime"

Lock milne ke baad

  • Redis me driver BUSY mark karo: SET driver:{D1}:status 'BUSY' EX 900
  • Available drivers ke geospatial index se hatao: GEOREM drivers:available {D1} — taaki koi aur search use phir na utha le.
  • DB update: UPDATE drivers SET status='BUSY', current_ride_id={request_id} WHERE driver_id='D1'
  • Driver ko notification: Kafka 'driver.ride_offered' → Notification Service FCM/APN se push bhejta hai — request_id, rider name, pickup location, estimated fare, ETA to pickup ke saath.

Driver app par modal khulta hai — ride details aur Accept / Decline buttons, 30 second ke timeout ke saath. Jawab nahi aaya to auto-decline.

Lock khud kab khulta hai?

Ephemeral node ka session timeout 30 seconds hai. Agar Driver Matching Service crash ho jaye ya driver jawab na de → Zookeeper node khud delete kar deta hai → lock release → doosri service instance agle driver ke saath retry kar sakti hai. Diagram ke shabdon me: "This lock is released, server have to delete the ephemeral node manually, of zookeeper will automatically delete it, if the server gets session timeout/disconnected/dies" — matlab normal case me server khud delete karta hai, marne par Zookeeper safai kar deta hai.

Warning — Critical

Driver assignment ke liye Zookeeper distributed locking MUST hai. Bina lock ke, multiple ride requests ek hi driver ko simultaneously assign kar sakti hain → double booking → dono riders ko same driver dikhega → bhayanak UX + revenue loss. Ephemeral nodes failure par automatic lock release ensure karte hain.

Interview Favorite

Interviewers hamesha poochte hain: "Double assignment kaise roko ge?" Jawab: Zookeeper ephemeral sequential nodes. Request R1 driver D1 ko lock karne jata hai → /locks/drivers/D1/request_R1_seq0001 banata hai, check karta hai ki wo sabse lowest sequence hai → lock mila. Concurrent request R2 ko seq0002 milta hai → dekhta hai seq0001 pehle se hai → lock fail → agla driver try karta hai.

06.4Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 4: Driver Accept / Decline — faisla driver ke haath me

Push notification milte hi driver app ride details dikhata hai. Ab do raaste hain.

Driver Accept kare to

Driver POST /v1/api/ride/rides par {request_id, action: 'accept'} bhejta hai. Ride Service pehle validate karta hai:

  1. Zookeeper lock abhi bhi held hai? EXISTS /locks/drivers/{driver_id}/{request_id}
  2. Request abhi bhi pending hai? Redis se GET ride_request:{request_id} ka status.

Phir ek transaction me trip banti hai: trips table me INSERT (status 'MATCHED', matched_at = now), ride_requests ka status MATCHED, drivers ka current_trip_id set — sab ek saath COMMIT. Redis me trip:{trip_id}:status 'MATCHED' EX 3600 aur driver:{driver_id}:current_trip {trip_id} EX 3600 set hota hai, pending request delete ho jati hai.

Ab Zookeeper lock delete kar do — DELETE /locks/drivers/{driver_id}/{request_id} — ab lock ki zaroorat nahi, driver pakka assign ho gaya hai. Rider ko Kafka 'ride.matched' se khabar jati hai: push notification "Driver ne tumhari ride accept kar li! ETA 5 min" aur WebSocket message jisme driver ki details — name, photo, rating, vehicle details, current location, eta 5 min. Rider app map par driver dikhata hai, ETA countdown chalta hai, 'Contact Driver' button aa jata hai.

Driver Decline kare to

{request_id, action: 'decline'} aate hi: Zookeeper lock release, driver phir AVAILABLE mark (SET driver:{driver_id}:status 'AVAILABLE'), aur matching agle sabse paas wale driver ke saath retry. Agar saare drivers decline kar dein → rider ko 'Unable to find driver, please try again later'.

Yahan ek subtle baat hai: decline par driver ko sazaa nahi milti is step me — wo bas pool me wapas chala jata hai. Lekin matching dobara shuru hoti hai taaki rider ko intezaar kam karna pade.

06.5Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 5: Real-Time Location Tracking — WebSocket ki taakat

Diagram me 'Location Update Svc (websocket server)' drivers se location updates lete hue dikhta hai. Driver app GPS se har 3–5 seconds me location bhejta hai — WebSocket connection WS /v1/driver/location par, Authorization: Bearer {JWT} ke saath, connect hone par hi authenticate hota hai.

Ek location ping ka safar

  1. Ping aata hai: {driver_id, lat: 37.7849, lon: -122.4194, timestamp, accuracy: 10m, speed: 25 km/h}. Service validate karta hai — driver authenticated hai, trip in progress hai ya driver available hai.
  2. Redis geospatial update: driver AVAILABLE hai to GEOADD drivers:available {lon} {lat} {driver_id}; trip par hai to SET location:{driver_id} '{lat, lon, timestamp}' EX 60 (1 min TTL); aur real-time subscribers ke liye Redis Pub/Sub par PUBLISH driver_location:{driver_id}.
  3. Kafka par 'driver.location_updated' event — Trip Update Consumer ise history ke liye persist karta hai.

Diagram ke shabdon me Kafka → Trip Update Consumer wala flow "persistently update the driver location, status, rider house tracking (operations), which could driver movement, e.g ETA message" ke liye hai — matlab location sirf live dikhane ke kaam ki nahi, operations aur ETA ke liye bhi store hoti hai.

Rider ko update kaise milta hai?

Rider app WS /v1/trip/{trip_id}/track subscribe karta hai. Location Update Service driver ki location forward karta hai: {type: 'driver_location', lat, lon, timestamp, eta_min: 4}. App map par marker ghumata hai, ETA recalculate karta hai — "Driver is 0.5 km away".

ETA calculation

Location Map Service (Google Maps Directions API) se current route lo: driver location → pickup → drop. Response: {distance_remaining: 2.5 km, duration_remaining: 8 min, route_polyline}. Redis me ETA update: HSET trip:{trip_id}:details 'eta_min' 8.

Geofencing — "driver aa gaya!"

Jab driver pickup se 50 meter ke andar aa jaye: distance(driver_location, pickup_location) < 50m detect karo → rider ko notify karo "Your driver has arrived!" → trip status 'DRIVER_ARRIVED'.

Interview Favorite

Ye samjhana mat bhoolna: WebSocket vs HTTP polling. Polling = 100K riders × 20 req/min = 2M req/min = 33K req/sec ka overhead. WebSocket = persistent connection, update sirf tab jab location badle, 100x kam traffic, <100ms latency.

Warning — Geofencing skip mat karo

Jab driver pickup ke 50m ke andar ho: Haversine formula se distance(driver_location, pickup_location) calculate karo. <50m ho to 'driver_arrived' event trigger karo → rider ko "Your driver has arrived!" — push notification + vibrate + sound.

06.6Deep Dive — Low Level Design

Step 6: Trip Start — meter chalu

Driver rider ko pickup karke app me 'Start Trip' dabata hai — POST /v1/api/ride/{trip_id}/start. Ride Service pehle validate karta hai ki trip ka status 'MATCHED' ya 'DRIVER_ARRIVED' hai (kuch aur hai to start allowed nahi). Phir:

  • Trip update: status 'IN_PROGRESS', start_time = now(), start_location = current driver location.
  • Fare meter chalu: distance/time ke basis par ongoing fare calculate hona shuru.
  • Redis: SET trip:{trip_id}:status 'IN_PROGRESS' EX 7200 — 2 ghante ki max trip duration ka TTL.

  1. Periodically driver ki location lo: GET location:{driver_id}.
  2. Distance traveled = consecutive location updates ke beech ke distances ka jod (sum).
  3. Time elapsed = current_time − start_time.
  4. Ongoing fare = base_fare + (distance × distance_rate) + (time × time_rate) × surge_multiplier.
  5. Rider app har 30 seconds me dikhata hai — jaise "Current fare: $15.20".

Ratio se multiplier — worked example

demand_ratio < 1.5
Koi surge nahi — multiplier = 1.0
1.5 ≤ ratio < 3
Low surge — multiplier = 1.3x
3 ≤ ratio < 5
Medium surge — multiplier = 1.5x
ratio ≥ 5
High surge — multiplier = 2.0x ya zyada, max 3.0x

8. Zookeeper Driver Locking — Deep Dive (diagram ka critical hissa)

Problem: Bina locking ke, multiple concurrent ride requests ek hi driver ko assign karne ki koshish karengi → double assignment. Diagram me Zookeeper block ke andar 'Driver Lock' ke neeche ephemeral nodes driver1_001, driver1_002, driver1_003 bane dikhte hain — yahi woh mechanism hai jo is problem ko jad se khatm karta hai.

Mechanism, step by step

  1. Ephemeral node creation: Driver Matching Service driver D1 ko request R1 ke liye lock karna chahta hai → CREATE /locks/drivers/D1/request_R1 (ephemeral, sequential).
  2. Lock check: /locks/drivers/D1 ke saare children lo, sequence number se sort karo. Tumhara node sabse lowest sequence par hai → lock mila, assignment aage badhao. Koi aur node lower sequence par hai → lock fail — driver pehle hi kisi aur request ke paas hai.

Diagram ke shabdon me: "when the driver matching service want to create a lock it creates a ephemeral node. if it is successfully, means it got the lock. Incase it got the NumberAlreadyExistException, then the driver is already assigned to someone (another) or try after sometime"

Success path

Lock mila → (1) Redis me driver BUSY mark karo, (2) drivers:available geospatial index se hatao, (3) DB me drivers.status = 'BUSY', (4) driver ko accept/decline ke liye notification bhejo, (5) driver ke jawab ka intezaar — timeout 30 seconds.

Failure path

Lock nahi mila → (1) partial locks release karo, (2) candidate list ke agle sabse paas wale driver par jao, (3) D2 ke saath lock phir try karo, (4) saare drivers try ho gaye aur sab fail → rider ko 'No drivers available'.

Accept, decline, timeout, crash — chaaro cases

  • Driver accepts: DB me trip record banao, Zookeeper lock delete kar do (ab zaroorat nahi), driver assigned — lock ka kaam khatm.
  • Driver declines: Lock delete, driver Redis me phir AVAILABLE, agle driver ke saath matching retry.
  • Timeout (30 sec me jawab nahi): Zookeeper session timeout trigger → ephemeral node auto-delete → driver AVAILABLE mark → system agle driver ke saath retry → asal driver ko der se khabar: 'Ride request expired'.
  • Service crash: Driver Matching Service lock hold karte hue mar jaye → Zookeeper session loss detect karta hai → us session ke saare ephemeral nodes 30 seconds ke andar auto-delete → drivers khud unlock → nayi service instance matching retry kar sakti hai.

Diagram ke shabdon me: "This lock is released, server have to delete the ephemeral node manually, of zookeeper will automatically delete it, if the server gets session timeout/disconnected/dies"

Zookeeper hi kyun, Redis kyun nahi?

  • Mazboot consistency guarantees — Zookeeper CP system hai.
  • Built-in session management — ephemeral nodes khud saaf ho jate hain.
  • Atomic lock operations ordering ke saath.
  • Failure par auto-cleanup.
  • Redis SETNX kaam kar sakta hai, lekin manual cleanup logic zyada likhna padega.

Alternative hai Redis distributed lock Redlock algorithm ke saath — lekin critical driver assignment use case ke liye Zookeeper hi pasandeeda hai.

Monitoring

  • Lock acquisition time track karo — typical <10ms.
  • Lock acquisition fail rate >50% ho to alert — Zookeeper me gadbad ka sanket.
  • Har driver ke ephemeral node count par nazar — 0 ya 1 hona chahiye, kabhi >1 nahi.
  • Session timeouts monitor karo — bahut zyada timeouts matlab connectivity issues.

9. Scaling & Optimization Techniques — 12 techniques ek saath

Lesson ki scaling list ko ek-ek karke samjho — har technique kisi ek specific bottleneck ko todti hai:

  1. Redis Geospatial indexing: GEORADIUS 1M drivers ko <10ms me search karta hai, O(log(N)) complexity. Geographic region ke hisaab se shard karo.
  2. Zookeeper driver locking: Ephemeral nodes se double assignment rokna, timeout par auto-cleanup, <10ms lock acquisition.
  3. WebSocket location updates: Ek instance par 100K concurrent connections, persistent connection — HTTP polling ke muqable 100x kam traffic.
  4. Kafka event streaming: 100K+ location updates/sec, services decouple — location persistence, analytics, notifications alag-alag consumers.
  5. Surge pricing: Dynamic supply/demand balancing, har 60 seconds per area recalculate, Redis me 2-min TTL ke saath.
  6. Database sharding: Trips ko pickup location geohash se, drivers ko driver_id se — horizontal scaling ka raasta.
  7. Caching strategy: Driver locations (60s TTL), availability (geospatial), surge (120s TTL), fare estimates (300s TTL).
  8. Push notifications: Real-time alerts ke liye FCM/APN, in-app updates ke liye WebSocket — hybrid approach se best latency.
  9. Geofencing: Pickup ke 50m ke andar driver arrival detect karo, automatic status updates trigger karo, manual actions ghatao.
  10. Rate limiting: Rider 10 ride requests/hour, driver 1 location update / 3 sec — abuse aur API flooding roko.
  11. CDN for assets: Driver/vehicle photos aur map tiles CloudFront se — 95% cache hit rate, origin ka load kam.
  12. Database optimization: Geospatial queries ke liye PostGIS, (status, location) par indexes, historical data ke liye month-wise partitioning.

Scale par roz 100M+ push notifications, delivery rate 95–99%, push latency p50 1–2 sec.

Interview FAQs — 36 sawal, poore jawab

Ye sawal lesson ke apne Q&A (double assignment, WebSocket tracking, surge, geospatial, notifications) aur unse jude current-era interview topics se bane hain. Har jawab khud me poora hai — bina lesson dobara khole revise ho jayega.

Q1Double assignment kaise roko ge — ek driver do riders ko kyun nahi mil sakta?

Zookeeper distributed locking se. Jab teen requests R1, R2, R3 ek saath driver D1 ko chahte hain, to har matching service instance ek ephemeral sequential node banata hai — /locks/drivers/D1/request_R1_seq0001, seq0002, seq0003. Sirf sabse lowest sequence (R1) ko lock milta hai; baaki dono dekhte hain ki unse chhota sequence pehle se hai, to agle driver par chale jate hain.

Lock milte hi D1 Redis me BUSY mark hota hai, geospatial index se hat jata hai aur DB me status BUSY ho jata hai. Driver accept kare to lock delete; decline ya 30-second timeout par Zookeeper session expire hokar ephemeral node khud delete kar deta hai aur D1 phir AVAILABLE ho jata hai. Alternative Redis Redlock hai, par usme manual TTL management aur ordering ki kamzori hai — isiliye critical assignment ke liye Zookeeper behtar hai.

Q2Real-time location tracking WebSocket se kaise kaam karta hai?

Driver app JWT ke saath WS /v1/driver/location par persistent connection banata hai. GPS har 3–5 seconds me ping bhejta hai — {lat, lon, accuracy, speed, timestamp}. Location Update Service har ping par Redis geospatial index update karta hai, location cache set karta hai (60s TTL), Redis Pub/Sub par publish karta hai, aur Kafka par driver.location_updated event bhejta hai jo Trip Update Consumer history ke liye persist karta hai.

Rider side par app WS /v1/trip/{trip_id}/track subscribe karta hai; gateway Pub/Sub se location lekar rider ko forward karta hai — app marker ghumata hai, ETA recalculate karta hai. Har 30 sec Directions API se ETA refresh hota hai, aur driver ke pickup ke 50m ke andar aate hi geofence 'driver_arrived' event bhejta hai. Heartbeat har 30 sec, disconnect par exponential backoff se reconnect.

Q3Surge pricing dynamically kaise calculate hoti hai?

Sheher ko geohash grid (5km × 5km cells) me baanto. Surge Calculator har 60 seconds me har cell ke liye available drivers (Redis GEORADIUS count) aur pichhle 10 minute ki pending requests ginta hai. demand_ratio = pending / available. Ratio <1.2 → 1.0×, 2.0 tak → 1.2×, 3.0 tak → 1.5×, 5.0 tak → 1.8×, usse zyada → 2.0–3.0×, cap hamesha 3.0×.

Example: Downtown SF me 45 requests aur 15 drivers → ratio 3.0 → 1.8× → $15 wala fare $27. Multiplier Redis me 2-minute TTL ke saath rehta hai. Smoothing ke liye exponential moving average (new = 0.7×old + 0.3×calculated) achanak jump rokta hai. Rider ko surge explicitly accept karna padta hai, aur high surge par offline drivers ko bonus/incentive se online bulaya jata hai. Production data me surge high demand par driver supply 30–40% badha deta hai.

Q4Nearby drivers dhoondhne ke liye geospatial queries efficiently kaise handle karte ho?

Redis geospatial commands. GEOADD drivers:available {lon} {lat} {driver_id} locations ko geohash-encoded sorted set me rakhta hai. Query: GEORADIUS ... 5 km WITHDIST ASC COUNT 10 — 1M drivers me se 10 nearest ~10ms me, complexity O(N + log M). Memory lagbhag 100 bytes per driver, matlab 1M drivers ≈ 100 MB.

Vehicle type ke liye alag indexes (drivers:available:sedan, :suv) filtering tez karte hain. Har location update ek GEOADD hai (~1ms); 100K drivers har 5 sec update karein to 20K updates/sec — Redis ke 100K ops/sec ke andar. Persistence aur complex spatial queries ke liye PostGIS (GIST index, ST_DWithin) rakho — 10–50ms, isliye production hybrid hota hai: Redis real-time ke liye, PostGIS sach aur analysis ke liye, Kafka se async sync. Radius me koi na mile to 5km → 10km → 20km badhao.

Q5Notification system ka design kaisa hai — riders aur drivers ko real-time updates kaise milte hain?

Multi-channel design hai. Notification Service Kafka events (ride.matched, trip.started, driver.ride_offered, trip.completed) consume karke channel chun-ta hai — app band hai to push (Android par FCM, iOS par APN), app khula hai to WebSocket (<100ms). Device tokens login par register hote hain; invalid token mile to inactive mark kar do.

Push best-effort hai — isliye critical notifications (driver assigned, payment failed) par SMS fallback rakho ($0.01–0.05 per SMS). Payload me deep link hota hai taaki tap karte hi trip screen khule. Multi-device sync, user preferences (promotions off, ride updates on), rich notifications (driver photo, Accept/Decline buttons) aur delivery/open rate analytics bhi isi service ke hisse hain. Scale par roz 100M+ push, delivery rate 95–99%, push latency p50 1–2 seconds.

Q6Fare estimate kaise banta hai? Poora hisaab batao.

Teen tukde jodte hain: base fare (flat, $2.50) + distance fare (distance × $1.50/km) + time fare (duration × $0.30/min), aur is jod par surge multiplier lagta hai. Distance aur duration Google Maps Distance Matrix API se aate hain — seedhi doori nahi, sadak wala route.

Lesson ka worked example: 5.2 km, 12 min → $2.50 + $7.80 + $3.60 = $13.90 subtotal; surge 1.5× lagte hi $20.85. Response me breakdown ke saath vehicle options bhi jate hain (sedan $20.85, SUV $28.50, bike $12.00). Estimate Redis me 5-minute TTL ke saath cache hota hai — utni der hi valid hai, kyunki surge aur traffic badalte rehte hain.

Q7Ride matching ka poora flow kya hai — request se driver assign tak?

Rider request karta hai → Ride Service fare estimate aur payment method validate karta hai → request DB me PENDING banti hai, Redis me 10-min TTL ke saath rehti hai → Kafka par ride.requested event jata hai → Driver Matching Service GEORADIUS se 5km ke andar ke available drivers distance-wise sorted nikaalta hai, vehicle type se filter karta hai → sabse paas wale par Zookeeper lock try karta hai → lock mile to driver ko push notification (accept/decline, 30 sec timeout) → accept par trip MATCHED banti hai aur rider ko driver details + ETA milta hai.

Poora assignment 1 second se kam me hona chahiye — yahi is system ki sabse sakht latency requirement hai.

Q8ETA dynamically kaise calculate aur update hota hai?

Har 30 seconds me Location Update Service Google Maps Directions API ko query karta hai — origin = driver ki current location, destination = pickup (trip se pehle) ya drop (trip ke dauran). Response me distance_remaining, duration_remaining aur route_polyline milte hain. ETA Redis me trip details ke saath update hota hai aur WebSocket se rider ko broadcast — rider app par 'Driver is 0.5 km away, arriving in 4 min' jaisa live update dikhta hai.

Ye static estimate nahi hai — traffic aur driver ki asal movement ke saath badalta rehta hai, isiliye periodic refresh zaroori hai.

Q9Driver matching service crash ho jaye jab uske paas locks hon, to kya hoga?

Kuch bura nahi hoga — yahi ephemeral nodes ki khoobsurati hai. Zookeeper session loss detect karta hai, aur crash huee service ke banaye saare ephemeral nodes 30 seconds ke andar khud delete ho jate hain. Drivers unlock ho jate hain, nayi service instance matching dobara try kar sakti hai. Na manual cleanup, na atakey hue drivers. Yahi wajah hai ki lock ke liye aisi cheez chahiye jo holder ke marne par khud saaf ho jaye — plain DB flag ya Redis key bina TTL ke yahan fansa sakti hai.

Q10Driver ride decline kar de ya 30 seconds me jawab na de to kya hota hai?

Decline par Zookeeper lock release hota hai, driver phir AVAILABLE mark hokar pool me wapas chala jata hai, aur matching agle sabse paas wale driver ke saath retry hoti hai. Timeout (30 sec me koi jawab nahi) par bhi wahi: session expire → node auto-delete → driver AVAILABLE → next driver. Agar saare candidate drivers decline kar dein ya koi lock na mile, to rider ko 'Unable to find driver, please try again later' milta hai. Rider ko har decline par ghanto intezaar nahi karna padta kyunki candidate list pehle se sorted hoti hai.

Q11Redis hi kyun? Driver locations ke liye seedha database kyun nahi?

Kyunki load aur latency dono alag league ke hain. 100K+ drivers har 3–5 seconds me location bhejte hain — ye 20K–33K writes/sec hai, aur matching query ko 1M records me se ~10ms me jawab chahiye. Redis geospatial sorted set ye dono aaram se deta hai: GEOADD ~1ms, GEORADIUS ~10ms.

Lekin ek warning hamesha yaad rakho: Redis cache hai, sach nahi. TTL expire hone par data ud sakta hai. Isliye persistent record PostgreSQL/PostGIS me rehta hai, aur final assignment se pehle driver status DB se validate hota hai — Redis speed deta hai, DB sach.

Q12Geohash kya hai aur surge grid me kaise kaam karta hai?

Geohash lat/lon ko ek string me encode karta hai jisme common prefix ka matlab hai — paas-paas ki jagah. Character length precision tay karta hai: 5 chars ≈ 5km × 5km cell, 6 chars ≈ 1.2km × 600m, 7 chars ≈ 150m × 150m. Lesson driver locations ke liye 6-char precision ki salah deta hai — accuracy aur performance ka balance.

Surge ke liye sheher ko 5km × 5km geohash cells me baanta jata hai (jaise Downtown SF = 9q8yy) aur har cell ka supply/demand alag track hota hai — isiliye ek ilaake me surge 2× ho sakta hai aur do kilometer door 1.0×. Redis geo bhi andar se yahi geohash encoding sorted set me rakhta hai.

Q13CAP theorem is system me kahan-kahan kaise apply hota hai?

Ek hi system, do alag jawab — yahi is lesson ki sabse achhi baat hai. User-facing cheezon (map par drivers, ETA, trip status) ke liye Availability >> Consistency: map 2 second purana chalega, par app rukna nahi chahiye. Lekin driver assignment ke liye Consistency >> Availability: ek driver galti se bhi do rides ko nahi milna chahiye, isliye wahan Zookeeper jaisa CP system lagta hai.

Interview me 'system AP hai ya CP hai' ka ek-shabd wala jawab kamzor hota hai; operation-wise batao — kaunsi cheez AP, kaunsi CP, aur kyun.

Q14WebSocket ki jagah HTTP polling use karein to kya tootega?

Numbers batao: 100K riders har 3 seconds me poll karein → 100K × 20 req/min = 2M requests/min = ~33K requests/sec sirf location poochhne ke liye. Har request ke saath HTTP headers, connection overhead aur 1–3 second ki polling delay. WebSocket me persistent connection hota hai — update sirf tab jata hai jab location badle, overhead na ke barabar, latency <100ms. Lesson ke hisaab se WebSocket lagbhag 100x kam traffic leta hai. Bandwidth bachane ke aur tareeke: driver 10 meter se kam hile to update skip karo, speed <5 km/h ho to frequency ghatao, JSON ki jagah Protocol Buffers use karo.

Q15Kafka is design me kahan-kahan use hota hai?

Kafka event streaming ki reedh hai — services ko decouple karta hai. Topics: ride.requested (matching trigger), driver.ride_offered, ride.matched, trip.started, trip.completed, aur sabse bhari — driver.location_updated (100K+ messages/sec).

Location events ke teen consumers hain: Trip Update Consumer location history persist karta hai, Analytics Service metrics banata hai, Fraud Detection GPS spoofing pakadta hai. Ek producer, kai consumers — yahi decoupling ka fayda hai: naya consumer jodne ke liye producer ko chhuna nahi padta. Partitioning driver_id aur trip_id se hoti hai taaki ordering aur parallel processing dono milein.

Q16Trip cancellation ke rules kya hain — kis par fee, kis par penalty?

Driver accept se pehle rider cancel kare → koi fee nahi, request delete, pre-authorization refund. Accept ke baad cancel kare → cancellation fee (jaise $5), driver ko usme se hissa milta hai (jaise $3), driver AVAILABLE hokar pool me wapas. Trip ke beech me rider cancel allowed hi nahi.

Driver cancel kare to reason dekha jata hai: rider 5 minute wait ke baad no-show → valid, koi penalty nahi. Bina wajah cancel → rating aur acceptance rate ghatega, baar-baar ho to temporary suspension. Request ke 2 minute ke andar (driver assign hone se pehle) rider ka free grace period hai. App error ya bahut lambe wait jaisi valid wajah par support se full refund milta hai.

Q17Payment flow kaise kaam karta hai — failure par kya hota hai?

Trip complete hote hi Ride Service asal distance/time se final fare nikaalta hai, rider ka saved payment method leta hai, aur payment gateway (Stripe/Razorpay) par payment intent banakar confirm karta hai. Success par trip me payment_status COMPLETED aur gateway ka payment_id store hota hai; rider ko email/SMS receipt — summary, fare breakdown, PDF.

Failure par payment_status FAILED mark hota hai aur exponential backoff se retry hota hai, ya rider ko payment method update karne ko kaha jata hai. Payment ko trip completion se alag rakha gaya hai taaki paisa atke to bhi trip ka record aur driver ki availability na ruke — driver turant AVAILABLE hokar agli ride le sakta hai.

Q18Ratings anonymous kyun rakhi jati hain, aur unka asar kahan padta hai?

Diagram me Rating block par 'anonymous' saaf likha hai — ratings trip se alag store hoti hain, driver rating dekh sakta hai par ye nahi ki kis rider ne di. Isse rider bina dare sach bol sakta hai aur badle (retaliation) wali rating ka khel rukta hai. Driver bhi rider ko rate karta hai, dono ke averages update hote hain.

Asar: driver rating future matching me priority deti hai, rider rating driver ke acceptance faisle ko affect karti hai (kam rating = zyada wait), aur lagatar <4.0 ratings account review trigger karti hain. Analytics me rating trends dekh kar problem drivers ko manager review ke liye pakda jata hai.

Q19Driver availability heartbeat mechanism kaise kaam karta hai?

Driver 'Go Online' dabata hai → DB me status AVAILABLE, Redis geo index me GEOADD, aur status key 5-minute TTL ke saath. Phir app har 30 seconds me WebSocket heartbeat bhejta hai — {driver_id, lat, lon, status} — jisse TTL refresh aur position update hota rehta hai.

Agar heartbeat 2 minute tak miss ho jaye (app crash, phone band, network gaya) to driver ko offline maan kar geo index se hata diya jata hai. Isi se 'ghost drivers' nahi bante — map par wo drivers nahi dikhte jo asal me gayab hain. Aur 30 minute tak koi ride accept na karne wale driver ko 'Still available?' prompt aata hai, 5 minute me jawab na mile to auto-offline.

Q20System ko scale kaise karoge — 10x load aa jaye to?

Har layer ka apna jawab hai. Geospatial: Redis geo ko geographic region se shard karo (US-West, US-East, EU, APAC). Database: trips ko pickup geohash se aur drivers ko driver_id hash se shard karo; purani trips month-wise partition aur 2 saal se purani S3 Glacier me archive. WebSocket: ek gateway 10K connections — 1M concurrent users ke liye 100 instances, sticky sessions ke saath. Kafka: location topic ko driver_id se 100 partitions me baanto. Zookeeper: 5-node ensemble, quorum 3, leader failover <1 sec. CDN static assets (photos, map tiles) par 95% cache hit. Aur rate limiting har jagah — rider 10 requests/hour, location 1 update/3 sec.

Q21Geofencing kya hai aur driver arrival kaise detect hota hai?

Geofencing matlab ek jagah ke aas-paas ka virtual ghaira (fence) banana aur usme entry detect karna. Har location ping par Haversine formula se distance(driver_location, pickup_location) calculate hota hai. Distance <50 meter hote hi 'driver_arrived' event trigger hota hai — trip status DRIVER_ARRIVED, rider ko push notification 'Your driver has arrived!' vibrate aur sound ke saath.

Bina geofencing ke rider andaze se bahar aata rehta ya driver intezaar karta rehta — ye chhota sa feature pickup experience ko bahut saaf bana deta hai, isliye lesson ise 'kabhi skip mat karo' wali warning ke saath deta hai.

Q22Fraud detection ride sharing me kaise kaam karta hai?

Teen bade fraud patterns dekhe jate hain: (1) Fake GPS — driver asal me hil nahi raha par location jump kar rahi hai (spoofing). (2) Collusion — driver aur rider ek hi insaan ya mile hue log, nakli trips se incentives/bonus game kar rahe hain. (3) Fare manipulation — driver jaanboojh kar lamba route le raha hai taaki distance fare badhe.

Detection Kafka ke location event stream par chalta hai — historical patterns par trained ML model suspicious trips flag karta hai, phir manual review hota hai. Location history persist karna (Trip Update Consumer) isiliye bhi zaroori hai — bina history ke fraud ka saboot hi nahi banta.

Q23Rider ko map par paas ke drivers real-time me kaise dikhte hain?

Drivers ki locations Redis geospatial index me har 3–5 seconds me update hoti rehti hain. Rider app jab map kholta hai, to uske area ke liye GEORADIUS query se available drivers milte hain, aur WebSocket/Pub/Sub ke through unki movement live update hoti rehti hai — isiliye map par gaadiyan hilti hui dikhti hain, photo jaisi static nahi.

Feature list me ye bhi hai ki rider available drivers ko unke current pickup/drop-off ke saath dekhta hai. Hotspot locations (airport, station) ke liye nearby drivers ki list 10-second TTL ke saath cache karne se baar-baar wahi query Redis par nahi padti.

Q24Database schema me kaunsi tables hain aur PostGIS kyun chahiye?

PostgreSQL me paanch main tables: Riders, Drivers, Trips, Ratings, Payments. Trips sabse central hai — rider_id, driver_id, pickup/drop geography points, status enum (PENDING se COMPLETED/CANCELLED tak), estimated vs actual fare, surge multiplier, saare timestamps. Ratings me trip_id UNIQUE hai taaki ek trip par dohri rating na ho, aur Payments me bhi trip_id UNIQUE.

PostGIS isliye chahiye kyunki pickup/drop sirf lat/lon numbers nahi hain — un par spatial queries chalti hain (ST_DWithin se radius search, ST_Distance se doori). GIST index in queries ko tez karta hai. Indexes (rider_id, created_at), (driver_id, created_at) aur (status, pickup_location) par GIST — ye teeno asal query patterns se aaye hain.

Q25Ride request expire ho jaye to kya hota hai? TTLs ka jaal samjhao.

Har garam cheez ka TTL hai aur har TTL ke peeche ek wajah. Ride request Redis me 10 minute (EX 600) — utne me driver na mile to request khud saaf. Fare estimate 5 minute — uske baad daam badal sakte hain. Driver status 5 minute — heartbeat se refresh, miss ho to gayab. Driver location 1 minute — purani location dikhana jhooth bolne jaisa hai. Surge multiplier 2 minute — har 60 sec recalculate. Trip status active trip ke dauran 2 ghante, complete hone par record 24 ghante.

TTL ka matlab hai koi safai wala process likhna hi nahi padta — data apne aap purana hokar ud jata hai, aur system kabhi stale state par faisla nahi leta.

Q26Driver ko ride offer ka notification kaise milta hai aur timeout ka kya scene hai?

Matching Service lock lete hi Kafka par driver.ride_offered event daalta hai → Notification Service driver ke device tokens nikaal kar FCM (Android) / APN (iOS) se push bhejta hai — request_id, rider name, pickup location, estimated fare aur ETA to pickup ke saath. Driver app par Accept/Decline wala modal khulta hai.

Timeout 30 seconds hai — jawab na aaye to auto-decline maan kar lock release ho jata hai aur matching agle driver par chali jati hai. Driver ko baad me 'Ride request expired' ki khabar milti hai. 30 second ka ye number lock ke Zookeeper session timeout se juda hai — dono ek hi ghadi se chalte hain.

Q27Agar kisi area me drivers hi na hon to system kya karta hai?

Pehle search radius badhta hai — 5km me koi nahi to 10km, phir 20km (exponential backoff jaisa). Phir bhi koi na mile to rider ko saaf jawab milta hai: 'No drivers available, please try again' — jhooti ummeed nahi. Saath me system doosri taraf supply bulane ki koshish karta hai: surge badhne se us area ke offline drivers ko 'Earn more! Go online now' wale incentives jate hain.

Operations side par agar high-demand area me available drivers 10 se kam ho jayein to alert aur incentive campaigns trigger hote hain. Aur rider ke liye 'Notify me when prices drop / drivers available' jaisa option intezaar ko kam kashtdayak banata hai.

Q28Ongoing fare trip ke dauran kaise calculate hota hai?

Trip IN_PROGRESS hote hi fare meter chalu ho jata hai. System periodically driver ki current location leta hai; distance traveled = consecutive location updates ke beech ki dooriyon ka jod; time elapsed = ab ka time − start_time. Ongoing fare = base_fare + (distance × distance_rate) + (time × time_rate) × surge_multiplier.

Rider app har 30 seconds me current fare dikhata hai — jaise 'Current fare: $15.20'. Isse trip ke aakhir me koi surprise nahi milta; rider poore raste paisa badhta dekh raha hota hai. Final fare complete hone par asal route distance se dobara pakka (finalize) hota hai.

Q29Location history kyun store karte ho? Live location hi kaafi nahi?

Live location sirf 'abhi' ke liye hai; history kai kaam aati hai. Trip Update Consumer Kafka se har location event persist karta hai — isse (1) final fare ke liye asal route distance nikalta hai, (2) rider-driver dispute me saboot milta hai ('driver lamba ghuma tha' wala jhagda), (3) fraud detection patterns seekhta hai, (4) operations analytics — driver movement, ETA ki accuracy, demand heatmaps — bante hain, aur (5) driver ko '2 km north jao, wahan rides zyada hain' jaisi salah milti hai.

Isliye design me live ke liye Redis aur history ke liye Kafka → persistent store, dono alag raaste hain.

Q30Pool/shared rides ya scheduled rides jodna ho to design kahan badlega?

Scheduled rides ke liye request me ek future time jud jayega — request pehle se banti hai, matching time aane par trigger hoti hai, aur lesson saaf kehta hai ki surge sirf nayi (immediate) requests par lagta hai, pre-booked rides apne original price par lock rehti hain.

Pool rides zyada bada badlav hai: matching ab ek rider–ek driver nahi rahi; ek driver ke route par kai riders ko batch karna padega — route overlap, pickup order optimization aur har rider ke liye alag fare split (Pool API design me vehicle options me pehle se dikhta hai). Matching algorithm geo-search se aage badh kar route-matching ban jata hai, aur trip entity me ek ride ke andar kai rider-legs aayenge. Baaki reedh — locking, tracking, payment — waisi hi rahegi.

Q31Ek WebSocket gateway kitne users sambhal sakta hai? Connection scaling kaise hoti hai?

Lesson ka number: ek WebSocket gateway instance ~10K concurrent connections. 100K active trips matlab 100K drivers + 100K riders = 200K connections → 20 instances. Aur 1M concurrent users ke liye 100 instances.

Load balancer sticky sessions (IP hash) use karta hai taaki ek trip ke dauran user usi instance par rahe. Instance mar jaye to client exponential backoff (1s, 2s, 4s, 8s, max 30s) se reconnect karta hai aur last timestamp se missed updates maang leta hai — 1–2 second ki rukawat me kaam chal jata hai. Redis Pub/Sub instances ke beech ka pul hai: kisi bhi instance par aaya update sahi rider tak pahunch jata hai.

Q32Zookeeper ki jagah Redis lock (Redlock) use karein to kya trade-off hai?

Redlock ka tareeka simple hai: SETNX lock:driver:D1 {request_id} EX 30 — 1 aaya to lock mila, 0 aaya to pehle se locked. Lekin teen kamzoriyan hain: (1) TTL manually sambhalna padta hai — service slow ho to kaam khatm hone se pehle TTL expire hokar lock doosre ko mil sakta hai. (2) Concurrent attempts ki koi ordering nahi milti — sequential nodes wali fairness gayab. (3) Holder crash ho jaye to TTL ghadi ka intezaar karna padta hai, Zookeeper session-based cleanup zyada saaf hai.

Isliye lesson critical driver assignment ke liye Zookeeper ko pasand karta hai — wahan galti ka matlab hai double booking aur revenue loss. Kam critical locks ke liye Redis lock chal sakta hai.

Q33Surge pricing me smoothing kyun lagate hain? Seedha ratio wala multiplier kyun nahi?

Kyunki bina smoothing ke multiplier har 60 seconds me uchhal-kood karega — 1.0× → 2.0× → 1.3× → 2.2×. Rider ko ye fare ka pagalpan lagega, aur log surge girne ka 'game' khelne lagenge (request-cancel-request). Exponential moving average — new_surge = 0.7 × old + 0.3 × calculated — badlav ko dheere karta hai: 1.2× → 1.35× → 1.5× wali chadhai fair lagti hai.

Saath me teen aur suraksha: max cap 3.0× (price gouging rokna), surge sirf nayi requests par (purani bookings surakshit), aur rider ko surge explicitly accept karna padta hai — bina bataye daam nahi badhta.

Q34Driver aur rider ke beech chat/call jaisi feature ho to woh kahan fit hogi?

Lesson me rider app par match ke baad 'Contact Driver' button (call/message) ka zikr hai. Design ke hisaab se ye feature Notification/Communication layer me baithega — asal phone numbers kabhi share nahi hote; dono taraf masked/proxy numbers ya in-app messaging channel use hota hai, taaki privacy bane rahe aur trip ke baad sampark khatm ho jaye.

Real-time hone ke kaaran messaging bhi WebSocket wale raaste par chalegi, aur saare messages trip_id se jude store honge taaki dispute ke time record mile. Ye lesson ke core flow ko nahi badalta — bas matched trip ke upar ek communication layer jud jati hai.

Q35Interview me ye system design 45 minute me kaise pesh (present) karoge?

Order wahi rakho jo lesson ka hai: (1) Pehle functional requirements saaf karo — 8 features, rider aur driver dono taraf ke. (2) NFRs me CAP wala split batao — assignment strongly consistent, baaki availability-first; numbers bolna mat bhoolna: assignment <1 sec, location 3–5 sec. (3) Core entities aur APIs lifecycle order me. (4) HLD ka diagram banakar har component ka ek-line kaam. (5) Deep dive me teen cheezon par time do — geo-matching (GEORADIUS), Zookeeper lock (double assignment), WebSocket tracking; yahi interviewers ke favourite hain. (6) Surge, cancellation, scaling ko numbers ke saath band karo.

Sabse badi galti: shuru me hi database schema me ghuss jana. Pehle flow, phir gehrai — aur har gehrai par ek number (10ms, 30 sec, 3.0× cap) zaroor rakho.

Q36Rapido jaisi bike-taxi ya Ola jaisi multi-category service me kya alag hoga?

Reedh (backbone) wahi rehti hai — matching, locking, tracking, payment. Farak teen jagah aata hai: (1) Vehicle type ek first-class filter hai — bike, auto, sedan, SUV ke alag geo indexes (drivers:available:bike aise), kyunki rider ko bike ki jagah sedan nahi bhejna. (2) Pricing per-km rates category-wise alag hain — diagram ka note hi kehta hai: Ride = Rs 70/km (waiting Rs 1/min), Cab = Rs 50/km. (3) Bike rides me pickup/drop aksar tang galiyan hoti hain — ETA aur geofence ki precision (50m wali) wahan aur mehtvapurna ho jati hai.

Interview me ye batao ki category bas ek enum field nahi hai — wo matching pool, pricing table aur driver onboarding (documents, vehicle verification) teeno ko alag kar deti hai.
