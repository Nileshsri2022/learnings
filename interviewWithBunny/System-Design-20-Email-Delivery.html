Email Delivery System — Overview

Ek line me yaad rakho: User compose karta hai → Compose Svc validate karta hai → Mail Send Svc → Outbox Delivery Orchestrator (SLA-based priority) → Kafka queues → SMTP relay → Receiver SMTP validate karta hai (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) → Inbox → Mailbox DB → Elastic Search se search index. Yehi poora system hai — baaki sab isi flow ka detail hai.

Ye lesson Gmail / Outlook jaise email system ko scale par design karna sikhata hai. Email duniya ka sabse purana aur sabse bada distributed system hai — 1.8 billion se zyada users, roz billions of emails, aur har email ko guaranteed deliver karna hai, chahe server crash ho jaye, network down ho, ya receiver ka server band ho.

Video — pehle ye dekho

Lesson ka video yahan hai — 32 minute ka deep dive: https://youtu.be/87GhPP3jMp0. Neeche ka SPA usi video + lesson page ka poora Hinglish notes version hai.

End-to-end flow — ek nazar me

  1. Compose: User web client me email likhta hai — To, Cc, Bcc, subject, body, attachment (25MB tak).
  2. Validate: Compose Svc recipients check karta hai, attachment S3 par upload karke virus scan karta hai, draft har 30 second me auto-save hota hai.
  3. Send: Mail Send Svc quota check karta hai (15GB), spam/policy check karta hai, phir email ko Outbox table me likhta hai — yehi durable queue hai.
  4. Orchestrate: Outbox Delivery Orchestrator Kafka se email uthata hai aur SLA ke hisaab se priority decide karta hai — HIGH (<1 min), NORMAL (5 min), LOW (30 min).
  5. Deliver: SMTP Worker receiver ke SMTP server se connect karta hai — DNS MX lookup, EHLO, MAIL FROM, RCPT TO, DATA — phir SPF/DKIM/DMARC validation.
  6. Receive: Receiver ke Inbound SMTP Service email leta hai, spam/malware filter chalata hai, aur Mailbox DB me daalta hai — INBOX ya SPAM folder.
  7. Search: Email Elastic Search me index hota hai — subject, body, sender, attachment sab searchable.
  8. Notify: Notification Svc push notification bhejta hai (VIP sender ho to turant, warna silent badge).

1. Functional Requirements

System ko kya-kya karna chahiye — 8 features. Interview me FR bolte waqt sirf list mat ginao — har feature ke peeche ka kyun bhi bolo.

Feature 1 — Account banao (unique email address)

User apna mailing account bana sakta hai — email address unique hona chahiye. Do users ka same email id kabhi nahi ho sakta. Isiliye account creation ke time high consistency chahiye (CAP wale section me detail hai).

Feature 2 — Compose aur send (To, Cc, Bcc)

User ek ya zyada recipients ko email bhej sakta hai. To — main recipient, Cc — carbon copy (sabko dikhta hai), Bcc — blind carbon copy (kisi aur recipient ko nahi dikhta). Bcc ka data model me alag se dhyaan rakhna padta hai — mailbox_items me bcc_emails field hai, lekin wo sirf sender ko dikhta hai.

Feature 3 — Folders aur labels

User apna Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash dekh sakta hai aur custom labels/folders bana sakta hai. Folder sirf ek folder field hai mailbox_items me — email move karna matlab field update karna, email copy karna nahi.

Feature 4 — Search (subject, body, sender, attachments)

Subject, body, sender — aur attachments ke andar bhi search. Ye feature poora system ka sabse mushkil hissa hai, isiliye iske liye alag Elasticsearch service hai (Step 6 me detail).

Feature 5 — Attachments (25MB tak)

Images, docs, PDFs — 25MB per email tak. Attachment kabhi database me store nahi hota — hamesha S3/blob storage me, DB me sirf uska URL rehta hai.

Feature 6 — Threads (conversation grouping)

Same conversation ke replies ek saath thread me group hote hain. Thread detection In-Reply-To aur References headers se hoti hai (Step 5 me detail).

Feature 7 — Spam filtering, policy checks, virus scan

Har incoming email par spam filter chalta hai, SPF/DKIM/DMARC policy check hota hai (PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN), aur attachment ka virus scan hota hai. Spam score 0.8 se zyada ho to email SPAM folder me jata hai.

Feature 8 — Reliable delivery (retry, bounce, status)

Email deliver hona hi chahiye — retry mechanism, bounce handling (agar recipient exist nahi karta to sender ko batana), aur delivery status notifications. Ye poora lesson ka sabse important feature hai — isiliye Outbox pattern hai.

2. Non-Functional Requirements

System kaisa hona chahiye — scale, performance, reliability. NFR me numbers bolna interview me sabse zyada marks dilata hai.

Scale

DimensionRequirement
Users1.8B+ users, roz billions of emails sent/received
StoragePetabytes of email data; attachments S3/blob storage me
TrafficPeak hours me lakhs of emails per minute

CAP Theorem — Availability >> Consistency

Email system me Availability, Consistency se zyada important hai — user ko apna inbox hamesha khulna chahiye, chahe koi replica thoda peeche ho. Lekin ek jagah high consistency zaroori hai: same user account ke liye — koi duplicate email same email_id ke saath kabhi nahi aana chahiye. Isiliye mailbox_items ka composite primary key hai (user_id, email_id) — duplicate insert hi nahi ho sakta.

Durability — 11 9's

Email kabhi kho nahi sakta. S3 11 9's durability deta hai (99.999999999%) — matlab practically zero data loss, chahe server crash ho jaye. Outbox pattern bhi isi liye hai: email pehle durable DB me likha jata hai, tab jaake Kafka me publish hota hai.

Reliability — guaranteed delivery

Email delivery guaranteed hai — retry mechanisms ke saath. Temporary failure ho to exponential backoff se retry (1 min, 5 min, 15 min — max 10 attempts, 24 ghante). Uske baad bhi fail ho to DLQ (Dead Letter Queue) me jata hai, jahan ops team manually investigate karti hai.

Interview tip: NFR bolte waqt hamesha number ke saath bolo — "1.8B users, petabytes storage, 11 9's durability, <100ms search". Bina number ke NFR sirf theory lagta hai.

3. Core Entities

System ke 7 core entities — inke fields yaad rakho, kyunki schema design ke sawaal yahin se aate hain. Har entity ka email_id poore system me unique hota hai.

Entity 1 — Users

user_id, email_address (unique), password_hash, name, quota (15GB default), created_at

Entity 2 — Message / Email

email_id (poore system me unique), message_id (RFC 5322), thread_id (conversation grouping), from_email, to_emails[], cc_emails[], bcc_emails[], subject, body (HTML/plain text), has_attachment, url_attachment, sent_at, metadata

email_id vs message_id — farq samjho: email_id system ka apna UUID hai (internal tracking ke liye). message_id RFC 5322 standard hai — format <{email_id}@gmail.com> — jo email headers me jata hai aur receiver side deduplication ke liye use hota hai.

Entity 3 — Thread / Conversation

thread_id, subject (pehle email ka subject), participant_emails[], email_ids[] (sent_at ke order me), last_updated_at, message_count

Entity 4 — Email Metadata

labels[] (Inbox/Sent/Drafts/Spam/Trash/Custom), is_read, is_starred, importance (high/normal/low)

Entity 5 — Mailbox_items (Inbox schema)

user_id, email_id, message_id, thread_id, from_email, to_emails, subject, snippet (body ke pehle 100 characters), has_attachment, url_attachment (S3 URLs)

Entity 6 — Outbox (pending sends)

user_id, email_id, message_id, thread_id, from_email, to_emails, subject, body, has_attachment, url_attachment, scheduled_at (retry / delayed send ke liye)

Entity 7 — Attachment

attachment_id, email_id, filename, size_bytes, content_type, s3_url (blob storage), virus_scan_status

Snippet kyun? Inbox list me poora body load karna heavy hota hai. Isiliye mailbox_items me snippet rehta hai — body ke pehle 100 characters — taaki inbox listing fast ho. Poora body tabhi load hota hai jab user email kholta hai.

Architecture ek line me: clients → Load Balancer & API Gateway → Services → Databases. Har service ka apna kaam hai, apna database hai.

Mailbox DB

Mailbox DB do cheezein store karta hai: Mailbox_items (Inbox — jo email aa chuka hai) aur Outbox (pending sends — jo email jaana baaki hai). Dono ka schema section 7 me detail me hai.

Outside Internet — Receiver SMTP

Email system ke bahar receiver SMTP servers hote hain (Yahoo SMTP, Outlook SMTP) — ye emails SMTP protocol se receive karte hain. Aur Inbound SMTP Service bahar se aane wale emails receive karta hai (Outlook/Yahoo SMTP → Gmail Inbox).

6. Deep Dive — Step 1: Composition & Draft Saving

Sabse pehle user email likhta hai. Example: To — alice@yahoo.com, Cc — bob@outlook.com, Subject — "Meeting Tomorrow", Body — "Hi Alice...", Attachment — presentation.pdf (5MB).

Attachment upload (agar attachment hai)

POST /v1/attachments/upload — multipart/form-data ke saath {file: presentation.pdf}. Compose Service file ko S3 par upload karta hai: s3://email-attachments/{user_id}/{email_id}/presentation.pdf. Phir ClamAV virus scan karta hai — agar infected hai to reject, warna clean mark karta hai. Response: {attachmentId: 'ATT123', storageUrl: 's3://...', size: 5MB, contentType: 'application/pdf'}

Draft save — auto-save har 30 second

POST /v1/emails/draft — body: {to: ['alice@yahoo.com'], cc: ['bob@outlook.com'], subject, body, attachmentId: 'ATT123'}. Compose Service:

  • email_id (UUID) generate karta hai
  • message_id banata hai (RFC 5322: <{email_id}@gmail.com>)
  • INSERT INTO drafts (email_id, user_id, to_emails, subject, body, has_attachment, url_attachment, updated_at)
  • Response: {emailId: 'E123', status: 'DRAFT'}

Draft retrieval

GET /v1/emails/E123 — poora email object return karta hai, taaki user edit kar sake. Draft auto-save isiliye important hai: agar browser crash ho jaye ya light chali jaye, to likha hua email kho nahi jata.

Draft aur Outbox me farq: Draft wo email hai jo abhi bheja nahi gaya (user likh raha hai). Outbox wo email hai jo bhejne ke liye ready hai (user ne Send daba diya). Dono alag tables hain, alag purpose hai.

6. Deep Dive — Step 2: Send Flow (Outbox & Delivery Orchestrator)

User Send dabata hai — ab asli system design shuru hota hai. Ye step poore lesson ka dil hai.

1. Mail Send Service validation (3 checks)

  • Quota check: SELECT used_quota, quota_limit FROM users WHERE user_id={user_id} — agar used_quota >= quota_limit ho to 403 "Quota exceeded" return hota hai.
  • Recipients validate: Email format sahi hai? Domain resolve hota hai? (DNS MX record lookup) Anti-spoofing checks — sender domain verification.
  • Spam/policy check: Content analysis (suspicious keywords, excessive links), rate limiting (max 500 emails/day free users ke liye), suspicious pattern ho to CAPTCHA.

2. Outbox me insert — durable queue

INSERT INTO outbox (user_id, email_id, message_id, thread_id, from_email, to_emails, subject, body, has_attachment, url_attachment, scheduled_at: now()) — Outbox table ek durable queue hai. Email pehle DB me likha jata hai, tab jaake aage badhta hai. Server crash ho jaye to bhi email safe hai.

3. Kafka publish

Kafka.send('email.outbound', {emailId: 'E123', toEmails: [...], priority: 'NORMAL', createdAt: timestamp}) — topic email.outbound, 100 partitions. Dhyaan do: Kafka message me sirf emailId jata hai, poora body nahi — body 25MB tak ho sakta hai, Kafka message limit 1MB hai.

4. Response — 200 OK, optimistic UI

200 OK {emailId: 'E123', status: 'SENDING'} — user ko email turant Sent folder me dikhta hai (optimistic UI), lekin asli delivery async hoti hai, peeche se.

Critical — Interview me yehi poochhte hain

Outbox pattern mandatory hai. KABHI Kafka me publish mat karo bina Outbox DB me persist kiye. Agar Kafka publish ho gaya aur server crash ho gaya DB commit se pehle — to email lost. Outbox at-least-once delivery guarantee karta hai (email crash se bachta hai, success tak retry hota hai).

Outbox Delivery Orchestrator Kafka ka email.outbound topic consume karta hai — 100 consumer instances, ek per partition. Iska kaam: har email ki priority decide karna aur sahi SMTP queue me bhejna.

6. Deep Dive — Step 4: SMTP Delivery & SPF/DKIM/DMARC

SMTP Worker priority queues se email uthata hai — High: 50 workers, Normal: 100 workers, Low: 20 workers. Phir receiver ke SMTP server se baat hoti hai. Ye 14 steps diagram se liye gaye hain — interview me inhe sequence me bolna hai.

Steps to connect SMTP server

  1. DNS Request (Checks/Pings): dig MX outlook.com → MX records: outlook-com.olc.protection.outlook.com (priority 5)
  2. MX Receiver Response: SMTP server ke IP addresses return hote hain
  3. SMTP connection: Receiver SMTP se TCP connection (port 25/587/465), TLS handshake encryption ke liye
  4. SMTP handshake: EHLO gmail.com — receiver respond karta hai: 250-outlook.com Hello [IP]
  5. MAIL FROM: MAIL FROM:<sender@gmail.com> — receiver sender domain validate karta hai
  6. RCPT TO: RCPT TO:<alice@outlook.com> — receiver check karta hai recipient exist karta hai ya nahi (local delivery table)
  7. DATA: Email headers + body bhejte hain — Subject, From, To, Date, Message-ID, MIME headers (attachments ke liye)
  8. Email body: Plain text aur/ya HTML multipart, attachments Base64 encoded
  9. '.' (period): Email data khatam hone ka signal
  10. QUIT: Connection terminate
  11. SPF record check: Receiver DNS TXT record query karta hai — spf.gmail.com TXT: v=spf1 ip4:74.125.0.0/16 ~all — sending IP authorized hai ya nahi
  12. DKIM signature: Email headers me DKIM-Signature hota hai — receiver public key fetch karta hai DNS se (default._domainkey.gmail.com), signature verify karta hai (tampering rokta hai)
  13. DMARC check: Receiver DMARC policy check karta hai _dmarc.gmail.com par — policy p=reject (strict), p=quarantine, ya p=none. SPF/DKIM fail + DMARC=reject → email reject
  14. Delivery confirmation: 250 OK — Message accepted for delivery

SPF — Sender Policy Framework

SPF batata hai: kaunsi IP addresses is domain ke liye email bhej sakti hain. Receiver DNS se v=spf1 ip4:74.125.0.0/16 ~all padhta hai aur check karta hai — sending IP allowed range me hai? PASS ho to theek, FAIL ho to likely spoofed email hai — reject ya spam mark.

DKIM — DomainKeys Identified Mail

DKIM batata hai: email raaste me badla to nahi gaya. Sender apni private key se email sign karta hai (DKIM-Signature header). Receiver DNS se public key fetch karta hai (default._domainkey.gmail.com), body hash aur header signature verify karta hai. Match ho to PASS, warna FAIL — matlab email tamper hua hai.

DMARC — Domain-based Message Authentication

DMARC policy enforce karta hai: _dmarc.gmail.com par v=DMARC1; p=reject. Pehle alignment check hota hai — From header ka domain SPF/DKIM domain se match karta hai? SPF PASS + aligned ya DKIM PASS + aligned → DMARC PASS. Dono FAIL ho to policy ke hisaab se: reject → email reject (550), quarantine → spam folder, none → accept, sirf log.

Delivery ke baad

Outbox update hota hai: UPDATE outbox SET status='SENT', sent_at=now() WHERE email_id='E123', phir DELETE FROM outbox (pending queue se hatao). Aur Sent folder me insert: INSERT INTO mailbox_items (user_id, email_id, folder: 'SENT', ...) — user ko email Sent me dikhta hai.

Diagram-only detail: Diagram me MX Cache aur MX Resolver alag components hain (DNS lookup fast karne ke liye), aur Validation DB ke fields bhi diye hain — messageId, sender_user_id, recipient_email, recipient_domain, route_type (INTERNAL/EXTERNAL), policy_check aur spam_check (PASS/FAIL/PENDING), attachment_check, status.

8. Scaling & Optimization — 12 Techniques

1.8B users tak scale karne ke liye 12 techniques — har ek ka naam, kya karta hai, aur number yaad rakho. Interview me "how will you scale this?" ka jawab yahi hai.

Technique 1 — Outbox pattern

Durable queue: INSERT into outbox → Kafka publish → SMTP send → DELETE from outbox. Server crash par email lost nahi hota, exponential backoff se retry hota hai.

Technique 2 — SLA-based priority queues

3 Kafka topics (high/normal/low). HIGH — same domain aur known contacts, <1 min delivery. NORMAL — external, 5 min. LOW — bulk, 30 min, rate limited. Worker allocation: 50/100/20.

Technique 3 — Consistent hashing

sending_user_id se hash karke same Kafka partition — ek user ke emails hamesha same consumer ke paas, send order preserved (A phir B → A pehle deliver).

Technique 4 — Database sharding

Mailbox_items user_id se shard — ek user ke saare emails ek shard par. Shard routing: hash(user_id) mod N. Billions of emails tak horizontal scaling.

Technique 5 — Elasticsearch for search

Subject/body par full-text search, stemming (meeting → meet), ngram autocomplete, faceted filters (folder, has_attachment, date range). 10 shards per index, <100ms query latency.

Technique 6 — S3 for attachments

Attachments blob storage me (DB me nahi). Presigned URLs se direct upload (server bypass). Lifecycle policy — 1 saal baad Glacier me archive, 90% cost saving purane attachments par. Download se pehle virus scan.

Technique 7 — Kafka event-driven architecture

Services decoupled: Mail Send → SMTP Workers → Mailbox Receiver → Search Indexer → Notification. Async processing — user ko 200 OK turant, asli delivery baad me. Consumers badha kar horizontal scale.

Technique 8 — SMTP connection pooling

Frequently contacted domains ke saath persistent connections (gmail.com → outlook.com connection reuse). TLS handshake ~200ms bachta hai har baar. Pool size: 100 per SMTP worker.

Technique 9 — Spam filtering pipeline

Multi-stage, total <400ms: (1) IP reputation check — blacklist lookup <10ms, (2) SPF/DKIM/DMARC validation — DNS queries ~50ms, (3) Content analysis — Bayesian filter ~100ms, (4) Link/attachment scan ~200ms. Jahan possible ho, parallel processing.

Technique 10 — Thread detection caching

Recent threads Redis me (key: message_id, value: thread_id), TTL 7 din. Har reply par DB query nahi — 95% cache hit rate active conversations ke liye.

Technique 11 — Read replica scaling

1 master (writes: naye emails, outbox updates) + 10 read replicas (reads: inbox listing, search results, thread views). Read/write split, replicas lag <1 sec — email dekhne ke liye acceptable hai.

Technique 12 — Rate limiting

Per user: 500 emails/day free, 2000/day paid. Per IP: 100 requests/min (spam bots rokne ke liye). Per domain: naye domain ko gradual ramp-up — pehle 10 emails slow, phir badhao agar bounce nahi aaye.

9. Interview Q&A — Reliable Delivery & Retry

Sawaal: Reliable email delivery kaise ensure karte ho — retry mechanisms aur failures kaise handle karte ho? Ye lesson ka sabse zyada poochha jaane wala sawaal hai.

(1) Outbox pattern — durable queue

User email bhejta hai → INSERT INTO outbox (durable storage) → Kafka email.outbound publish → SMTP worker process karta hai. Success ho to DELETE from outbox, fail ho to outbox me hi rehta hai retry ke liye. Guarantee: email server crash se bachta hai (DB me persisted hai), at-least-once delivery (success ya max attempts tak retry).

(2) Retry strategy — exponential backoff

1st retry 1 min baad, 2nd 5 min baad, 3rd 15 min baad — max 10 attempts, 24 ghante ke andar. Retry tabhi hota hai jab failure temporary ho: 4xx SMTP codes (jaise 421 Service unavailable, 450 Mailbox busy), network timeouts, receiver server down.

(3) Failure handling — permanent failures

5xx codes (jaise 550 User not found, 552 Mailbox full) — retry band karo, status='FAILED' mark karo, aur sender ko bounce notification bhejo: "Delivery failed: User alice@yahoo.com does not exist" — sender ke inbox me system message ke roop me INSERT hota hai.

(4) Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)

Max retries (10 attempts, 24 ghante) ke baad email DLQ Kafka topic (email.failed) me jata hai. Ops team manually investigate karti hai. Possible issues: misconfigured recipient server, persistent network partition, invalid recipient jo initial validation se nikal gaya.

(5) Delivery confirmation

SMTP 250 OK response → receiver ne email accept kiya. Tab UPDATE outbox SET status='SENT', sent_at=now() aur DELETE from outbox. Lekin dhyaan do: receiver SMTP accept karne ke baad bhi spam filter se reject kar sakta hai — final delivery DSN (Delivery Status Notification) se track hoti hai, agar supported ho.

(6) Idempotency — duplicate kaise rokte hain

Retry ki wajah se same email_id multiple baar bheja ja sakta hai. Receiver message_id header se deduplicate karta hai: Message-ID: <{email_id}@gmail.com> — same message_id dobara aaye to duplicate inbox me nahi jata.

Interview answer — ek line me

Outbox pattern (durable DB queue) + Kafka + SMTP workers with exponential backoff retry (1 min, 5 min, 15 min, max 10 attempts, 24 hr). Failures → bounce notification. DLQ for manual investigation. Idempotency via message_id header.

9. Interview Q&A — SLA Priority Routing

Sawaal: Outbox Delivery Orchestrator SLA-based priority routing kaise implement karta hai?

(1) Priority determination

HIGH priority (target <1 min): Same domain (gmail.com → gmail.com, internal routing), known contacts (recipient sender ke contacts me hai, auto-suggestion enabled), VIP senders. NORMAL priority (target 5 min): External first-time contact (gmail.com → yahoo.com, koi prior interaction nahi), subject me urgent keywords nahi. LOW priority (target 30 min): Bulk sends (>100 recipients), newsletter emails (unsubscribe link ya bulk headers se detect), marketing campaigns.

(2) Implementation

Orchestrator email.outbound consume karta hai aur priority decide karta hai:

Priority logic
IF (recipient_domain == sender_domain):
    priority = 'HIGH'        // Same domain
ELSE IF (recipient IN sender_contacts):
    priority = 'HIGH'        // Known contact
ELSE IF (subject CONTAINS ['urgent', 'asap', 'immediate']):
    priority = 'ELEVATED'
ELSE IF (recipient_count > 100 OR 'List-Unsubscribe' header present):
    priority = 'LOW'
ELSE:
    priority = 'NORMAL'

Phir sahi Kafka topic me route: Kafka.send('email.smtp.high', emailPayload) ya normal ya low.

9. Interview Q&A — SMTP + SPF/DKIM/DMARC Walkthrough

Sawaal: Complete SMTP delivery process samjhao — SPF, DKIM, aur DMARC validation ke saath. Ye walkthrough sequence me yaad rakho.

(1) DNS MX lookup

SMTP worker ko alice@outlook.com ko bhejna hai. Query: dig MX outlook.com → Response: outlook-com.olc.protection.outlook.com (priority 5) IP addresses ke saath. Sabse low priority number wala MX server chuno (priority 5 = highest priority, low number = high priority).

(2) TCP connection

Receiver SMTP server se connect: port 25 (SMTP), 587 (submission), ya 465 (SMTPS with TLS). TLS handshake: STARTTLS command se encrypted connection — email interception (MITM attacks) rokta hai.

(3) SMTP handshake

Client: EHLO gmail.com. Server: 250-outlook.com Hello [74.125.0.1]. Server apne supported extensions batata hai: 250-STARTTLS, 250-AUTH PLAIN LOGIN, 250-SIZE 35000000 (max 35MB email).

(4) Sender verification

Client: MAIL FROM:<sender@gmail.com>. Server: 250 OK (sender accept karta hai, SPF se baad me check bhi kar sakta hai).

(5) Recipient verification

Client: RCPT TO:<alice@outlook.com>. Server local delivery table check karta hai: SELECT user_id FROM users WHERE email='alice@outlook.com'. Exist kare to 250 OK, nahi to 550 User not found (reject — sender ko bounce milta hai).

(6) Email transmission

Client: DATA. Server: 354 Start mail input; end with <CRLF>.<CRLF>. Client headers + body bhejta hai: From, To, Subject, Date, Message-ID <E123@gmail.com>, DKIM-Signature (v=1; a=rsa-sha256; d=gmail.com; s=default; h=from:to:subject; bh=base64hash; b=signaturebase64), Content-Type multipart/mixed (attachments ho to), blank line, body (plain text ya HTML), attachments Base64 encoded. Phir . (akeli line par period = message khatam).

(7) SPF validation

Server DNS query karta hai: dig TXT gmail.com → v=spf1 ip4:74.125.0.0/16 ip4:74.125.224.0/19 ~all. Check: sending IP (74.125.0.1) allowed ranges me hai? PASS (IP authorized) ya FAIL (unauthorized IP, likely spoofed). FAIL ho to reject ya spam mark kar sakta hai.

(8) DKIM validation

Server DKIM-Signature header padhta hai: d=gmail.com (signing domain), s=default (selector — kaunsi key use karni hai), h=from:to:subject (signed headers), bh (body ka hash), b (headers ka signature). Public key fetch: dig TXT default._domainkey.gmail.com (RSA ya Ed25519). Verify: body hash karo → bh se compare karo; headers ko public key se verify karo → b se compare karo. PASS (signature valid, email tamper nahi hua) ya FAIL (signature invalid, email modify hua).

(9) DMARC validation

Server query karta hai: dig TXT _dmarc.gmail.com → v=DMARC1; p=reject; rua=mailto:dmarc@gmail.com (policy=reject strict, aggregate report dmarc@gmail.com ko). Alignment check: From header ka domain SPF/DKIM domain se match karta hai? SPF: MAIL FROM domain = gmail.com? DKIM: d= domain = gmail.com? SPF PASS + aligned → DMARC PASS. DKIM PASS + aligned → DMARC PASS. Dono FAIL + policy=reject → Reject (550 DMARC policy violation). Dono FAIL + quarantine → accept lekin spam mark. Dono FAIL + none → accept, sirf log.

(10) Final acceptance

Sab checks pass (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, spam filters) → Server: 250 OK — Message accepted for delivery. Client: QUIT. Server: 221 Goodbye (connection close).

(11) Post-delivery

Server email ko alice@outlook.com ke mailbox me daalta hai, Gmail ko DMARC aggregate report bhejta hai (daily summary of auth results), Gmail delivery confirmation track karta hai aur outbox status='SENT' update karta hai.

Result

Authenticated email delivery with anti-spoofing protection — SPF IP spoofing rokta hai, DKIM email tampering rokta hai, DMARC policy enforce karta hai. Legitimate emails deliver hote hain, spam/phishing block hote hain.

FLesson 20 · Email Delivery System

Interview FAQs

Interview me poochhe jaane wale 36 sawaal — reliable delivery, SMTP handshake, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, spam filtering, scaling aur search se. Har jawab Hinglish me, detail ke saath.

Q01Email system design karte waqt pehle kya scope poochhna chahiye?
Pehle ye 5 cheezein clear karo: kitne users (yahan 1.8B+), roz kitne emails (billions), attachment limit kya hai (25MB per email), search chahiye ya nahi (subject/body/sender), aur delivery guarantee kya hai (at-least-once ya exactly-once). Scope clear kiye bina design shuru karna interview me sabse badi galti hai — kyunki scale hi saare decisions badal deta hai. Jaise 100 users ke liye single DB kaafi hai, lekin 1.8B users ke liye sharding, Kafka aur Elasticsearch zaroori ho jate hain.
Q02Outbox pattern kya hai aur ye reliable delivery ke liye kyun zaroori hai?
Outbox pattern me email bhejne se pehle use Outbox naam ki durable DB table me likha jata hai, aur uske BAAD Kafka me publish hota hai. Agar server Kafka publish ke baad crash ho jaye, to email Outbox me safe rehta hai aur retry ho sakta hai. Bina Outbox ke — seedha Kafka publish karo aur crash ho jaye to email hamesha ke liye lost. Isiliye rule hai: pehle DB commit, phir Kafka publish. Ye at-least-once delivery guarantee karta hai.
Q03Kafka message me email body kyun nahi bhejte?
Kyunki email body attachments ke saath 25MB tak ho sakta hai, jabki Kafka ka message size limit 1MB hai. Bada message bhejoge to broker overload ho jayega aur baaki messages slow ho jayenge. Sahi tareeka: DB me poora email store karo, Kafka me sirf email_id bhejo. SMTP worker jab process kare, tab DB se poora email fetch kar le. Kafka lightweight rehta hai aur system fast rehta hai.
Q04At-least-once aur exactly-once delivery me kya farq hai? Email me kaunsa use hota hai?
At-least-once ka matlab: email kam se kam ek baar to deliver hoga hi — ho sakta hai retry ki wajah se do baar bhi deliver ho jaye. Exactly-once ka matlab: email ek hi baar deliver hoga, kabhi duplicate nahi. Email system me at-least-once use hota hai, kyunki network failures ke beech exactly-once guarantee karna practically impossible hai. Duplicate ko message_id se deduplicate karke handle karte hain — receiver same message_id dobara aaye to ignore kar deta hai.
Q05Duplicate email inbox me kaise rokte hain?
Do tareeke hain. Pehla: mailbox_items ka composite primary key (user_id, email_id) — same user ke liye same email_id dobara insert hi nahi ho sakta, database khud reject kar dega. Doosra: message_id header (RFC 5322) — retry ki wajah se same email dobara bheja jaye to receiver message_id dekh kar pehchan leta hai ki ye duplicate hai aur use ignore karta hai. Dono mil kar duplicate-free inbox guarantee karte hain.
Q06SPF kya karta hai? Ek line me samjhao.
SPF batata hai ki kaunsi IP addresses ek domain ke liye email bhej sakti hain. Sender apne DNS me TXT record publish karta hai — jaise v=spf1 ip4:74.125.0.0/16 ~all — matlab sirf is IP range se aane wale emails asli hain. Receiver sending IP ko is list se match karta hai. Match ho to PASS, warna FAIL — matlab koi aur aapke naam se email bhej raha hai (spoofing). SPF IP spoofing rokta hai.
Q07DKIM kya karta hai? SPF se kaise alag hai?
DKIM batata hai ki email raaste me badla to nahi gaya. Sender apni private key se email ke headers aur body ka digital signature banata hai (DKIM-Signature header). Receiver DNS se public key fetch karta hai (default._domainkey.gmail.com) aur signature verify karta hai. Agar body ya header me ek bhi character badla ho to signature fail ho jata hai. SPF IP check karta hai (kaun bhej raha hai), DKIM content check karta hai (kya bheja gaya hai) — dono alag-alag attacks rokte hain.
Q08DMARC ki teen policies — reject, quarantine, none — me kya farq hai?
DMARC tab kaam karta hai jab SPF aur DKIM dono fail ho jayein. Policy p=reject ho to email seedha reject kar diya jata hai (550 error, sender ko bounce). Policy p=quarantine ho to email accept hota hai lekin SPAM folder me dala jata hai. Policy p=none ho to email accept hota hai aur sirf log me entry hoti hai — kuch block nahi hota. Naye domain ke liye pehle p=none se shuru karte hain (monitoring), phir quarantine, aur finally p=reject — taaki genuine emails galti se block na ho jayein.
Q09MX record kya hai? dig MX outlook.com kya return karta hai?
MX (Mail Exchange) record batata hai ki kisi domain ke emails kaunsa server receive karega. dig MX outlook.com karne par milta hai: outlook-com.olc.protection.outlook.com (priority 5) IP addresses ke saath. Priority number jitna chhota, utni zyada preference — matlab pehle us server ko try karo. Agar wo server down ho to agle priority wale MX server ko try karte hain. Bina MX lookup ke SMTP worker ko pata hi nahi chalega ki email kahan bhejna hai.
Q10SMTP handshake ke steps kya hain? Sequence me batao.
Pehle DNS MX lookup se receiver server ka IP nikalo. Phir TCP connection (port 25/587/465) aur TLS handshake (STARTTLS). Phir EHLO gmail.com — server 250 se respond karta hai aur apni capabilities batata hai. Phir MAIL FROM: — sender verify. Phir RCPT TO: — recipient exist karta hai ya nahi. Phir DATA — headers + body bhejo. Phir akeli line par period (.) — message khatam. Aakhir me QUIT — connection close. Har step par server 250/354 jaise codes se respond karta hai.
Q11SMTP codes 250, 354, 421, 450, 550, 552 ka matlab kya hai?
250 OK — command accept hua, sab theek hai. 354 — DATA bhejna shuru karo, period se khatam karo. 421 — service unavailable (temporary, retry karo). 450 — mailbox busy (temporary, retry karo). 550 — user not found (permanent, retry mat karo, bounce bhejo). 552 — mailbox full (permanent). Rule yaad rakho: 4xx matlab temporary failure — retry karo; 5xx matlab permanent failure — retry band karo aur sender ko bounce notification do.
Q12Bounce handling kaise hoti hai?
Jab receiver permanent error deta hai — jaise 550 User not found ya 552 Mailbox full — to SMTP worker retry band kar deta hai, outbox me status='FAILED' mark karta hai, aur sender ko bounce notification bhejta hai: 'Delivery failed: User alice@yahoo.com does not exist'. Ye bounce sender ke inbox me ek system message ke roop me insert hota hai, taaki sender ko pata chale ki uska email deliver nahi hua aur kyun nahi hua.
Q13Exponential backoff retry schedule kya hai?
Pehla retry 1 minute baad, doosra 5 minute baad, teesra 15 minute baad — aur aise hi gap badhate hue max 10 attempts, 24 ghante ke andar. Gap badhane ka reason: agar receiver server down hai to use recover hone ka time do; turant-turant retry karoge to uska load aur badh jayega. Retry sirf temporary failures (4xx codes, network timeout) par hota hai — permanent failures (5xx) par retry nahi hota.
Q14Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) kab use hoti hai?
Jab email max retries (10 attempts, 24 ghante) ke baad bhi deliver nahi hota, to use DLQ Kafka topic (email.failed) me daal dete hain. DLQ ka matlab ye nahi ki email delete ho gaya — wo wahan safe pada rehta hai aur ops team manually investigate karti hai. Common reasons: misconfigured recipient server, persistent network partition, ya invalid recipient jo initial validation se nikal gaya. DLQ ke bina aise emails silent kho jate.
Q15SLA priority routing kaise decide hoti hai? HIGH, NORMAL, LOW ka matlab?
Priority 4 cheezon se decide hoti hai: same domain ho (gmail → gmail) ya recipient contacts me ho to HIGH — target 1 minute se kam delivery, 50 workers. Subject me urgent/asap/immediate ho to ELEVATED. Bulk send ya newsletter ho (100+ recipients ya List-Unsubscribe header) to LOW — target 30 minute, 20 workers, rate limited. Baaki sab NORMAL — target 5 minute, 100 workers. Isse important emails fast pahunchte hain aur bulk emails system ko slow nahi karte.
Q16Auto-suggestion ka priority routing se kya relation hai?
Auto-suggestion ka matlab hai: jab user To field me type karta hai to system contacts se naam suggest karta hai. Agar suggestion milta hai to matlab recipient jaana-pehchana hai — ya to contacts me hai ya pehle email interaction hua hai. Aise recipients ko HIGH priority milti hai, kyunki jaane-pehchane logon ko email fast pahunchna chahiye. Naye/unknown recipient ko koi suggestion nahi milta, to wo NORMAL priority me jata hai. Lesson ke 6 scenarios me se 3 me auto-suggestion hi HIGH priority ka trigger hai.
Q17Ek hi user ke emails ka order kaise preserve hota hai?
Consistent hashing se. Email ko sending_user_id se hash karke Kafka partition decide hota hai — matlab ek user ke saare emails hamesha same partition aur same consumer ke paas jate hain. Consumer ek-ek karke process karta hai, to order preserve rehta hai: user ne Email A phir Email B bheja to A pehle deliver hoga, B baad me. Agar alag-alag partitions me jate to B pehle aur A baad me deliver ho sakta tha — jo user ko confusing lagta.
Q18Spam score 0.8 ka matlab kya hai? Spam pipeline ke stages aur timings kya hain?
Spam score 0 se 1 ke beech ek number hai — kitni probability hai ki email spam hai. Score 0.8 se zyada ho to email SPAM folder me jata hai. Pipeline 4 stages me total 400ms se kam me chalta hai: (1) IP reputation check — blacklist lookup, 10ms se kam; (2) SPF/DKIM/DMARC validation — DNS queries, lagbhag 50ms; (3) Content analysis — Bayesian filter, lagbhag 100ms; (4) Link aur attachment scan — lagbhag 200ms. Jahan possible ho stages parallel chalte hain taaki total time kam rahe.
Q19Bayesian filter spam detect kaise karta hai?
Bayesian filter words ki probability se kaam karta hai. Use pehle se pata hota hai ki kaunse words spam emails me zyada aate hain (jaise 'free money', 'click now', 'lottery') aur kaunse normal emails me. Naye email ke words dekh kar wo probability nikalta hai ki ye email spam hai ya nahi. Jaise-jaise zyada emails aate hain, filter seekhta rehta hai aur accurate hota jata hai. Yehi wajah hai ki spam folder me kabhi-kabhi genuine email bhi chala jata hai — filter probability par kaam karta hai, certainty par nahi.
Q20Thread detection kaise hoti hai? Reply ko purane thread se kaise jodte hain?
Har reply email ke headers me In-Reply-To aur References fields hote hain, jinme us message ka message_id hota hai jiska ye reply hai. System in headers ko existing message_id se match karta hai. Match mil jaye to thread ko UPDATE karta hai — email_id ko array me append karta hai aur last_updated_at refresh karta hai. Match na mile to naya thread create hota hai. Recent threads Redis me cache hote hain (message_id → thread_id, 7 din TTL, 95% hit rate) taaki har reply par database query na karni pade.
Q21Search ke liye Elasticsearch kyun use karte hain? Database LIKE query kyun nahi?
Database me LIKE '%meeting%' query poori table scan karti hai — billions of emails par ye seconds ya minutes legi. Elasticsearch inverted index banata hai — har word ke liye pehle se pata hota hai ki wo kin emails me hai — to search milliseconds me ho jati hai (100ms se kam). Uske upar stemming (meeting → meet) aur ngram (autocomplete) jaise features database me possible hi nahi hain. Isiliye search ka data alag se Elasticsearch me index hota hai, aur database sirf storage ke liye rehta hai.
Q22Stemming aur ngram autocomplete kaise kaam karta hai?
Stemming words ko unke root form me badal deta hai — meeting, meetings, meet — teeno ka root 'meet' hai. To user 'meeting' search kare to 'meet' wala email bhi mil jata hai. Ngram subject ko chhote-chhote tukdon me tod deta hai — 'meeting' se 'me', 'mee', 'meet', 'meeti' jaise tukde index hote hain. Jab user 'meet...' type karta hai to completion suggester in tukdon se match karke suggestions deta hai: 'meeting', 'meetings', 'meet and greet'. Dono mil kar search aur autocomplete fast aur smart banate hain.
Q2325MB attachment limit kaise enforce hoti hai? S3 presigned URL flow kya hai?
Attachment upload ke time Compose Service file ka size check karta hai — 25MB se zyada ho to seedha reject. Upload S3 par hota hai, database me nahi — DB me sirf S3 URL rehta hai. Presigned URL flow me server user ko ek temporary signed URL deta hai, jisse user ka browser seedha S3 par upload karta hai — file server se hokar nahi jati, to server ka bandwidth aur time bachta hai. Upload ke baad ClamAV virus scan hota hai — infected file ho to reject aur clean ho to attachmentId milta hai, jo send email API me use hota hai.
Q24Virus scan kab hota hai — upload par ya download par?
Dono par. Upload ke time ClamAV scan karta hai — infected file ho to upload hi reject ho jata hai aur attachmentId nahi milta. Lekin lesson ye bhi kehta hai ki download se pehle bhi virus scan hota hai — kyunki virus definitions update hote rehte hain, jo file upload ke time clean thi wo baad me infected nikli ho sakti hai. Isiliye virus_scan_status field PENDING, CLEAN ya INFECTED hoti hai, aur download tabhi allow hota hai jab status CLEAN ho.
Q2515GB quota kaise enforce hoti hai? 403 kab aata hai?
Har user ke liye users table me quota_limit (15GB free) aur quota_used (ab tak kitne bytes use hue) rehta hai. Send karte waqt Mail Send Service SELECT karke check karta hai — agar used_quota >= quota_limit ho to 403 'Quota exceeded' return hota hai aur email send nahi hota. Quota_used har email aur attachment store hote hi update hota hai. Email delete karne par quota free hoti hai — isiliye Trash khali karna quota wapas deta hai.
Q26Rate limiting kitne levels par hoti hai?
Teen levels par. Per user: free account 500 emails/day, paid account 2000 emails/day — isse zyada bhejoge to block. Per IP: 100 requests/minute — spam bots ko rokne ke liye, kyunki bot ek IP se hazaaron requests bhejta hai. Per domain: naye domain ko gradual ramp-up — pehle 10 emails slow bhejte hain, agar bounce nahi aaye to rate badhate hain. Ye teeno mil kar spam aur abuse rokte hain, aur genuine users ko koi takleef nahi hoti.
Q27Mailbox_items ko user_id se shard kyun karte hain? Cross-shard query kab hoti hai?
Kyunki ek user ke saare kaam — inbox listing, thread view, folder change — ek hi user ke data par hote hain. Agar ek user ke saare emails ek hi shard par hain to ye queries single shard se fast ho jati hain, bina doosre shards ko chhede. Shard routing hash(user_id) mod N se hoti hai. Cross-shard query sirf global search ke liye chahiye hoti — jaise saare users me koi word dhoondhna — aur wo kaam Elasticsearch karta hai, database nahi. Isiliye sharding billions of emails tak scale karta hai.
Q28Read replicas ka lag 1 second se kam acceptable kyun hai?
Email system me 1 master (writes: naye emails, outbox updates) aur 10 read replicas (reads: inbox listing, search results, thread views) hote hain. Replica lag ka matlab hai master par likha data replica tak pahunchne me 1 second tak lag sakta hai. Email dekhne ke liye ye acceptable hai — user ko 1 second purana inbox dikh jaye to koi problem nahi. Lekin account creation ya quota check jaise kaam master se hote hain, kyunki wahan stale data se duplicate account ya galat quota decision ho sakta hai. Read/write split ka yehi funda hai.
Q29CAP theorem me Availability >> Consistency ka email system me kya matlab hai?
CAP theorem kehta hai network partition ke time availability aur consistency me se ek chunna padta hai. Email system availability chunta hai — user ka inbox hamesha khulna chahiye, chahe koi replica thoda peeche ho. Lekin ek jagah high consistency zaroori hai: same user account ke liye — duplicate email same email_id ke saath kabhi nahi aana chahiye, aur do users ka same email address kabhi nahi hona chahiye. Isiliye account creation aur mailbox insert strongly consistent hain, baaki reads eventually consistent chalenge.
Q30Draft auto-save har 30 second me kyun hota hai?
Kyunki user email likhte waqt browser crash ho sakta hai, light ja sakti hai, ya tab galti se band ho sakta hai. Har 30 second me draft save hota rahe to zyada se zyada 30 second ka likha hua khoyega. Har keystroke par save karna server par bahut load dalega, aur kabhi save na karna user ka kaam kho dega — 30 second dono ke beech ka balance hai. Draft alag Drafts DB me save hota hai, Outbox se alag, kyunki draft abhi bheja nahi gaya hai.
Q31Optimistic UI kya hai? Sent folder me email turant kaise dikhta hai?
Optimistic UI ka matlab hai: user ko result turant dikha do, backend ka kaam peeche se hota rahe. Jab user Send dabata hai to API 200 OK {status: 'SENDING'} return karta hai aur UI email ko turant Sent folder me dikha deta hai — jabki asli delivery (SMTP, validation) abhi baaki hai aur async hoti hai. Agar delivery fail ho jaye to baad me bounce notification se bataya jata hai. Isse user ko lagta hai system instant hai, jabki heavy kaam background me chal raha hai.
Q32Notification me VIP sender aur normal sender me kya farq hai?
Notification Svc user ki settings aur email ki importance dekh kar decide karta hai. Agar sender VIP hai (jaise boss) ya subject me important keywords hain to turant push notification bhejta hai — FCM ke through mobile par. Warna silent notification — sirf app icon par badge count badhta hai, user ko disturb nahi karta. Har email par push aaye to user notifications band kar dega — isiliye importance-based notification user experience ke liye zaroori hai.
Q33Email forwarding rule kaise implement hoti hai?
User ek rule banata hai — jaise 'boss@company.com se aane wale emails personal@gmail.com par forward karo'. Jab aisa email aata hai to Notification Svc internal send trigger karta hai: POST /v1/emails/send — lekin headers modify karke, X-Forwarded-From header add karke taaki pata chale ye forwarded email hai, original nahi. Forward hua email phir normal send flow se jata hai — Outbox, Kafka, SMTP — bilkul waise hi jaise user ne khud bheja ho.
Q34SMTP connection pooling se kya fayda hai? TLS handshake kitna mehenga hai?
Har naye SMTP connection par TLS handshake karna padta hai, jo lagbhag 200ms leta hai. Agar har email ke liye naya connection banao to ye 200ms har baar waste hota hai. Connection pooling me frequently contacted domains (jaise gmail.com → outlook.com) ke saath persistent connection banaye rakhte hain aur reuse karte hain — handshake ek baar, phir kai emails usi connection par. Pool size 100 per SMTP worker hoti hai. Peak time par lakhs of emails me ye saving bahut badi ho jati hai.
Q35Bulk aur newsletter emails LOW priority kyun hote hain?
Kyunki bulk emails (100+ recipients) aur newsletters time-sensitive nahi hote — inhe 30 minute me deliver hona acceptable hai. Agar inhe HIGH priority de di to ye workers ko kha jayenge aur important emails (same domain, known contacts) slow ho jayenge. Isiliye LOW priority: 20 workers, 100 emails/min per domain rate limit, aur off-peak hours me process karte hain. Ye abuse prevention bhi hai — spammer bulk bhej kar system ko overload nahi kar sakta.
Q361.8B users par sabse bada bottleneck kya hoga aur use kaise handle karoge?
Sabse bada bottleneck storage aur write throughput hai — billions of emails roz, petabytes data. Ise 4 cheezon se handle karte hain: (1) user_id se sharding — data N shards me bat jata hai, har shard millions of users handle karta hai; (2) attachments S3 me — database me sirf URL, to DB halka rehta hai; (3) Kafka event-driven flow — services decoupled, consumers badha kar scale karo; (4) read replicas — 1 master + 10 replicas, reads distribute ho jate hain. Inke bina single database ya single server pehle din hi down ho jayega.
