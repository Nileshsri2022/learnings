Ch 2 — How Generative AI Works

Ye chapter foundation models ke andar jhaankta hai — tokens, embeddings, attention, pre-training aur generation loop — aur phir chaar tareeke sikhata hai model se kaam nikalne ke: prompting, RAG, fine-tuning aur agents. End me ek unified workflow me sabko jod diya jata hai.

Foundation models — engines

Foundation models bade neural networks hain jo bahut saare data par train hote hain. Tokens text ki units hain — model text ko tokens me tod kar padhta hai. Context window limit karta hai ki model ek baar me kitna dekh sakta hai. Embeddings words ko numerical vectors me badal dete hain jisme meaning chhupa hota hai — similar words ke vectors paas-paas hote hain.

Kaam karne ka tareeka: pre-training (bahut saara text padh kar language seekhna), phir embedding aur attention mechanisms (kaunsa word kis par dhyaan de), aur phir generation loop — model ek token at a time predict karta hai aur next token sample karta hai.

Fig 2.1 — Tokens → Embeddings → Attention → Generation loop. Diagram kitaab ke description se banaya gaya hai.

Prompting ki seedhi — zero-shot se self-consistency tak

Prompt engineering ek ladder ki tarah sikhayi gayi hai:

  • Zero-shot: bina example ke seedha sawaal — "Is email ka summary likho."
  • One-shot: ek example dekar batana kya chahiye.
  • Few-shot: 2–3 examples dekar pattern samjhana.
  • Chain-of-thought: "step by step socho" — model ko reasoning dikhane ko kehna.
  • Role-based: model ko ek role dena — "tum ek senior support agent ho."
  • Self-consistency: kai baar jawab nikal kar sabse common wala chunna.

Salah ye hai: clear raho, context do, role set karo, aur iterate karo — pehla prompt aakhri nahi hota.

RAG — apne data se jawab

RAG ka matlab hai: model ko jawab dene se pehle organisation ke apne documents se relevant hissa nikaal kar prompt me daal do, taaki model apni yaad-dasht par bharosa na kare. Advanced patterns: ek "hypothetical" jawab ko search query ki tarah use karna, documents ko chhote chunks me todna, context compress karna, aur model ko khud decide karne dena ki kab retrieve karna hai.

Ch 4 — Data Readiness and Model Selections

Ye chapter kehta hai — data hi AI ka fuel hai. Model kitna bhi achha ho, agar data ready nahi hai to kuch nahi hoga. Data ko chaar qualities se judge karo: volume, variety, velocity aur veracity — aur sabse mushkil hissa hai unstructured data (documents, audio, images).

Data ki chaar qualities — 4 Vs

  • Volume: kitna data hai — kam data se model theek se seekh nahi pata
  • Variety: kitne tarah ka data hai — text, images, audio, tables
  • Velocity: data kitni tez aa raha hai aur badal raha hai
  • Veracity: data kitna sahi aur bharosemand hai

Data readiness journey — neev banana

Data governance, lineage (data kahan se aaya), catalogue (data kahan pada hai) aur data mesh ki zaroorat batayi gayi hai. Unstructured data — documents, audio, images — readiness ka sabse mushkil hissa hai, kyunki use saaf karna aur structure dena padta hai.

Fig 4.1 — Data flywheel. Diagram kitaab ke description se banaya gaya hai.

Data prepare karna — fuel taiyaar karo

Practical steps: cleaning (gandagi hatao), de-duplication (duplicate hatao), tagging aur labelling (data ko label do). Iske liye SageMaker Ground Truth aur Ground Truth Plus use hote hain — human labellers se data label karwana.

SageMaker Lakehouse — Bronze, Silver, Gold

SageMaker Lakehouse ek hi jagah hai jahan S3, Redshift aur doosre stores milte hain. Data teen tiers me rakha jata hai:

TierKya hota haiExample
BronzeRaw data — jaise aaya, waise pada haiLogs, raw documents, feeds

GoldCurated data — business use ke liye taiyaarAggregated, labelled, ready-to-use datasets

Model selection — cost, latency, quality ka trade-off

Model chunna ek trade-off hai: chhote models tez aur saste hote hain, bade models reasoning me behtar hote hain. Bedrock ka "model arena" (evaluation feature) models ko ek hi prompt par side by side compare kar sakta hai — same sawaal, alag models, dekh lo kaun behtar jawab deta hai.

FAQs — 36 Sawal-Jawab

Interview aur revision ke liye — har chapter se sawal. Jawab kitaab ke content se hain, Hinglish me.

01Generative AI aur normal machine learning me kya farq hai?
Machine learning data se patterns seekh kar predict karta hai — jaise spam hai ya nahi. Generative AI naya content banata hai — text, images, audio, video ya code. Kitaab kehti hai ye predictive AI se generative AI ki taraf shift hai, aur LLMs iske central example hain.
02Transformer me encoder aur decoder ka kaam kya hai?
Encoder poora input dono directions me padh kar samajhta hai — understanding aur classification ke liye best hai. Decoder left-to-right generate karta hai — text generation ke liye best hai. Dono ko jodne se translation aur summarization jaise kaam hote hain.
03McKinsey survey ke numbers kya kehte hain?
McKinsey State of AI 2025 survey ke hisaab se 88% organisations kam se kam ek function me AI use kar rahi hain aur 79% generative AI use kar rahi hain. Gartner ka forecast hai 2025 me GenAI spending $644 billion. WEF kehta hai 2030 tak 39% key skills badal jayengi.
04Tokens aur context window kya hote hain?
Tokens text ki units hain — model text ko tokens me tod kar padhta hai. Context window limit karta hai ki model ek baar me kitna dekh sakta hai. Window se zyada text ho to purana context kat jata hai, isliye lambe documents ko chunks me todna padta hai.
05Embeddings ka matlab kya hai?
Embeddings words ko numerical vectors me badal dete hain jisme meaning chhupa hota hai. Similar words ke vectors paas-paas hote hain. Isi se search aur similarity kaam karti hai — query ka vector documents ke vectors se compare hota hai.
06Zero-shot, few-shot aur chain-of-thought me kya farq hai?
Zero-shot: bina example ke seedha sawaal. One-shot: ek example. Few-shot: 2–3 examples se pattern samjhana. Chain-of-thought: model ko 'step by step socho' kehna taaki reasoning dikhe. Role-based: model ko role dena. Self-consistency: kai baar jawab nikal kar common wala chunna.
07RAG kya hai aur kab use karna chahiye?
RAG me model ko jawab dene se pehle organisation ke apne documents se relevant hissa nikaal kar prompt me daal diya jata hai, taaki model apni yaad-dasht par bharosa na kare. Jab data fresh ho, private ho ya model ke training data me na ho — tab RAG best hai. Advanced patterns: hypothetical answer ko query banana, chhote chunks, context compression.
08Fine-tuning kab karna chahiye aur kab nahi?
Fine-tuning tab jab style, vocabulary, structure ya edge cases matter karte hain — jaise company ke tone me likhna. Ye sabse mehenga aur slowest setup hai, lekin sabse precise hai. Agar sirf apna data use karna hai to pehle RAG try karo — Table 2.1 kehta hai RAG sabse practical hai.
09Bedrock kya hai?
Bedrock AWS ki serverless model service hai — alag-alag providers (Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama 2, Mistral, Cohere, Titan, Nova, Stability AI, AI21 Labs aur doosre) ke models ek hi jagah se use karo, bina apna infrastructure manage kiye. Tokens ke hisaab se charge hota hai.
10Nova family me kaunse models hain?
Nova Amazon ki apni family hai: Nova Pro, Nova Lite, Nova Reel (video), Nova Canvas (images), Nova Sonic (speech) — aur Nova Lite 2 aur Nova Pro 2 bhi naam se aate hain.
11Bedrock AgentCore kya karta hai?
AgentCore agents ko deploy karne ka runtime hai. Iske saath aate hain: observability, identity service, memory service, browser tool, code interpreter, gateway, evaluator aur policy service. Evaluator prati region 1,000 configurations tak support karta hai.
12Guardrails kya hote hain?
Guardrails har request par lagne wale controls hain: content filters, topic controls, denied topics, word filters, sensitive-information filters aur automated reasoning (policy ke against formal checks). Input aur output dono par lagte hain.
13Data ke 4 Vs kaunse hain?
Volume (kitna data), Variety (kitne tarah ka), Velocity (kitni tez aa raha hai), Veracity (kitna sahi aur bharosemand hai). Data ko in chaar se judge karo — aur unstructured data (documents, audio, images) readiness ka sabse mushkil hissa hai.
14Data flywheel kya hai?
Zyada usage se zyada data milta hai, zyada data se model behtar hota hai, behtar model se aur usage badhta hai. Ek baar chakra ghoom gaya to har round me model aur data dono behtar hote hain.
15Bronze, Silver, Gold tiers ka matlab kya hai?
SageMaker Lakehouse me data teen tiers me rakha jata hai: Bronze — raw data jaise aaya waise; Silver — cleaned data, duplicates hataye hue; Gold — curated data, business use ke liye taiyaar.
16Golden dataset kya hota hai?
Known question-and-answer pairs ka set, jisse har baar model ko test kiya ja sake. Bina golden dataset ke tumhe pata hi nahi chalega ki model behtar hua ya kharab — har change ke baad isi se compare karo.
17Hotel booking assistant me kaunse 6 tools hain?
search_hotels (SQL LIKE search), get_room_details, check_availability (dates compare karke), book_room (booking insert + confirmation), get_booking_details (email se lookup), cancel_booking (delete). Database PostgreSQL 15 hai — 10 hotels × 5 rooms = 50 rooms.
18Agent ko tools kis sequence me call karne chahiye?
System prompt agent ko batata hai: helpful hotel assistant bano aur tools ko sequence me call karo — pehle search, phir details, phir availability, phir booking. Agent khud decide karta hai kaunsa tool kab chahiye, aur AgentCore memory se baat-cheet yaad rakhta hai.
19Blue/green, canary, rolling aur shadow deployment me kya farq hai?
Blue/green: naya environment banao, phir switch karo. Canary: traffic ka chhota hissa pehle naye version par. Rolling: thoda-thoda update karo. Shadow: naye version ko real traffic ki copy do bina users ko affect kiye. Kitaab kehti hai canary se shuru karo.
20Spot instances kitne saste hote hain?
Kitaab ka claim hai Spot instances on-demand se 70–90% saste hote hain. Savings Plans bhi use karo. Dhyaan rakhna — Spot instances kabhi bhi wapas liye ja sakte hain, isliye fault-tolerant workloads ke liye best hain.
21Production alarms ke thresholds kya hain?
P99 latency 10 seconds se zyada, time-to-first-token 3 seconds se zyada, throttled requests 5% se zyada, error rate 2% se zyada (teen lagataar 5-minute windows tak). Ye Ch 6 ke example thresholds hain — apne app ke hisaab se adjust karo.
22Rephrasing check 0.85 cosine similarity ka matlab kya hai?
Agar dobara likha gaya sawaal 0.85 se zyada cosine similarity score kare to use wahi intent maano. Aur 8 turns se zyada lambi conversations ko long flag karo. Ye AgentCore production guidance ka hissa hai.
23Tree of Thoughts kya hai?
Seedha ek line me sochne ke bajay multiple reasoning branches explore karo, har ek ko score karo, phir best wali chuno. Contract-review example: 500 contracts/month, 12 clauses, $240,000 liability cap, 94% accuracy, lagbhag 30 contracts human review me.
24Reflexion kaise kaam karta hai?
Agent apni galtiyon se seekhta hai aur lessons store karta hai. Example numbers: accuracy 82% se 91% (12 weeks) aur 94% (24 weeks); escalations 90 se 45 se 30; 127 lessons 6 mahine me, $0.009 per failure.
25Voice AI kitna sasta padta hai?
Nova Sonic $0.15 per call vs human agent $1.75–2 per call. 300,000 calls par monthly cost $525,000 se $245,000 ho jati hai — $280,000 monthly saving, $3.36 million per year. Ye kitaab ke representative numbers hain, kisi ek company ke nahi.
26Memory ki cost kitni hoti hai?
15 turns lagbhag 3,000 tokens — $0.135 per conversation. Hierarchical memory $0.041 — lagbhag 70% sasti. Lambi conversations me memory cost ko bhi budget me rakho.
27Use case chunne ka framework kya hai?
Teen sawaal: Value (revenue, cost, risk — kitna fayda?), Feasibility (data hai? model kar sakta hai? integration hoga?), Readiness (log taiyaar hain? governance hai?). Teeno ka jawab haan ho to use case aage badhao.
28Telecom assistant example ke numbers kya the?
75% deflection, 97% guardrail pass rate, latency 3.8s se 2.6s, $2.3 million per year saved, retrieval accuracy 84% se 93%, escalation 25% down, cost per ticket 22% down. Ye Ch 8 ka sabse detailed example hai.
29Shadow AI kya hai aur isse kaise handle karein?
Shadow AI matlab employees ka public AI tools ko bina approval ke use karna — company ka data bahar ke tools me daal dena. Handle karne ke steps: pehle discovery (kaun kya use kar raha hai), phir policy, phir approved alternatives do.
30Three lines of defence kya hain?
First line — Business (roz ke risks manage karna). Second line — Risk/Compliance (policies banana, monitor karna). Third line — Audit (independent check). Teeno alag teams hain — ek hi team sab kuch nahi karti.
31EU AI Act aur US rules me kya farq hai?
EU AI Act risk-based tiers use karta hai — high-risk systems par sabse sakht duties. US me state-level rules hain, jaise Colorado ka AI Act. Finance aur healthcare ke alag sector rules bhi hain.
32Bank wale example me model ko kyun block kiya gaya?
Ek bank ne model ko bina licence ke personalised financial advice dene se block kar diya — kyunki advice dena regulated kaam hai aur model ke paas licence nahi tha. Ye governance ka practical example hai: capability hona kaafi nahi, permission bhi chahiye.
33AI fluency ke chaar levels kaunse hain?
Level 1 Awareness (pata hai kya hai), Level 2 Usage (roz use karta hai), Level 3 Building (khud banata hai), Level 4 Leading (doosron ko lead karta hai). Har level ka alag training path hai.
34Agentic commerce kitna bada hoga?
Kitaab ka estimate hai 2030 tak US B2C commerce ka $900 billion se $1 trillion — agents logon ke behalf par khareedari karein. Saath me PwC estimate hai AI economic value $15.7 trillion by 2030.

Kitaab ke andar print hua ISBN check nahi ho saka — is O'Reilly edition me koi copyright page nahi hai. Do numbers dikhte hain: URL ka number 9781806104451 aur Preface ke colour-images link me 9781806673957.
