Ch 1 — Introduction and AI System Overview

AI applications ab sirf demo me nahi, production me bann rahe hain — aur mushkil sawaal badal gaya hai: "kya model ye kar sakta hai?" se "kya hum aisi kai applications ko reliably, baar-baar aur safely ship aur run kar sakte hain?" Ye chapter terms define karta hai aur wo lifecycle batata hai jiske aas-paas baaki poori book ka platform banta hai.

1.1 · Large-scale AI kya hai

  • Foundation models wo models hain jinki training aur serving par parameters aur activations ko ghumane aur compute karne ka kharcha haavi rehta hai — kai accelerators ke paar. Isiliye system efficiency pehle darje ka design variable ban jaati hai.
  • Mechanical sympathy: software is tarah design karna ki tumhe hardware ka working mental model ho — uski memory hierarchy, parallelism, interconnects — taaki code machine ke khilaaf nahi, uske saath kaam kare.

1.2 · Teen resources: compute, memory, communication

Har AI workload in teen me se kisi ek se bandha hota hai — jo uske particular shape ke liye sabse kam ho. Non-bottleneck ko fix karne se kuch nahi hota.

1.3 · Metrics — latency, throughput, utilisation, efficiency

  • Latency: ek unit kaam khatm karne ka time. Throughput: per unit time kitne units. Utilisation: resource ka kitna hissa busy hai. Efficiency: useful kaam vs energy, cost ya time.
  • Ye chaaro ek dusre ki jagah nahi le sakte — ek ko sudharna dusre ko bigaad sakta hai.
  • Goodput — book ka north-star metric: useful, correct, completed kaam per unit cost/time/energy. Raw FLOPS ya raw tokens/sec nahi. Aisa system jo bahut kaam karta hai lekin wo kaam baad me discard ho jaata hai ya unusable output deta hai — uska throughput high hai lekin goodput low hai. Goodput performance ko business aur energy cost se jodta hai.

1.4 · Modern accelerator architectures (intro)

GPU kai parallel streaming multiprocessors, high-bandwidth memory, matrix math ke liye Tensor Cores, aur multi-GPU/multinode kaam ke liye fast interconnects (NVLink/NVSwitch, InfiniBand) ke aas-paas bane hote hain.

1.5 · DeepSeek R1 case study — chapter ka central example

  • Context: DeepSeek ne ek frontier-class reasoning model train aur serve kiya jabki uske paas NVIDIA H800 GPUs the — H100 ka export-limited variant — sabse powerful hardware nahi.
  • Kya kiya: hardware limit ko maan lene ke bajaye, unhone poora system stack dobara engineer kiya — communication, memory handling, kernel design — taaki har GPU se zyada useful kaam nikle.
  • Numbers: H800 ke paas H100 ke high-speed interconnect bandwidth ka lagbhag 50% hai. DeepSeek ne custom communication aur overlapping se iski bharpai ki. Chapter isse ek hi speedup number ke roop me nahi, ek demonstration ke roop me present karta hai — systems kaam hardware gap ka bada hissa band kar sakta hai.
  • Lesson: bottleneck raw compute nahi tha — communication aur scheduling tha.
  • Scale ka framing: chapter 100-trillion-parameter models ki hypothetical future par baat karta hai jo tens of thousands GPUs par train honge — us scale par 1% efficiency gain bhi bahut bada paisa aur energy ke barabar hai.

Ch 2 — AI System Hardware Overview

Is chapter me modern AI accelerator system ki anatomy hai — Grace Blackwell superchip se lekar NVL72 rack tak. Hardware samajhna hi performance engineering ki pehli shart hai, kyunki har tuning decision inhi numbers ke upar khadi hoti hai.

2.1 · Grace Blackwell Superchip (GB200 / GB300)

  • Ek package me Grace ARM CPU + do Blackwell GPUs, jo NVLink-C2C par coherent memory space share karte hain.
  • Point ye hai: CPU aur GPU ko PCIe se jude do alag machines ki tarah nahi, ek hi memory complex ki tarah treat karo.
  • NVLink-C2C: superchip ke andar CPU–GPU link, 900 GB/s bidirectional. GPU Grace memory ko seedha padh sakta hai — HBM se kam bandwidth, lekin PCIe se bahut zyada.
  • Grace CPU: 480 GB LPDDR5X, lagbhag 500 GB/s.

2.2 · Blackwell GPU

  • Ek package me do dies, total lagbhag 208 billion transistors (text ke breakdown me lagbhag 104 billion per die).
  • Fifth-generation Tensor Cores, second-generation Transformer Engine, aur FP8 aur FP4 ka native support.
  • HBM3e: GPU package par stacked high-bandwidth memory. B200 full text me 192 GB diya gaya hai (summary table me 180 GB — alag configurations/variants ko reflect karta hai), lagbhag 8 TB/s par.
  • GB200 NVL72 figure: text me 8 TB/s per GPU, summary table me 20.7 TB/s — alag configuration ke liye. Dono figures yahan isiliye diye gaye hain kyunki book me dono jagah alag values hain; ek ko chup-chap chunna galat hota. Asli point: HBM bandwidth terabytes/sec me measure hoti hai aur wahi main memory bottleneck hai.
Numbers par note: jahan book me ek hi hardware ke liye alag jagah alag figures hain (jaise B200 memory 192 GB vs 180 GB, HBM bandwidth 8 TB/s vs 20.7 TB/s), is SPA me dono diye gaye hain — kabhi ek ko chupke se nahi chuna gaya. Apne target hardware par dobara measure karo.

2.3 · NVLink 5 aur NVSwitch

  • NVLink 5: per GPU 1.8 TB/s bidirectional bandwidth (900 GB/s har disha me).
  • NVSwitch: kai GPUs ko usi speed par ek dusre se baat karne deta hai.
  • Ek NVL72 rack me 72 GPUs ek hi NVLink domain me hote hain, lagbhag 130 TB/s aggregate intra-rack bandwidth ke saath. Dusre level ke switches se ek NVLink domain ko 576 GPUs tak badhaya ja sakta hai.

2.4 · GPU memory hierarchy

2.5 · MIG (Multi-Instance GPU)

Ek physical GPU ko 7 isolated instances tak baanta ja sakta hai — har ek ki apni memory, compute aur caches. Chhote jobs ya isolation ke liye useful hai.

2.6 · Power aur thermal management

  • High-end GPUs power-limited hote hain, sirf compute-limited nahi. Sustained clocks cooling par depend karte hain.
  • NVL72 scale par liquid cooling norm hai, aur power caps ek tuning tool hain, sirf safety limit nahi.

2.7 · Future — Rubin architecture (2026, book ki timeline me)

Blackwell ke baad ki architecture — zyada compute, zyada memory bandwidth, aur tighter CPU–GPU integration. Ye direction of travel hai, abhi khareedne wala product nahi.

2.8 · Advanced architectures — GB200, GB300, DGX, NVL72, Vera Rubin

Book in sabko ek hi family ke roop me dekhti hai: Grace + Blackwell ko jodne wale superchip designs, DGX systems, NVL72 rack, aur Vera Rubin (Rubin generation ka platform). Har ek me wahi pattern — CPU–GPU ko ek memory complex banana, NVLink se jodna, aur rack-scale par bandwidth badhana.

2.9 · NVIDIA vs AMD — book ki comparison

Book ki comparison chhoti aur qualitative hai: AMD ki MI series raw compute par ek credible alternative hai, lekin software stack alag hai (ROCm vs CUDA). Choice ko ecosystem aur library maturity ke mamle me bhi dekha jaata hai, sirf hardware ke mamle me nahi.

Ch 3 — OS, Docker, and Kubernetes Tuning for GPU-Based Environments

GPU fast hai, lekin OS, driver aur container galat tune ho to GPU khaali baitha rehta hai. Ye chapter host ko GPU workload ke hisaab se tune karna sikhata hai — kernel settings, NUMA, huge pages, Docker, Kubernetes aur telemetry tak.

3.1 · NVIDIA driver aur CUDA toolkit

  • Persistence mode: driver ko loaded rakho taaki har job par GPU re-initialisation ka delay na ho — nvidia-smi -pm 1 se enable hota hai.
  • Driver/CUDA pairing galat ho to sabse common pitfall hai — version hamesha match karo.
bash — driver / CUDA / MIG check
nvidia-smi
nvcc --version
nvidia-smi -pm 1                 # persistence mode on
nvidia-smi -i 0 -mig 1           # MIG enable (GPU 0)
nvidia-smi -L                    # MIG devices list karo
nvidia-smi mig -cgi 1g.5gb      # MIG profile set karo
nvidia-smi -q -d POWER           # power query

3.2 · NUMA awareness, CPU affinity, IRQ tuning

  • Multi-socket servers me memory aur devices ek particular NUMA node se attached hote hain. Galat node par chalne wala process har memory ya DMA access par cross-NUMA penalty deta hai.
  • Pinning se bacho: numactl / taskset se process ko sahi node par pin karo, aur memory ko local node se bind karo (numactl --membind).
  • IRQ affinity: network aur GPU interrupts ko usi NUMA node ke cores par bind karo jis node par device hai, taaki interrupt handling nodes ke paar na jaaye.

3.3 · Memory management — huge pages aur locking

  • Transparent Huge Pages (THP): bade allocations ke liye 4 KB ki jagah 2 MB pages — TLB misses aur kernel overhead kam hota hai. Book always ya madvise mode recommend karti hai; verify karo /sys/kernel/mm/transparent_hugepage/enabled se.
  • Pinned (page-locked) memory: host memory jise GPU seedha DMA se padh sakta hai. Iske liye high locked-memory limit chahiye (ulimit -l unlimited ya high value); bina iske data loaders slow pageable copies par gir jaate hain.
bash — CPU governor aur huge pages
cpupower frequency-set -g performance
cat /sys/kernel/mm/transparent_hugepage/enabled
ulimit -l unlimited   # locked memory limit badhao

Ch 4 — Tuning Distributed Networking Communication

Distributed training me GPU compute se zyada baar network hi bottleneck hota hai. Ye chapter NCCL, topology, congestion, SHARP aur InfiniBand/RoCE ko cover karta hai — wo sab kuch jo nodes ke beech data ko tez rakhta hai.

4.1 · NCCL — NVIDIA Collective Communications Library

  • NCCL collective operations (all-reduce, all-gather, broadcast, reduce-scatter) ke liye hai — GPUs aur nodes ke paar.
  • Ye topology ke hisaab se ring, tree ya dusre algorithms chunti hai. Distributed-training communication ka main lever yahi hai.
  • PAT (Parallel Aggregated Tree): NVLink Switch topologies ke liye NCCL ka algorithm — single ring ke bajaye parallel me aggregate karta hai, NVL72 par synchronisation time kam karta hai.

4.2 · SHARP / CollNet — in-network reduction

  • All-reduce ka hissa InfiniBand switches par offload ho jaata hai — switch khud reduction karta hai, GPU ke bajaye.
  • Chahiye: SHARP plugin aur NCCL_COLLNET_ENABLE=1. Fayda sirf bade reductions par, SHARP-capable fabric par milta hai.

4.3 · Congestion aur incast

Jab kai senders ek saath ek receiver se baat karte hain, queues bhar jaati hain aur throughput collapse ho jaata hai. Fix sirf zyada bandwidth nahi — topology + scheduling + congestion control ka combination hai.

4.4 · GPUDirect RDMA aur hierarchical collectives

  • GPUDirect RDMA: GPU seedha NIC se baat karta hai — host memory aur kernel networking stack bypass — low latency, high throughput.
  • Hierarchical collectives: pehle node/NVLink domain ke andar reduction karo, phir nodes ke paar — taaki mehenga inter-node link bahut kam data carry kare.

Ch 5 — GPU-Based Storage I/O Optimizations

Agar data GPU tak utni tez nahi pahunch raha jitni tez GPU use kha raha hai, to GPU idle baitha hai. Cluster scale par ye aksar sabse sasta bada win hota hai — storage I/O fix karo, GPU utilisation khud upar aa jaayega.

5.1 · Storage I/O bottleneck

Data ko padhna, decompress karna aur GPU tak move karna — agar ye GPU ke consume karne se slow hai, to GPU idle hai. Cluster scale par ye sabse cheap large win hota hai.

5.2 · GPUDirect Storage (GDS)

  • GPU seedha NVMe ya network storage se padhta hai — CPU bounce buffer bypass — cuFile API ke through.
  • Bade datasets ke liye CPU load aur latency dono kam hoti hai.

5.3 · NVIDIA Magnum IO

6.2 · Warp — 32 threads, lockstep

  • Warp = 32 threads jo ek warp scheduler par lockstep me execute hote hain.

Ch 7 — Profiling and Tuning GPU Memory Access Patterns

Zyaadatar kernels compute se nahi, memory access pattern se slow hote hain. Ye chapter memory hierarchy ko detail me kholta hai — coalescing, bank conflicts, AoS vs SoA, unified memory, prefetching — aur Nsight Compute se measure karna sikhata hai.

7.1 · Memory hierarchy, detail me

  • Registers: sabse tez, per-thread. Shared memory / L1: per-SM — shared manually managed hoti hai, L1 automatic. L2: saare SMs ke beech shared, bahut bada. HBM: main device memory. Host memory: sabse slow, PCIe/NVLink se pahunchta hai.

7.2 · Coalescing — sabse bada lever

Jab warp ke 32 threads consecutive addresses access karte hain, hardware unhe ek ya kuch transactions me serve kar deta hai. Strided ya scattered access utne hi data ke liye kai transactions mangta hai — bandwidth waste hoti hai.

7.3 · Bank conflicts

Shared memory 32 banks me banti hai. Agar warp ke kai threads ek hi time par ek hi bank ko alag addresses ke saath access karein, to accesses serialise ho jaate hain. Padding ya layout reorder karne se conflict khatm hota hai.

7.4 · AoS vs SoA — data layout

  • AoS (array-of-structures) vs SoA (structure-of-arrays). GPU kernels jo ek field ko kai elements ke paar process karte hain, unke liye SoA aam taur par behtar coalesce karta hai.

7.5 · Unified / managed memory

  • Ek hi pointer jo system host aur device ke beech on demand migrate karta hai. Code simple ho jaata hai, lekin agar kernel aisa data chhu le jo abhi host par hai, to page-fault stall hota hai.
  • Explicit prefetch (cudaMemPrefetchAsync) se ye stall chhupaya jaata hai.

7.6 · Cache utilisation

L2 shared resource hai. Jo kernel aisa data stream karta hai jise wo dobara kabhi use nahi karega, wo dusre kernels ke liye cache pollute karta hai. L2 me prefetching (cp.async.bulk.prefetch) aur kya L2 me rakhna hai — ye explicit tuning choices hain.

7.7 · Code — shared-memory tile, vectorised loads, prefetch

  • Tile ko shared memory me load karo, __syncthreads() se synchronise karo, phir shared memory se compute karo — bank conflicts se bachne ke liye padding ke saath.
  • float4 vectorised loads (alignment checks ke saath), aur cudaMemPrefetchAsync / cp.async se agla tile pehle se stage karo jab current tile compute ho raha ho.
  • Nsight Compute profiling: kernel par ncu chalao, global load/store efficiency, L1/L2 hit rates aur DRAM throughput padho, aur "Issues" section se decide karo ki kernel memory-bound hai ya nahi.
cuda — shared-memory tile with padding (reconstructed)
__global__ void tiledKernel(const float* in, float* out, int n) {
    __shared__ float tile[32][32 + 1];  // +1 padding: bank conflict se bacho
    int x = blockIdx.x * 32 + threadIdx.x;
    int y = blockIdx.y * 32 + threadIdx.y;
    tile[threadIdx.y][threadIdx.x] = in[y * n + x];
    __syncthreads();   // sab threads tile load hone ka intezar karein
    // compute shared memory se — global dobara touch nahi
    out[y * n + x] = tile[threadIdx.y][threadIdx.x] * 2.0f;
}

Ch 8 — Occupancy Tuning, Warp Efficiency, and Instruction-Level Parallelism

Chapter 6 ne occupancy define ki thi — ye chapter uska trade-off kholta hai. Central point: maximum occupancy hamesha fastest nahi hoti. High ILP wala kernel 50% occupancy par 100% se tez ho sakta hai.

8.1 · Occupancy, detail me

  • Occupancy decide hoti hai: threads per block, registers per thread, shared memory per block — SM ki limits (max warps, max registers, max shared memory) ke against.
  • Occupancy Calculator trade-off curve dikhata hai.

8.2 · Register pressure aur spilling

Agar kernel per thread bahut zyada registers use karta hai, to compiler values ko local memory me spill kar deta hai — jo global memory me rehti hai, aur slow hoti hai. Registers kam karo to occupancy badhti hai, lekin ILP kam ho sakta hai.

8.3 · Warp efficiency aur divergence

  • Warp efficiency: warp ke threads ka average kitna hissa active hai. Divergence ise kam karti hai — 16/16 split wala warp uniform warp se double kaam karta hai.
  • Branch ko hatakar (predication, warp intrinsics) efficiency wapas milti hai.

8.4 · Instruction-Level Parallelism (ILP)

Ek thread ke andar independent instructions jo hardware parallel me issue kar sakta hai — bina zyada warps ke latency chhupa deti hain. Loop unrolling aur independent loads ILP badhane ke main tareeke hain.

8.5 · Occupancy–ILP trade-off — chapter ka central point

Maximum occupancy hamesha fastest nahi hai. High ILP wala kernel 50% occupancy par 100% se tez ho sakta hai, kyunki har thread per cycle zyada useful kaam karta hai. Optimum aam taur par beech me hota hai, aur use measure karna padta hai.

8.6 · Code — launch bounds, warp intrinsics, unrolling

  • __launch_bounds__(maxThreadsPerBlock, minBlocksPerMultiprocessor) se compiler ko occupancy target bataya jaata hai, taaki wo registers us hisaab se limit kare.
  • Divergent-branch version vs branchless version: warp intrinsics (__shfl_sync, __ballot_sync) se shared memory ke bina reduction compute karo.
  • Loop-unrolled version: kai independent loads pehle issue karo, phir use karo — ILP badhta hai. Nsight Compute se achieved occupancy aur IPC pehle aur baad me check karo.
cuda — launch bounds + warp shuffle reduction (reconstructed)
// compiler ko occupancy target batao — registers us hisaab se limit honge
__global__ void __launch_bounds__(256, 4)
myKernel(const float* in, float* out, int n) {
    int i = blockIdx.x * blockDim.x + threadIdx.x;
    float v = (i < n) ? in[i] : 0.0f;
    // warp shuffle reduction — shared memory ke bina
    for (int off = 16; off > 0; off >>= 1)
        v += __shfl_down_sync(0xffffffff, v, off);
    if ((threadIdx.x & 31) == 0) out[blockIdx.x] = v;
}
// loop unrolling: kai independent loads ek saath issue karo
// #pragma unroll 4 — ILP badhta hai, latency chhupta hai

Ch 9 — Increasing CUDA Kernel Efficiency and Arithmetic Intensity

Roofline ne bataya: kernel ya to memory-bound hai ya compute-bound. Ye chapter arithmetic intensity badhana sikhata hai — fusion, Tensor Cores, mixed precision, FP8/FP4 aur sparsity se kernel ko memory-bound se compute-bound ki taraf le jaana.

9.1 · Arithmetic intensity

Arithmetic intensity = FLOPs per byte moved from memory. Low intensity wala kernel memory-bound hota hai. Intensity badhao — data ko registers/shared memory me reuse karke, ya ops fuse karke — aur kernel roofline ke compute-bound region ki taraf badhta hai.

9.2 · Kernel fusion

Kai chhote kernels (jaise elementwise ops ki sequence) ko ek me merge karo — intermediate results registers/shared memory me rehte hain, global memory me likhe-padhne nahi padte, aur launch overhead ek baar hi lagta hai.

9.3 · Tensor Cores, applied

  • Matrix operations ko GEMM (ya convolution) ke roop me FP16/BF16/TF32/FP8/FP4 me express karo — tile dimensions 8 ya 16 ke multiples me, data Tensor Core path ke liye aligned.
  • Libraries (cuBLAS, CUTLASS) ye kaam zyada cases me hand-written code se behtar karti hain.

9.4 · Mixed precision

  • Bulk math FP16/BF16 me, FP32 accumulation aur master copy of weights ke saath.
  • Book ke hisaab se mixed-precision training me lagbhag 1.5–3.5× speedup — model aur kernel mix par depend karta hai, matmul-heavy workloads par gain bada.
  • TF32: FP32 matmul ka fast mode — PyTorch me torch.set_float32_matmul_precision("high") se enable hota hai; numerically safe FP32 matmuls ke liye bada speedup.

9.5 · FP8 aur FP4

  • FP8 Tensor Cores compute-bound kernels par FP16/BF16 ka lagbhag 2× throughput dete hain, aur activation/weight bandwidth aadhi kar dete hain.
  • FP4 (NVFP4) FP8 ka throughput phir se double karta hai.
  • Training ke liye book hybrid FP8 recommend karti hai — forward activations/weights ke liye E4M3, gradients ke liye E5M2 — delayed scaling window 256–1024 ke saath, Transformer Engine ke through.
  • Inference ke liye pehle FP8 evaluate karo; NVFP4 tabhi adopt karo jab calibration ke baad quality acceptable dikhe.
  • Microscaling: poore tensor ke bajaye values ke chhote group ke liye alag scale factor — low precision me accuracy nahi girti. Blackwell ki FP4 microscaling hi wo mechanism hai jo FP4 ko inference ke liye usable banata hai.

9.6 · 2:4 structured sparsity

Weights ka 50% ek fixed pattern me zero karo (har 4 me se 2) — hardware is pattern ko exploit karke un layers ke GEMM throughput ko lagbhag double kar deta hai. Shart: model ko us pattern par prune/retrain karna padta hai.

10.1 · Intra-kernel pipelining

Ek kernel ke andar agle tile ka load current tile ke compute ke saath overlap karo, taaki SM kabhi memory ka wait na kare. Multi-stage shared-memory buffers aur asynchronous copies se implement hota hai.

10.2 · cp.async aur TMA

  • cp.async: global se shared memory tak asynchronous copy — issuing thread block nahi hota, compute chalta rehta hai jab copy in-flight hai.
  • TMA (Tensor Memory Accelerator): hardware unit jo poore tensors ko global se shared memory me asynchronously move karta hai — descriptors tensor layout describe karte hain. 10.4 · Cooperative thread block clusters

  • 8 thread blocks tak ka cluster (Blackwell par non-portable limit badhakar 16) — cooperate kar sakte hain, distributed shared memory se data share kar sakte hain, aur group ke roop me synchronise kar sakte hain.
  • Clusters data ko paas rakhte hain aur persistent-style designs me relaunch overhead kam karte hain.

10.5 · Persistent kernels

Kernel ek baar launch hota hai aur kai units of work par loop karta hai — apne threads reuse karta hai — har unit ke liye relaunch karne ke bajaye. Launch cost amortise hoti hai aur state registers/shared memory me rehti hai.

Jab ek loop me kai chhote kernels baar-baar launch hote hain, to CPU launch overhead hi bottleneck ban jaata hai — har launch tens of microseconds. CUDA Graphs is overhead ko ek CPU call me band kar dete hain.

12.1 · Dynamic scheduling

Run time par decide karo ki kaun sa kaam kahan chalega — actual data ya load ke hisaab se, pehle se fixed schedule ke bajaye. Irregular workloads ke liye madadgar hai jahan har item ka kaam alag hota hai.

12.2 · CUDA Graph — capture aur launch

  • Captured sequence — kernels, copies, events — jo ek unit ke roop me ek CPU call se launch hoti hai. Per-kernel launch overhead khatm ho jaata hai; khaas taur par valuable jab CPU bottleneck ho.
  • Graph capture: stream par operations record karo (cudaStreamBeginCapture / cudaStreamEndCapture), phir instantiate aur launch karo (cudaGraphLaunch). Captured pointers stable rehne chahiye — isiliye graphs ke saath stream-ordered allocations aur static memory pools use hote hain.
  • Conditional nodes: graph nodes jinki execution device par evaluate hone wali condition par depend karti hai — graph CPU par laute bina branch kar sakta hai.

12.3 · Device-initiated orchestration / dynamic parallelism

  • Ek kernel GPU se hi dusre kernels launch kar sakta hai — CPU round-trip ke bina. Useful jab agla kaam intermediate results par depend karta hai jo GPU par compute hue hon.
  • Work queues: persistent kernel queue se work items pull karta hai — CPU sirf work enqueue karta hai, GPU khud schedule karta hai.

Har GPU ke paas model ki poori copy hoti hai aur alag data shard process karta hai; gradients all-reduce hote hain. Simple aur fast — jab model ek GPU me fit ho jaaye.

13.5 · Activation checkpointing aur gradient accumulation

  • Activation checkpointing: forward pass me activations discard karo, backward pass me recompute karo — lagbhag 30% extra compute ke badle me badi memory saving, taaki bada batch ya model fit ho.
  • Gradient accumulation: optimiser step se pehle kai micro-batches ke gradients accumulate karo — bina us batch ki memory liye, bada effective batch size mil jaata hai.

13.6 · AMP (Automatic Mixed Precision)

torch.autocast bulk math FP16/BF16 me chalata hai, FP32 accumulation ke saath; gradient scaler FP16 underflow se bachata hai.

13.7 · Data loading optimise karna

Slowest 1% requests ki latency. Serving me tail average jitni hi important hai, kyunki kuch slow requests user experience aur SLO violations par haavi rehte hain.

16.4 · Batching aur concurrency

Bade batches throughput badhate hain lekin latency bhi badhate hain. Sahi batch size wohi hai jo latency SLO meet karte hue highest throughput de — aur use measurement se nikalna padta hai.

Ch 18 — Advanced Prefill-Decode and KV Cache Tuning

KV cache hi inference serving ki memory ka sabse bada hissa hai. Is chapter me chunked prefill, paged KV cache, radix-tree prefix cache, quantised KV aur offloading — har ek kaam ke liye ek tuning knob.

18.1 · Chunked prefill

Lambe prompt ki prefill ko chunks me baanto jo decode steps ke saath interleave hote hain — taaki ek lambi prefill decode ko block karke dusre requests ke liye latency spike na banaye.

18.2 · Paged KV cache

  • KV cache ko fixed-size blocks (pages) me store karo jo on demand allocate hote hain — virtual memory ki tarah. Fragmentation kam hoti hai, aur prefix share karne wale requests ke beech memory share ho sakti hai.
  • Block size tuning: chhote blocks waste kam karte hain lekin metadata overhead badhate hain; bade blocks efficient hote hain lekin chhoti sequences par zyada memory waste karte hain.

18.3 · Radix tree (prefix tree) aur prefix caching

Data structure jo cached prefixes ko unki token sequence se index karta hai — naya request jaldi se longest cached prefix dhoondh kar reuse kar sakta hai jo uske prompt se match karta hai.

18.4 · KV cache eviction

Cache bhar jaaye to decide karo kaun se blocks evict karein (least-recently-used, ya priority se) taaki sabse useful prefixes cached rahein.

18.5 · Quantised KV cache aur offloading

  • Quantised KV cache: KV values FP16 ke bajaye FP8 ya INT8 me store karo — memory aadhi, bade batches ya lambe contexts possible, chhoti quality cost jo measure karni chahiye.
  • KV cache offloading: kam use hone wale KV blocks CPU memory ya NVMe par move karo — bahut lambe context wala request bhi chal sake, is keemat par ki agar wo block dobara chahiye to fetch slow hoga.

Appendix — AI Systems Performance Checklist (175+ Items)

Ye appendix koi naya argument nahi — ek field checklist hai. 19 sections, 175+ practical reminders, book ke apne numbers ke saath jahan matter karte hain. Neeche har section ka substance hai, apne shabdon me — 175+ items ko verbatim copy karne ke bajaye unka saar.

A.1 · Performance tuning aur cost optimization mindset

  • Pehle mehenga fix karo (80/20) — agar 90% time kuch kernels ya ek communication phase me hai, to wahi fix karo, 1% wali cheez nahi. Jaise: training 40% data loading, 50% compute, 10% communication ho to pehle data loading fix karo, jahan use aadha karna possible hai.
  • Har change se pehle aur baad me profile karo — non-memory-limited job ko activation checkpointing slow kar sakta hai (compute ke badle memory trade karta hai).
  • Adaptive autotuning (RL/Bayesian) use karo jahan fayda ho; optimisation time budget karo — AMP/prefetch se 2× win sasta hai; custom kernel chhota increment de sakta hai; flagship training run par 5% gain bhi hafton ki tuning justify kar sakta hai.
  • Framework upgrades ke saath current raho — aksar free speedups hote hain. Vendors/community ke saath codesign karo. Cloud pricing levers use karo (spot/reserved, behtar price/performance wale purane GPUs).
  • Utilisation dashboards monitor karo; throughput-sensitive hyperparameters tune karo; document aur reuse karo; optimisation ko complexity ke saath balance karo — agar torch.compile target meet karta hai to custom kernel mat likho. Telemetry se bottlenecks predict karne ke liye ML use karo.

A.2 · Reproducibility aur documentation best practices

  • Configs, drivers, frameworks, OS settings aur benchmarks version-control me rakho (Git, tagged releases). Performance benchmarks ko CI/CD me add karo (MLPerf baseline) taaki regressions pakdi jaayein.
  • Poori pipeline ko end-to-end optimise karo, ek stage ko nahi. Monitoring/diagnostics automate karo (Prometheus/Grafana, alerts). Fault tolerance built-in rakho (distributed checkpointing, redundant hardware, dynamic rescheduling).
  • Aggressive compiler/profile-guided optimisation use karo aur dobara verify karo. Security/compliance ko performance sacrifice kiye bina integrate karo (encryption, zero-trust, HSMs, secure enclaves). Shared knowledge base rakho; modular, future-proof architectures design karo.

A.3 · System architecture aur hardware planning

  • Goodput per dollar/watt ke liye design karo, peak FLOPS ke liye nahi. Sahi accelerator chuno (naye GPUs, native FP8/FP4, tez interconnects).
  • Multi-GPU kaam ke liye NVLink/NVSwitch (NVL72) use karo, PCIe-only ke bajaye. CPU/GPU/memory balance karo — data loading/networking ke liye lagbhag 1 fast CPU core per GPU; per GPU hundreds of MB/s I/O.
  • Data local rakho (NVLink intranode/rack, InfiniBand inter-rack). Sabse slow link dhoondh kar fix karo. GPUs ko diminishing returns se aage scale mat karo — 2N par jaane se pehle N GPUs par 95% utilisation tak pahuncho. Cooling/power is tarah plan karo ki boost clocks sustain rahein.

A.4 · Unified CPU-GPU "superchip" architecture

  • Grace+Blackwell ko ek coherent memory complex treat karo (NVLink-C2C 900 GB/s; Grace 480 GB LPDDR5X). Hot weights/activations HBM me rakho, overflow/infrequent data Grace memory me.
  • GPU ki Grace memory tak seedhi access prefetch/pipelining ke saath use karo taaki latency chhup jaaye. 72-core Grace CPU preprocessing/orchestration ke liye use karo.
  • Trillion-parameter models ke liye plan karo — CPU memory model pool ka hissa, cudaMallocAsync, managed memory aur cudaMemPrefetchAsync ke saath. Superchip-optimised algorithms consider karo jaise SuperOffload (speculation-then-validation, heterogeneous optimiser, ARM-based CPU optimiser).

A.5 · Multi-GPU scaling aur interconnect optimizations

  • NVL72 ka non-blocking NVLink Switch domain use karo (kisi bhi GPU se kisi bhi GPU tak full NVLink 5 speed, 900 GB/s har disha, lagbhag 130 TB/s aggregate per rack) aur DCGM NVLink counters/Nsight Systems se verify karo, assumption se nahi.
  • Jobs ko ek NVLink domain me colocate karo. NVSwitch ke liye NCCL PAT use karo. Fine-grained parallelism consider karo jo pehle feasible nahi tha (layer-wise/tensor parallelism 1.8 TB/s bidirectional per GPU par).
  • Saturation monitor karo aur all-to-all exchanges ko stagger karo agar zaroorat ho. 576-GPU NVLink domains ke liye hierarchical collectives plan karo (pehle local, phir inter-rack).

A.6 · Operating system aur driver optimizations

  • Lean HPC Linux chalao, performance governor, unnecessary services band karo. Swap disable karo ya critical buffers lock karo. Bade contiguous blocks preallocate karo.
  • OMP_NUM_THREADS/MKL_NUM_THREADS tune karo. NUMA-aware raho (numactl, taskset, --membind). NIC/GPU interrupts ko local NUMA node par bind karo.
  • THP enable karo (always/madvise, /sys/kernel/mm/transparent_hugepage/enabled se check karo). ulimit -l unlimited set karo. Drivers/CUDA nodes ke paar consistent rakho, persistence enable karo (nvidia-smi -pm 1, khaas taur par MIG ke saath).
  • OS housekeeping cores isolate karo. I/O tune karo (noatime, read-ahead, NVMe ke liye mq-deadline/noop). BIOS/firmware/NIC/NVSwitch firmware updated rakho. Containers me --ipc=host aur --ulimit memlock=-1 use karo.

A.7 · GPU resource management aur scheduling

  • Topology ko dhyan me rakh kar schedule karo (Kubernetes/SLURM, device plugins, Gres). MPS se chhote jobs ko ek GPU par overlap karo (jaise ek GPU par 4 training tasks).
  • MIG (7 slices tak) halke/inference jobs ke liye use karo, tightly coupled parallel jobs ke liye nahi. Persistence on rakho taaki MIG partitions jobs ke beech survive karein (MIG Manager boot par layout dobara banata hai, kyunki partitions reboot ke paar persist nahi hote).
  • Clocks lock karo (nvidia-smi -lgc/-lmc) jab consistency chahiye ho, ya power limit set karo. ECC on rakho (kuch percent bandwidth cost, multiweek runs ko protect karta hai). CPUs oversubscribe mat karo. NVSwitch systems par Fabric Manager running rakho. Jobs intelligently pack karo (jaise 4-GPU node par do 2-GPU jobs) bina memory/I/O exceed kiye.

A.8 · I/O optimization

  • Data loaders parallelise karo (num_workers badhao jab tak I/O bottleneck na rahe). Host memory pin karo (pin_memory=True). Compute aur transfers overlap karo (double-buffer, non_blocking=True, cudaMemcpyAsync).
  • Fast NVMe/parallel filesystems use karo, GDS jahan available ho. Badi files stripe/split karo. Lakhon chhoti files ko containers (tar/RecordIO) me pack karo ya batched-read libraries use karo. Client-side caching use karo.
  • Light compression use karo (LZ4/Zstd fast mode, multithreaded decompression, GDS/cuFile ke saath GPU decompression). Throughput measure karo (dstat/DCGM) aur I/O stalls hatakar GPU utilisation lagbhag 70% se 95% se upar le jaao.
  • Multinode ke liye I/O scale karo (data shard karo, per-node caches, local disks preload karo — Chapter 5 ka 160 GB/s example). Checkpoints/logs asynchronously likho, checkpoints compress karo, har Nth iteration par log karo. cuda-checkpoint/CRIU GPU process ko suspend karke usi type ke dusre GPU par restore kar sakta hai — model checkpoints ka complement hai, replacement nahi.

A.9 · Data processing pipelines

  • Binary/memory-mapped formats use karo (TFRecord, LMDB). Filesystem tune karo (noatime, read-ahead, sharding). CPU-bound pipelines ke liye hyperthreading disable karne par consider karo.
  • Data-loader thread priority badhao (chrt/pthread_setschedparam). Hot data cache karo (page cache/RAM disk). Aggressively prefetch/buffer karo (prefetch_factor, DistributedSampler).
  • Transforms parallelise karo ya GPU par move karo (DALI). Repeated tokens ke liye embeddings/KV cache karo. Data nodes ke paar shard karo (DeepSeek 3FS example — har node ke NVMe par sharding se multi-TB/s). Pipeline monitor karo aur batch size adjust karo. Simple augmentation GPU par streams ke through apply karo. End-to-end tokens/s measure karo — ideal (synthetic vs real data) se 10% se kam overhead ka target rakho.

A.10 · Performance profiling, debugging, aur monitoring

  • Regularly profile karo (timeline ke liye Nsight Systems, kernels ke liye Nsight Compute/PyTorch Profiler). Phases label karne ke liye NVTX/torch.profiler.record_function() use karo.
  • Python overhead khatm karo (vectorise karo, per-step logging avoid karo). Utilisation drops dekho aur unhe events se correlate karo (jaise har 5 minute par drop = checkpointing).
  • Warp divergence, ranks ke beech load balance (rank 0 aksar extra kaam karta hai), memory growth (leaks — torch.cuda.memory_summary() / Nsight memory trace se) aur network/disk health check karo. 100 Gbps link par fully utilised ho to 12.5 GB/s expect karo (100 Gb/s ÷ 8).
  • Alerts set karo (DCGM health, ECC, throttling). Har driver/framework/code change par regression benchmarks chalao — kernel ka chup-chap Tensor Core use khona classic regression hai.

A.11 · GPU programming aur CUDA tuning optimizations

  • Memory hierarchy ka respect karo (registers > shared/L1 > L2 > HBM). Global loads coalesce karo. Shared memory tiling use karo aur bank conflicts dekho (data pad karo). 128 bytes par align karo, float2/float4 vectorised loads use karo.
  • Transfers minimise karo (kai chhote arrays ko ek buffer me pack karo, cudaMemPrefetchAsync se prefetch karo). Frequent cudaMalloc/cudaFree avoid karo (pools, cudaMallocAsync, PyTorch caching allocator use karo).
  • Occupancy vs ILP balance karo (25% se kam occupancy ho to zyada threads per block consider karo, lekin register spilling par nazar rakho). Transfers ko compute ke saath overlap karo (cudaMemcpyAsync, TMA, cp.async). L2 me prefetch karo (cp.async.bulk.prefetch).
  • Local synchronisation ke liye cooperative groups use karo. Divergence avoid karo (data sort/group karo taaki warps uniform rahein, __shfl_sync/__ballot_sync use karo). Reductions/scans ke liye warp intrinsics use karo. Concurrency ke liye streams use karo, dependencies ke liye events. Libraries prefer karo (cuBLAS, cuDNN, Thrust, NCCL, NIXL, NVSHMEM). Repeated loops ke liye CUDA Graphs use karo. Roofline se scaling check karo. PTX/SASS ko Nsight Compute se inspect karo.
  • torch.compile use karo (typical 10–40%, kai models par 20–50% tak, warm-up ke baad; graphs stable hon aur static memory pools hon to CUDA Graphs ke saath). Dynamic shapes ke liye plan karo (mark_dynamic, torch.export, set_stance("fail_on_recompile"), error_on_graph_break()). Jahan PyTorch achhi tarah fuse na kare, wahan torch.library.triton_op se Triton use karo. Fixed input sizes ke liye cudnn.benchmark=True enable karo, aur nondeterministic algorithms allow karo jab exact reproducibility zaroori na ho. Read-only data ke liye const __restrict__ use karo taaki wo L1 read-only cache use kare.

A.12 · Kernel scheduling aur execution optimizations

  • Synchronisations minimise karo (cudaDeviceSynchronize sirf jab zaroori ho). Chhote kernels fuse karo (launch cost tens of microseconds hoti hai; pehle torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead") try karo, phir hotspots ke liye TMA/warp specialisation ke saath Triton).
  • Data-dependent kaam ke liye dynamic parallelism judiciously use karo. Repeated kaam ke liye persistent kernels use karo. Thread block clusters evaluate karo (Blackwell par 16 blocks tak) — cluster-aware synchronisation aur shared-memory residency ke saath, occupancy vs residency profile karke.

A.13 · Arithmetic optimizations aur reduced/mixed precision

  • Mixed precision (FP16/BF16, FP32 accumulation/master weights) lagbhag 1.5–3.5× deti hai, matmul-heavy kaam par zyada. Naye hardware par FP16 ke bajaye BF16 prefer karo (loss scaling nahi).
  • FP8 use karo (FP16 ka lagbhag 2×) Transformer Engine aur FP16/FP32 accumulators ke saath, hybrid E4M3 forward / E5M2 gradients, delayed scaling 256–1024. Inference ke liye calibration ke baad NVFP4 use karo (FP8 ka lagbhag 2×).
  • GEMM/convolution ke liye Tensor Cores + TMA use karo (8/16 ke multiples me tiles, FP16/BF16/TF32). Safe FP32 matmuls ke liye TF32 use karo (torch.set_float32_matmul_precision("high")). 2:4 structured sparsity se pruned layers par lagbhag 2× GEMM speed lo (retraining with sparsity chahiye).
  • Bandwidth-limited ho to gradients/activations compress karo (FP16/BF16/FP8). Inference ke liye INT8/INT4 quantisation use karo (calibration ke baad FP16 se INT8 par 2–4×). Scaling ko compute ke saath fuse karo (microscaling), alag passes ke bajaye.

A.14 · Advanced tuning strategies aur algorithmic tricks

  • Har GPU generation ke hisaab se block/tile/unroll autotune karo (Triton num_warps/num_stages, TMA layouts, tensor-map descriptors; naye hardware par dobara benchmark karo; 20–30% typical autotuning gain cited hai).
  • Optimisers, attention aur normalisation fuse karo (Transformer Engine, FasterTransformer). FlashAttention use karo (tiled/streaming attention, bade intermediates nahi, khaas taur par lambe sequences ke liye).
  • Communication aur compute overlap karo (async NCCL, DDP overlap, har layer ke gradients ready hote hi all-reduce). Pipeline parallelism me kaafi microbatches aur interleaved schedule use karo. ZeRO/DeepSpeed/Megatron-LM sharding use karo. Jahan convergence allow kare, asynchronous/stale SGD consider karo. Inference ke liye prune/distil karo (lottery ticket, structured pruning).

A.15 · Distributed training aur network optimization

  • Jahan available ho RDMA use karo (InfiniBand/RoCE, GPUDirect RDMA). Ethernet par TCP buffers badhao (net.core.rmem_max/wmem_max, tcp_rmem/tcp_wmem), MTU 9000 use karo, BBR consider karo.
  • NIC interrupts ko local NUMA node par pin karo. NCCL tune karo (NCCL_NTHREADS 4→8/16, NCCL_BUFFSIZE 1 MB→4 MB+, SHARP/CollNet NCCL_COLLNET_ENABLE=1, SHARP_COLL_LOCK_ON_COMM_INIT=1, SHARP_COLL_NUM_COLL_GROUP_RESOURCE_ALLOC_THRESHOLD=0 — sirf bade reductions par SHARP-capable fabrics ke liye).
  • Slow networks ke liye gradient accumulation use karo (kam, bade all-reduces). Hierarchical all-reduce use karo (pehle intranode, phir internode). Oversubscription avoid karo — 8 GPUs all-reduce ke dauran 200 Gbps se zyada generate kar sakte hain, to ek 100 Gbps NIC unhe constrain karta hai; multiple NICs / 200/400 Gbps InfiniBand use karo.
  • Communication compress karo (FP16/FP8 gradients, quantised activations, sketching — DeepSeek ki gradient compression cited hai). Health monitor karo (packet loss, InfiniBand resend counters, TCP retransmits, iPerf/NCCL tests).

A.16 · Efficient inference aur serving

  • Kubernetes + custom metrics se orchestrate karo, live demand par autoscale karo. Bursty traffic ke liye serverless/microservices consider karo.
  • Dynamic/continuous batching use karo (bade batches throughput badhate hain, bahut bade latency badhate hain; ek stream se GPU na bhare to parallel streams chalao).
  • Disaggregated serving me KV transfer ke liye NIXL use karo (GPUDirect RDMA, CPU nahi). Lambe contexts ke liye KV cache hierarchically offload karo (Dynamo Distributed KV Cache Manager, paged/quantised KV TensorRT-LLM/vLLM me) — cache miss ki latency cost validate karke.
  • vLLM, SGLang, Dynamo, TensorRT-LLM se serve karo (quantisation, fusion, optimised attention, tensor/pipeline/expert/context parallelism, speculative decoding, chunked prefill, disaggregated prefill/decode, dynamic batching).
  • Tail latency (p99) monitor karo, server ko cores par pin karo, noisy neighbours se isolate karo, zaroorat ho to real-time scheduling use karo. Warm up karo (model load karo, dummy inferences chalao) taaki cold-start latency na ho.
  • QoS ke liye resources partition karo (latency-sensitive inference ke liye dedicated GPUs ya MIG slices, training ko inference se alag rakho). Preprocessing (tokenisation, batch collation) ke liye Grace CPU shared memory me use karo. Edge/latency-critical deployments ke liye tune karo.

A.17 · Multinode inference aur serving

  • Prefill/decode disaggregate karo aur har ek ko independently scale karo. GPU ko high occupancy par rakhne ke liye continuous batching/token pooling (vLLM) use karo.
  • Oversized models shard karo (tensor/pipeline parallelism, NVLink/InfiniBand aur overlapped communication ke saath). Bahut lambe contexts ke liye hierarchical memory/offload use karo — thodi latency ke badle bahut zyada capacity.

A.18 · Power aur thermal management

  • Speed ke saath perf/watt aur thermals bhi track karo. Throttling dekho (kuch cases me lagbhag 85°C, clocks max se girte hain — nvidia-smi dmon se check karo).
  • Energy-aware scheduling use karo (power capping, dynamic clock scaling, workload ko renewable/saste-energy times par shift karna). Memory-bound kaam ke liye kam clock/power limit se power bach sakti hai bina runtime loss ke — book ka example: 100%→80% power limit, lagbhag wahi speed lagbhag 20% kam power par; bahut lambi runs par 90% power se component stress kam hota hai minimal slowdown ke saath.
  • Workloads consolidate karo (90% par ek GPU, 45% par do GPUs se zyada energy-efficient hai per watt — jab tak wall-clock time priority na ho; idle nodes band karo). Proactively cool karo (zyada fixed fan speed, fins saaf karo, water flow/temperature control). Per-GPU power monitor karo (nvidia-smi) aur spiky phases smooth karo (dense layers power spike karte hain, all-reduce kam draw karta hai).

A.19 · Appendix conclusion

Checklist ko ek repeatable playbook ki tarah treat karo — profile karo, sahi layer par sahi bottleneck tune karo, scale karne se pehle verify karo — aur naye tricks milte hi isme add karte raho. Har item har situation par lagu nahi hota, aur field evolve karta rahega — lekin relevant categories se systematically guzarne se zyada tar performance issues diagnose aur resolve ho jaayenge. Cluster ko drastically scale karne se pehle kam nodes par profile karo: agar all-reduce 8 GPUs par iteration ka 20% le raha hai, to bade scale par wo aur bigdega — khaas taur par ek NVL72 / VR200 / VR300 rack system se aage.

Interview FAQs — AI Systems Performance Engineering

Current-era interviews me GPU performance engineering se poochhe jaane wale sabse kaam ke 36 sawaal — har jawab wahi hai jo is SPA ke chapters me detail me samjhaya gaya hai. Kisi bhi sawaal par click karke jawab kholo.

Q01 Goodput kya hai aur ye FLOPS ya tokens/sec se behtar metric kyun hai?
Goodput = useful, correct, completed kaam per unit cost/time/energy. Raw FLOPS ya tokens/sec sirf kitna kaam hua batate hain — wo kaam useful tha ya discard ho gaya, ye nahi. Aisa system jo bahut tokens generate karta hai lekin output unusable ho ya retry me discard ho jaaye, uska throughput high hai lekin goodput low. Isiliye book goodput ko north-star metric banati hai — ye performance ko business aur energy cost se jodta hai.
Q02 Roofline model kya hai aur isse kernel ko kaise analyse karte ho?
Roofline attainable performance (FLOPS) ko arithmetic intensity (FLOPs per byte) ke against plot karta hai. Ceiling = min(peak compute, peak memory bandwidth × intensity). Agar tumhara kernel diagonal (memory) wali line ke neeche hai to wo memory-bound hai — bandwidth ya data reuse fix karo. Agar flat (compute) ceiling ke neeche hai to compute-bound hai — Tensor Cores, fusion, low precision use karo. Roofline batata hai ki kya fix karna hai, guess nahi karna padta.
Q03 Occupancy kya hai? Kya 100% occupancy hamesha best hoti hai?
Occupancy = resident warps / SM ke maximum warps. Ye threads per block, registers per thread aur shared memory per block se decide hoti hai. Nahi, 100% hamesha best nahi hoti. High ILP (instruction-level parallelism) wala kernel 50% occupancy par 100% se tez ho sakta hai, kyunki har thread per cycle zyada useful kaam karta hai. Zyada occupancy ke liye registers kam karne padte hain, jo ILP ghata deta hai. Optimum beech me hota hai aur measure karna padta hai.
Q04 Warp divergence kya hai aur ise kaise kam karte ho?
Warp ke 32 threads lockstep me execute hote hain. Agar warp ke threads alag branches lein (jaise if-else), to dono branches serially execute hoti hain — 16/16 split wala warp uniform warp se double kaam karta hai. Fix: data ko sort/group karo taaki warps uniform rahein, branchless code likho, predication use karo, ya warp intrinsics (__shfl_sync, __ballot_sync) se branch ke bina compute karo.
Q05 Memory coalescing kya hai aur ye itna important kyun hai?
Jab warp ke 32 threads consecutive addresses access karte hain, hardware unhe ek ya kuch transactions me serve kar deta hai. Strided ya scattered access utne hi data ke liye kai transactions mangta hai — bandwidth waste hoti hai. Isiliye data layout (SoA vs AoS), thread-to-data mapping aur vectorised loads (float4) itne important hain: coalesced access hi HBM bandwidth ka poora fayda uthata hai.
Q06 Shared memory bank conflicts kya hote hain?
Shared memory 32 banks me banti hai. Agar warp ke kai threads ek hi time par ek hi bank ko alag addresses ke saath access karein, to accesses serialise ho jaate hain — parallel access ka fayda khatm. Fix: data ko pad karo (jaise tile[32][32+1]) ya layout reorder karo taaki threads alag banks access karein.
Q07 NCCL kya hai? Ring, tree aur CollNet me kya farak hai?
NCCL NVIDIA ki library hai collective operations (all-reduce, all-gather, broadcast, reduce-scatter) ke liye — distributed training ka main communication lever. Ye topology dekh kar algorithm chunta hai: ring har GPU ko neighbour se jodta hai, tree root par aggregate karta hai, CollNet/SHARP reduction ka hissa InfiniBand switch par offload karta hai. SHARP sirf bade reductions par SHARP-capable fabric par fayda deta hai. NVLink Switch topologies par PAT (Parallel Aggregated Tree) parallel aggregate karke synchronisation time kam karta hai.
Q08 GPUDirect Storage (GDS) kya hai aur kab use karna chahiye?
GDS se GPU seedha NVMe ya network storage se padhta hai — CPU bounce buffer bypass — cuFile API ke through. Bade datasets par CPU load aur latency dono kam hoti hai. Use karo jab data loading GPU ko idle rakh rahi ho aur dataset bada ho. Ye NVIDIA Magnum IO stack ka hissa hai.
Q09 NUMA awareness kyun zaroori hai? Galat NUMA node par process chalane se kya hota hai?
Multi-socket servers me memory aur devices ek particular NUMA node se attached hote hain. Galat node par chalne wala process har memory ya DMA access par cross-NUMA penalty deta hai. Fix: numactl/taskset se process ko sahi node par pin karo, memory ko local node se bind karo (--membind), aur NIC/GPU interrupts ko usi node ke cores par bind karo.
Q10 MIG kya hai aur kab use karna chahiye?
MIG (Multi-Instance GPU) ek physical GPU ko 7 isolated instances tak baant deta hai — har ek ki apni memory, compute aur caches. Chhote jobs ya isolation (jaise latency-sensitive inference) ke liye use karo. Tightly coupled parallel jobs ke liye nahi — wahan poora GPU chahiye. Partitions reboot ke paar persist nahi hote; MIG Manager boot par layout dobara banata hai, isiliye persistence on rakhna zaroori hai.
Q11 NVLink, NVSwitch aur InfiniBand me kya farak hai?
NVLink 5 per GPU 1.8 TB/s bidirectional — GPU-to-GPU, rack ke andar. NVSwitch kai GPUs ko usi speed par jodta hai — NVL72 me 72 GPUs ek domain me, ~130 TB/s aggregate. InfiniBand nodes ke beech ka fabric hai — 400 Gb/s tak, ~0.6 µs latency, lossless. Hierarchy yaad rakho: NVLink (intra-rack, sabse tez) → InfiniBand/RoCE (inter-node) → Ethernet/TCP (sabse slow, ~10 µs latency).
Q12 TMA kya hai aur ye cp.async se behtar kyun hai?
TMA (Tensor Memory Accelerator) hardware unit hai jo poore tensors ko global se shared memory me asynchronously move karta hai — descriptors tensor layout describe karte hain. cp.async se tez aur simple hai tiled GEMMs ke liye, aur threads ko individual loads issue karne se aazaad karta hai — threads compute par focus karte hain, TMA data movement sambhalta hai.
Q13 Warp specialization kya hai?
Block ke alag warps ko alag roles dena — producer warps sirf data load karte hain (TMA se) aur consumer warps sirf compute karte hain (Tensor Cores par wgmma). Isse wo synchronisation khatm hoti hai jo warna har stage boundary par saare warps ko wait karwati. Producer/consumer pattern pipelined GEMM ka core hai.
Q14 CUDA Graphs kab use karne chahiye?
Jab ek loop me kai chhote kernels baar-baar launch hote hain aur CPU launch overhead (har launch tens of microseconds) bottleneck ban jaaye. Graph poori sequence ko capture karke ek CPU call se launch karta hai. Shart: captured pointers stable rehne chahiye — isiliye graphs ke saath stream-ordered allocations (cudaMallocAsync) aur static memory pools use hote hain.
Q15 Stream-ordered allocation (cudaMallocAsync) cudaMalloc se kaise alag hai?
cudaMalloc host-synchronising hota hai aur allocator ko serialisation point bana deta hai. cudaMallocAsync/cudaFreeAsync me memory ki lifetime stream ke order se bandhi hoti hai — allocator memory reuse kar sakta hai jaise hi stream free point se guzarta hai, bina host sync ke. Isse allocations CUDA Graph capture ke compatible bhi ho jaati hain. Memory pools (cudaMemPool_t) fragmentation aur overhead aur kam karte hain.
Q16 DDP vs FSDP — kab kaun sa?
DDP: har GPU ke paas model ki poori copy, alag data shard; gradients all-reduce. Simple aur fast — jab model ek GPU me fit ho. FSDP: parameters, gradients aur optimiser state GPUs ke paar shard — ek GPU se bada model bhi train hota hai. Keemat: extra communication (layer se pehle all-gather, baad me reduce-scatter). ZeRO teen stages me shard karta hai — pehle optimiser state, phir gradients, phir parameters.
Q17 Activation checkpointing kya hai aur iska trade-off kya hai?
Forward pass me activations discard karo, backward pass me recompute karo — lagbhag 30% extra compute ke badle me badi memory saving, taaki bada batch ya model fit ho. Trade-off yaad rakho: non-memory-limited job ko ye slow kar sakta hai — isiliye pehle profile karo ki memory hi bottleneck hai ya nahi.
Q18 torch.compile kaise kaam karta hai? Graph break kya hai?
torch.compile teen layers se banta hai: TorchDynamo Python code ko FX graph me capture karta hai, AOTAutograd forward/backward graphs alag karta hai, TorchInductor Triton/CUDA code generate karta hai (fusion, tile sizes). Graph break woh jagah hai jahan Dynamo capture nahi kar sakta (jaise data-dependent Python control flow) aur eager fallback hota hai. Bahut zyada graph breaks compile ka fayda khatm kar dete hain. Typical gains 10–40% hain, warm-up ke baad — correctness pehle check karo.
Q19 Triton kya hai aur kab CUDA ke bajaye Triton likhna chahiye?
Triton Python-jaisi bhasha hai GPU kernels likhne ke liye — programmer ek tile ka kaam describe karta hai, Triton CUDA code generate karta hai, coalescing/pipelining handle karta hai aur block size/warps/stages autotune karta hai. Use karo jab torch.compile/Inductor achhi tarah fuse na kare ya custom kernel chahiye ho — CUDA se tez likhna aur maintain karna aasan hai, aur autotuning free me milti hai.
Q20 Speculative decoding kaise kaam karta hai?
Chhota, fast draft model kai tokens propose karta hai, bada target model unhe ek forward pass me verify karta hai. Draft aam taur par sahi ho to bada model ek pass me kai tokens nikalta hai — ek token per pass ke bajaye. Asli metric acceptance rate hai: draft galat ho to fayda khatm. vLLM/SGLang me draft aur target model configure karke acceptance rate aur tokens/sec measure karo.
Q21 TTFT aur TBT kya hain? Dono ko ek saath optimise kyun mushkil hai?
TTFT (time to first token) prefill se dominated hai — prompt process karne ka time. TBT (time between tokens) decode se dominated hai — ek token generate karne ka time. Prefill compute-bound hai, decode memory-bound — ek ko optimise karne wali setting (jaise bada batch) dusre ki latency badha sakti hai. Isiliye dono ko saath me SLOs ke roop me dekho, aur disaggregated prefill/decode dono ko alag tune karne deta hai.
Q22 Prefix caching kya hai aur ye kab bada win deta hai?
Pehle dekhe gaye prompt prefix (system prompt, common document) ke liye KV cache reuse karo — prefill dobara compute nahi karni padti. Repeated prompts (jaise wahi system prompt har request me) par bada win hai — TTFT directly kam hota hai. Radix tree cached prefixes ko token sequence se index karta hai taaki longest matching prefix jaldi mil jaaye.
Q23 Disaggregated prefill/decode kya hai? KV transfer ki cost kya hai?
Prefill (compute-bound) aur decode (memory-bound) ko alag GPU pools par chalao, taaki har ek ko independently scale aur tune kiya ja sake. Cost: prefill ke baad KV cache ko decode GPU par move karna padta hai — ye transfer disaggregation ki main cost hai. Isiliye transfer tez hona chahiye (GPUDirect RDMA, NIXL) aur compute ke saath overlapped. Prefill/decode ratio workload se match karna chahiye, warna ek side idle rehti hai.
Q24 Paged KV cache kya hai? Block size ka trade-off kya hai?
KV cache ko fixed-size blocks (pages) me store karo jo on demand allocate hote hain — virtual memory ki tarah. Fragmentation kam hoti hai aur prefix share karne wale requests memory share kar sakte hain. Trade-off: chhote blocks waste kam karte hain lekin metadata overhead badhate hain; bade blocks efficient hote hain lekin chhoti sequences par zyada memory waste karte hain.
Q25 Chunked prefill kyun zaroori hai?
Lamba prompt ek hi prefill me poora process ho to decode block ho jaata hai aur dusre requests ki latency spike hoti hai. Chunked prefill prompt ko chunks me baant kar decode steps ke saath interleave karta hai — TTFT thoda badh sakta hai, lekin TBT stable rehta hai aur koi request starve nahi hoti.
Q26 FP8 aur FP4 training vs inference me kaise use hote hain?
FP8 Tensor Cores FP16 ka lagbhag 2× throughput dete hain. Training me hybrid FP8 use hota hai — forward ke liye E4M3, gradients ke liye E5M2 — delayed scaling window 256–1024 ke saath, Transformer Engine ke through. Inference me pehle FP8 evaluate karo; NVFP4 (FP8 ka 2×) tabhi adopt karo jab calibration ke baad quality acceptable ho. Microscaling (chhote groups ke liye alag scale factor) hi FP4 ko usable banata hai.
Q27 2:4 structured sparsity kya hai?
Weights ka 50% ek fixed pattern me zero karo — har 4 me se 2. Hardware is pattern ko exploit karke un layers ke GEMM throughput ko lagbhag double kar deta hai. Shart: model ko us pattern par prune/retrain karna padta hai — bina retraining ke quality gir jaati hai.
Q28 FlashAttention kyun tez hai?
FlashAttention attention ko tile karke streaming tareeke se compute karta hai — bade intermediate matrices (N×N) kabhi materialise nahi hote. Tiling se data SRAM/shared memory me rehta hai, HBM traffic bahut kam ho jaata hai. Khaas taur par lambe sequences par bada win hai, jahan attention matrix memory me fit bhi nahi hoti.
Q29 Continuous batching static batching se kaise behtar hai?
Static batching me poora batch khatm hone ka intezar hota hai. Continuous batching me naya request running batch me add ho jaata hai jaise hi slot khaali ho. Variable-length LLM requests ke saath ye high utilisation paane ki main wajah hai — GPU kabhi poore batch ke khatm hone ka wait nahi karta.
Q30 Nsight Systems vs Nsight Compute — kab kaun sa?
Nsight Systems (nsys) timeline dikhata hai — poora system: CPU, GPU, kernels, copies, gaps. Pehle yahi chalao taaki pata chale time kahan ja raha hai (prefill? decode? data movement? idle?). Nsight Compute (ncu) ek kernel ke andar jhankta hai — occupancy, memory efficiency, L1/L2 hit rates, DRAM throughput, "Issues" section. Workflow: pehle Systems se bottleneck dhoondho, phir Compute se us kernel ko tune karo.
Q31 PyTorch me memory fragmentation kaise diagnose aur fix karte ho?
torch.cuda.memory_summary() se dekho — reserved vs allocated memory ka gap fragmentation dikhata hai. PyTorch ka caching allocator freed blocks reuse karta hai, lekin split blocks se fragmentation hoti hai. Fix: allocations ko stable rakho, cudaMallocAsync/memory pools use karo, batch size consistent rakho, aur PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True consider karo.
Q32 Power capping se performance kaise bachti hai? 80% power par speed wahi kaise rehti hai?
Memory-bound kaam me GPU compute units waise bhi poori tarah busy nahi hote — power limit kam karne se clocks thode girte hain lekin memory bandwidth wahi rehti hai, to runtime lagbhag wahi rehta hai. Book ka example: 100%→80% power limit, lagbhag wahi speed lagbhag 20% kam power par. Compute-bound kaam par aisa nahi hota — wahan power cap seedha speed ghata deta hai. Isiliye pehle jaano workload memory-bound hai ya compute-bound.
Q33 Multimillion-GPU clusters me sabse badi problems kya hain?
Per-kernel speed nahi — fault tolerance (cluster me kahin na kahin har kuch minutes me ek GPU fail hota hai), power aur cooling (hundreds of megawatts), aur network topology (all-to-all communication ko fast rakhna). Isiliye checkpointing, redundant computation aur fast recovery built-in hone chahiye. Book ka rule: 8 GPUs par 20% all-reduce share bade scale par sirf aur bigdega — pehle chhote scale par profile karo, tabhi scale out karo.
Q34 Kernel fusion se kya fayda hai? Pehle torch.compile try kyun karna chahiye?
Fusion kai chhote kernels ko ek me milata hai — intermediate results registers/shared memory me rehte hain (global memory round-trip nahi), aur launch overhead ek baar hi lagta hai. Pehle torch.compile(mode="reduce-overhead") try karo kyunki Inductor ye fusion automatically kar deta hai — custom kernel likhna tabhi jab compiler target meet na kare. Book ka rule: agar torch.compile target meet karta hai to custom kernel mat likho.
Q35 Tensor parallelism vs pipeline parallelism — inference me kab kaun sa?
Tensor parallelism har layer ko GPUs ke paar baant ta hai — har layer par all-reduce chahiye, isiliye fast interconnect (NVLink) zaroori hai; latency kam karne ke liye best hai. Pipeline parallelism layers ko stages me baant ta hai — request stages se guzarta hai; kaafi micro-batches chahiye warna pipeline bubbles (idle stages) capacity waste karte hain. MoE models me expert parallelism bhi aata hai — alag experts alag GPUs par. Aksar teeno combine hote hain.
Q36 Performance regression ko production me kaise pakadte ho?
Har driver/framework/code change par regression benchmarks chalao (MLPerf baseline ke roop me). Classic regression: kernel chup-chap Tensor Core use karna band kar de — speed gir jaati hai lekin koi error nahi aata. Isiliye utilisation dashboards, DCGM health alerts, ECC/throttling alerts aur TTFT/TBT SLO monitoring zaroori hai — aur har change se pehle aur baad me profile karo.
