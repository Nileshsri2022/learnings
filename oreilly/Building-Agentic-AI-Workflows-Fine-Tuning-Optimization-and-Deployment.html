01Foreword · Series Editor Foreword

Foreword — AI engineering, model development se alag kaam hai

Ye kitab intermediate developer ke liye hai — jo generative AI ko sirf samajhna nahi, istemal karna chahta hai. Iska sabse bada idea ye hai: AI engineering, model development se alag cheez hai. Model banana ek kaam hai; uske aas-paas bharosemand system banana doosra kaam hai.

01.1 AI engineering ka matlab kya hai

AI engineering ka matlab hai — language model ke aas-paas ek poora system khada karna: sahi model chunna, retrieval banana, prompting karna, experimentation aur evaluation karna, aur phir deploy karte waqt latency, cost, accuracy aur privacy — chaaron ko dhyaan me rakhna.

  • Ek model se kaam nahi chalta: zyadatar AI use cases me ek nahi, kai models chahiye hote hain — alag-alag kaam ke liye alag model.
  • Chhota model, bada kaam: koi ek model har kaam ke liye best nahi hota. Chhote models bade models ko accuracy me hara sakte hain — ye sirf experimentation se pata chalta hai.
  • Experiment karo, phir faisla karo: kai models ko aazmao, measure karo, aur phir chunno. Andaze se model chunna galat hai.

01.2 Kitab me kya-kya cover hai

Kitab me ye sab hai: prompting, RAG, fine-tuning, model evaluation, multi-agent systems, multimodality, tool use, model compression aur cloud deployment. Approach poora hands-on aur code-led hai — padhne ke baad reader ke paas projects ka ek portfolio banta hai.

Ant ka maksad ek hi hai: aise AI systems banana jo production me kaam karein — notebook me demo dene wale nahi, asli users ke saamne chalne wale.

Yaad rakhne wali baat: model ki taakat se zyada farak is baat se padta hai ki uske aas-paas ka system kitna socha-samjha hai — retrieval, evaluation, guardrails aur deployment. Yehi AI engineering hai.

Preface — Model akela solution nahi hota

Aaj AI roz-marra ki zindagi ka hissa ban chuka hai. LLMs kai fields me useful kaam kar sakte hain — kuch cases me ek achhe human expert ke barabar level par. Lekin sirf model ki skill se usable product nahi banta. Asli system me model ki skill ke saath practical software judta hai — aur uski quality maani nahi jaati, measure ki jaati hai.

02.1 Agentic AI kya hai

Kitab ka subject hai agentic AI — aise systems jo ek goal lete hain, plan karte hain, reason karte hain, tools istemal karte hain aur chalte-chalte adjust karte hain. Sirf ek sawaal ka jawaab dena inka kaam nahi; kaam ko poora karke dena inka kaam hai.

02.2 Kitab ka running theme — software ki tarah banao

Agentic systems ko waise hi design karna chahiye jaise baaki software banta hai:

  • Chhote hisson me todo: bada system ek hi block me nahi, chhote-chhote parts me banta hai.
  • Continuously test karo: har hissa test hota rahe.
  • Chosen metrics se measure karo: pehle se tay metrics ke against quality naapi jaaye.
  • Chhote kadmon me improve karo: ek saath bada badlaav nahi, chhote-chhote sudhaar.

02.3 Kitab ki structure — poora safar

Kitab ka safar is order me chalta hai:

  • Foundations: AI aur LLM kya hain, alignment kaise hoti hai, context kaise manage hota hai, useful jawaab kaise mile.
  • Workflow building: pehla workflow kaise banta hai.
  • Evaluation: system ko measure kaise karte hain.
  • Agents aur multi-agent systems: ReAct, supervisor pattern, deep research.
  • Multimodality aur coding agents: text se aage — image, audio, code.
  • Reasoning models aur computer use: sochne wale models aur computer chalane wale agents.
  • Fine-tuning, optimization aur deployment: production ke liye taiyaari.

02.4 Kaun padhe, kaise padhe

Kitab un developers ke liye hai jo pehle se code karte hain — khaas taur par AI/ML engineers, data scientists, software engineers, product builders aur engineering leaders.

Ek practice poori kitab me baar-baar aati hai: har badlaav se pehle aur baad me measure karo — latency, cost, accuracy aur quality, chaaron ko check karo.

Code Python me hai aur ye tools istemal hote hain: LangChain, LangGraph, OpenAI, Anthropic, Cohere, Hugging Face, Chroma aur Ollama. Saara code kitab ke saath wale GitHub repo me maujood hai.

02.5 Ethics aur kanoon

AI systems ko ethically aur legally istemal karna zaroori hai — copyright, privacy aur data-use rules ka samman karo. Kitab ka koi bhi hissa AI se logon ko nuksaan pahunchane ke liye encourage nahi karta.

03Part I · Chapter 1

Ch 1 — AI, LLMs aur Agents ka Introduction

Zyadatar logon ke liye AI ki asli challenge behtar model banana nahi hai — models ko achhe se deploy karna hai, jisme latency, cost, accuracy aur privacy ka balance ho. Is chapter me neev banti hai: AI kya hai, language model kaise kaam karta hai, prompt engineering kya hai, context engineering kya hai, tools kya hain, aur agentic AI workflow se aage kaise badhta hai.

03.1 AI me teen roles — aur AI engineer kaun hai

AI ke kaam me teen roles aate hain:

  • Data scientist: data se insight nikalta hai, analysis aur modelling karta hai.
  • Machine-learning engineer: models ko train karta hai aur unhe systems me lagata hai.
  • AI engineer: LLMs ke upar applications banata hai — model ko lena, uske aas-paas workflow, tools aur measurement banana, ye uska kaam hai.

Ye kitab teesre role — AI engineer — ke liye hai.

03.2 Language model kya hai — probabilistic engine

Language model ek probabilistic engine hai jo language par train hua hai: use text do, wo agla token predict karta hai. Token ek shabd ka hissa ya poora shabd hota hai. Tokenization — raw text ko numbers me badalne ka tareeka — chapter me Statue of Liberty wale sentence se samjhaya gaya hai.

Modern LLMs Transformer architecture par based hain. Transformer me attention hota hai — model ye weigh kar sakta hai ki kaunse shabd ek doosre se kitne strongly related hain. Yehi wajah hai ki models kai tasks par scale kar jaate hain.

Models ki training ko parameters se naapa jaata hai — parameters wo learned numbers hain jo model store karta hai. Bade models aam taur par zyada mehenge hote hain aur train karne ke liye zyada data chahiye hota hai. Lekin chhote models bhi focused kaam ke liye behtar ho sakte hain — isliye ek system me alag-alag jagah alag size ke models istemal ho sakte hain.

03.3 Do LLM families — reader aur writer

Kitab do main LLM families batati hai:

  • Autoencoding (bidirectional, “reader”) models — jaise BERT: poora text padhkar samajhte hain. Ye samajhne ke liye best hain — classification, search, analysis.
  • Autoregressive (“writer”) models — jaise GPT: text ko ek piece at a time generate karte hain. Ye likhne aur generate karne ke liye best hain.

Ek aur idea hai extended mind — AI ko insaani soch ka bahari extension mana ja sakta hai, jaise notebook: notebook aapki memory ka hissa ban jaata hai, waise hi AI aapki soch ka hissa ban sakta hai.

03.5 Alignment — model ko sahi raaste par rakhna

Do main alignment methods samjhaye gaye hain:

  • Instructional alignment: model ko instructions follow karne aur task par bane rehne ke liye train karna.
  • Behavior alignment: model ko sensibly aur safely behave karne ke liye train karna — isme RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) jaise techniques aate hain.

03.6 Prompt engineering — paanch tareeke

Prompt engineering ko paanch methods ke saath sikhaya gaya hai, har ek ke saath example:

  • Direct instruction: seedha bolo kya chahiye — jaise “is topic par kavita likho…”.
  • Few-shot prompting: kuch examples de do, model pattern seekh leta hai.
  • Chain-of-thought prompting: model se kaho step by step reason kare — isse accuracy badhti hai, jaise math problems par best LLMs ke saath lagbhag 67% se lagbhag 90% tak (chapter ke mutabik).
  • Prompt chaining: kaam ko prompts ki series me todo — har prompt ka output agle prompt ka input banta hai.
  • Prompt ordering: sabse important instructions pehle do — kyunki models prompt ke shuru aur ant ko beech se zyada weight dete hain.

03.7 Inference parameters — output ko control karna

Model ke output ko control karne wale parameters:

  • Temperature: output kitna varied ya deterministic hoga — low temperature = predictable, high = creative/varied.
  • top_p (nucleus sampling): sirf un tokens me se chunna jinki cumulative probability p tak ho.
  • top_k: choices ko sabse likely k tokens tak limit karna.
  • max_tokens: response ki length ki limit.
  • stop parameter: kis point par rukna hai.
Figure 1.6 — Temperature ka effect

Temperature low ho to model hamesha lagbhag ek jaisa, safe jawaab deta hai. Temperature high ho to jawaab varied aur creative hote hain — lekin galti ka chance bhi badhta hai. Isliye factual kaam me low temperature aur creative kaam me high temperature istemal hota hai.

Figure 1.6 — temperature ka effect, trend ke roop me. Exact chart values kitab ke prose me table ke roop me nahi diye gaye, isliye yahan sirf trend bataya gaya hai.
Figure 1.7 — top_p sampling

03.8 Prompt caching — baar-baar wali cheez sasti

Repeated prompt prefixes ko provider store karke rakhta hai — isse baad wali calls faster aur cheaper hoti hain. Cache lagbhag 5 se 10 minutes ki inactivity ke baad expire ho jaata hai. Prompt caching reasoning models ke liye bhi matter karta hai, jahan reasoning text kabhi-kabhi reuse ho sakta hai.

03.9 Structured output — model ko software component banao

Model se kaha ja sakta hai ki wo data JSON me, ek schema ke mutabik return kare. Isse model ek software component ki tarah kaam kar sakta hai — uska output seedha code me istemal ho sakta hai, parse karne ki jhanjhat nahi.

03.10 Context engineering — context window ek short-term memory hai

LLM ki context window ek badi short-term memory ki tarah hai — isme instructions, examples aur retrieved documents rehte hain. Achha engineer design karta hai ki is window me kya jaaye aur kya na jaaye.

Figure 1.10 — Context window — kya-kya andar hota hai

Figure 1.10 — context window ek short-term memory hai; isme jo daaloge, model wahi dekhega.

Models me positional bias hota hai: wo context ke shuru aur ant par beech se zyada dhyaan dete hain — isliye sabse achhi jaankari kinaron par rakhni chahiye.

Beech — kam dhyaan — beech ki jaankari dab jaati hai, model use miss kar sakta hai
Ant — zyada dhyaan — aakhiri instructions par bhi model ka dhyaan zyada hota hai

“Needle in a haystack” problem: model ko ek lambe document me se ek relevant fact dhoondhna hota hai. Jaise-jaise prompts bade hote hain, context windows bharte hain aur model ko focus karne me madad chahiye hoti hai.

03.12 Agentic AI — ek call se aage

Agentic AI ek call se aage ka step hai: agent ek aisa system hai jo plan kar sakta hai, reason kar sakta hai, act kar sakta hai, results observe kar sakta hai aur repeat kar sakta hai — goals, tools, memory aur autonomy ke saath.

  • Workflow: steps ka fixed sequence — har baar wahi order.
  • Agent: system khud decide karta hai ki agla step kaunsa ho.

03.13 LLM types — closed-source vs open-weight

Chapter LLM types ka survey deta hai:

  • Closed-source models (jaise OpenAI, Anthropic, Google ke models): API se milte hain, powerful hote hain, lekin cost aur control provider ke haath me hota hai.
  • Open-weight models (jaise Llama, Mistral): weights khule hote hain, khud host kar sakte ho — control zyada, lekin infrastructure tumhara.

Trade-offs dono taraf hain — kaunsa model kis kaam ke liye, ye experiment se tay hota hai.

13Interview FAQs · 36 sawaal


Interview FAQs — 36 sawaal-jawaab


Ye 36 FAQs kitab ke har chapter se liye gaye hain — interview me poochhe jaane wale concepts, numbers aur trade-offs ke saath. Har jawaab me kitab ka asli number ya experiment hai.


Q01Workflow aur agent me kya farak hai?
Workflow steps ka fixed sequence hota hai — har baar wahi order, deterministic. Agent khud decide karta hai ki agla step kaunsa ho — flexible lekin mehanga. Jahan fixed workflow kaam kare, wahan workflow sasta aur bharosemand hai.
Q02ReAct pattern kya hai?
ReAct = Reason + Act. Agent sochta hai, tool chunta hai, call karta hai, result observe karta hai — aur jab tak jawaab na mile, loop repeat karta hai. Ye basic agent loop hai, LangGraph ke create_react_agent se banta hai.
Q03Chain-of-thought prompting se accuracy kitni badhi?
Chapter 1 ke mutabik, math problems par best LLMs ke saath accuracy lagbhag 67% se lagbhag 90% tak badhi. CoT se model step by step reason karta hai, isliye mushkil sawaalon par behtar karta hai.
Q04Prompt caching kya hai aur kitni der chalta hai?
Repeated prompt prefixes provider store karke rakhta hai — baad wali calls faster aur cheaper hoti hain. Cache lagbhag 5 se 10 minutes ki inactivity ke baad expire ho jaata hai. Reasoning models me reasoning text bhi kabhi-kabhi reuse ho sakta hai.
Q05Positional bias kya hai?
Models context ke shuru aur ant par beech se zyada dhyaan dete hain. Isliye sabse important jaankari kinaron par rakhni chahiye — aur “lost in the middle” problem me beech wali summary miss ho jaati hai.
Q06Context engineering kya hai?
Context window ek badi short-term memory hai — isme instructions, examples aur retrieved documents rehte hain. Achha engineer design karta hai ki window me kya jaaye, kitna jaaye aur kis order me jaaye.
Q07Temperature, top_p aur top_k me kya farak hai?
Temperature output ki variety control karta hai (low = predictable, high = creative). top_p sirf un tokens ko consider karta hai jinki cumulative probability p ke andar ho. top_k choices ko sabse likely k tokens tak limit karta hai.
Q08Text-to-SQL workflow ke teen steps kya hain?
describe_database (schema do), model SQL likhe, run_sql_against_database (query chalao). Test me “How many patients?” ka jawaab 100 aaya, aur Pneumonia wali drug Vancomycin nikli.
Q09LangGraph me State aur checkpointer kya karte hain?
State object data ko steps ke beech carry karta hai. Checkpointer (MemorySaver) workflow ki state save karta hai — taaki baad wala sawaal pehle wali baat-cheet ko refer kar sake.
Q10Human-in-the-loop kyun lagaya jaata hai?
Workflow SQL chalane ya email bhejne se pehle ruk jaata hai, taaki insaan approve ya edit kar sake. Ye galat query ya galat email ko rokne ka sabse seedha tareeka hai.
Q11RAG workflow ke steps kya hain?
Document load karo, chunks me todo, har chunk ko embed karo, Chroma vector DB me store karo, query time par top-k similar chunks retrieve karo aur unhe context dekar jawaab banao.
Q12SQA benchmark kya naapta hai?
SQL Query Accuracy — model ki SQL query wahi result deti hai ya nahi jo ground-truth query deti hai. GPT-4o-mini: basic 33%, few-shot 37%, CoT 40%, semantic few-shot + CoT 45%.
Q13Precision, recall, MRR aur NDCG kya hain?
Precision = retrieved me se kitne relevant. Recall = relevant me se kitne mile. MRR = pehla relevant result kitna upar aaya. NDCG = ranking ki quality — relevant cheez upar ho to score zyada.
Q14Table 3.1 me best embedding model kaunsa tha?
Cohere V4 — MRR 0.85, Recall@5 0.95. Lekin differences chhote the (BGE 0.80 tak). Embedder chunte waqt cost, latency aur privacy bhi dekhni chahiye.
Q15“Lost in the middle” problem kya hai?
Jab model ko kai summaries diye jaate hain, to shuru ya ant wali relevant summary ke saath wo behtar karta hai — beech wali ke saath bura. Summaries zyada hon to accuracy aur girti hai.
Q16Multi-perspective evaluation se kya pata chala?
Retriever, generator aur poore system ki accuracy alag-alag naapi gayi — retriever aksar sabse kamzor hissa hota hai. Isliye retrieval sudhaarne se sabse bada fayda milta hai.
Q17Agent ki anatomy ke chaar hisse kya hain?
LLM (brain), tools (hands), memory (state) aur loop (think, act, observe). Ye chaaron milkar agent bante hain.
Q18Supervisor pattern kya hai?
Ek main agent plan karta hai aur sub-agents ko delegate karta hai — har task ke liye ek sub-agent. SDR agent me yehi pattern hai: lead search, qualify, email aur logging alag sub-agents karte hain.
Q19Table 4.1 me best LLM kaunsa tha aur sabse sasta kaunsa?
GPT-4.1-mini ki accuracy sabse zyada thi (0.87). Llama 4 Scout sabse sasta ($0.00005 per 1,000 tokens) aur sabse fast (0.3 sec) tha.
Q20Guardrails kya hote hain?
Rules jo agent ko limit karte hain — jaise approval ke bina email na bhejo, dangerous commands na chalao. Guidelines sirf suggest karti hain; guardrails enforce karte hain.
Q21BM25 kya hai?
Best Matching 25 — keyword-based ranking jo TF-IDF se documents rank karta hai. Embedding similarity ka sasta alternative hai, aur tab achha kaam karta hai jab exact words matter karte hon.
Q22Re-ranker kya karta hai?
Re-ranker ek doosra, zyada accurate model hota hai jo pehle cheap retrieval ke top results ko relevance ke hisaab se dobara score karta hai. Pehla step fast, doosra step accurate.
Q23Multi-agent ke teen patterns kaunse hain?
Supervisor (ek delegate kare), network (agents seedha baat karein) aur swarm (agents ek doosre ko task handoff karein). Multi-agent zyada accurate lekin slower aur mehanga hota hai.
Q24Agents ke characteristic failures kya hain?
Tool istemal karna jab nahi karna chahiye, tool na istemal karna jab karna chahiye, aur tools ke prompt me order se mislead ho jaana (positional bias).
Q25CLIP kya hai?
Contrastive Language-Image Pre-training — images aur captions ko ek hi vector space me paas laata hai. Matching pairs ki similarity maximize, non-matching ki minimize. Image retrieval me CLIP aur SigLIP sabse accurate rahe.
Q26Vision Transformer (ViT) kaise kaam karta hai?
ViT image ko patches me todta hai aur har patch ko ek shabd ki tarah treat karta hai — phir Transformer attention se image ko samajhta hai.
Q27Reasoning models kab istemal karne chahiye?
Sirf wahan jahan wo madad karte hain — math, logic aur multi-step problems me. Simple kaam me non-reasoning model utna hi achha aur bahut fast hota hai. Effort low se high karne par accuracy lagbhag flat rehti hai, cost/latency sharply badhte hain.
Q28Reasoning effort badhane se kya hota hai?
Accuracy reliably nahi badhti — ek experiment me accuracy flat rahi jab effort badha, jabki latency aur cost sharply badh gaye. Claude 4 Sonnet single-shot: bina reasoning 82.7%, reasoning ke saath 81.4%.
Q29Computer-use agent grounding kya hai?
Model ko screen par exact batana hota hai ki kahan click karna hai. Ye IoU (intersection over union) aur centroid score se naapa jaata hai — predicted click asli point ke kitna paas hai.
Q30LoRA kya hai?
Low-rank adaptation — fine-tuning ka method jo sirf chhote number me extra parameters train karta hai. Isse fine-tuning sasta aur fast hota hai. Unsloth library LoRA ko aur fast bana deti hai.
Q31Fine-tuning me best epochs kitne the aur kyun?
5 epochs best tha — policy-task score lagbhag 21.5%. 10 epochs overfit ho gaya: training data rat liya, naye sawaalon par bura kiya. Token accuracy dono ki lagbhag barabar thi (0.21) — isliye held-out score hi asli test hai.
Q32Calibration aur ECE kya hain?
Calibration ka matlab: model ki confidence uski accuracy se match kare. ECE (expected calibration error) dono ke gap ko naapta hai. Fine-tuning calibration ko behtar ya bura — dono — kar sakta hai, isliye check zaroori hai.
Q33Quantization kya hai?
Model ke weights ko kam bits me store karna — jaise 32-bit se 8-bit ya 4-bit. Memory (VRAM) bachti hai aur inference fast hota hai, lekin chhote models me accuracy gir sakti hai.
Q34Speculative decoding kaise kaam karta hai?
Chhota draft model kai tokens propose karta hai, bada target model unhe ek saath check karta hai aur agree wale accept kar leta hai. Throughput badhti hai, outputs wahi rehte hain. Qwen3-8B experiment me yehi dikhaya gaya.
Q35Matryoshka embeddings kya hain?
Embedding model ko is tarah train karna ki vectors truncate kiye ja sakein — jaise 768 se 128 dimensions — bina zyada accuracy khoye. Chhota vector fast retrieval ke liye, bada vector accurate re-ranking ke liye.
Q36Table 9.1 ke baare me kya dhyaan rakhna hai?
Uske do numeric columns ke headers reader ke text view me nahi dikhe the — isliye values exactly jaise dikhe waise reproduce ki gayi hain, bina headings invent kiye. Ye kitab ki reading ki ek honest limitation hai.
