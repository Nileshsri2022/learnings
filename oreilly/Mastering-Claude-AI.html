FAQs — 36 sawaal-jawaab

Kitab ke baare me sabse zyada poochhe jaane wale sawaal — har jawaab kitab ke notes se.

Claude kya hai?

Claude Anthropic ka AI assistant hai — transformer architecture par bana ek large language model (LLM), jo vast amounts of text par train hokar human-like responses generate karta hai. Lekin wo duniya ko waise nahi samajhta jaise humans samajhte hain.

Three H’s kya hain?

Claude ki teen defining features: helpfulness (genuinely assist karna), harmlessness (harmful content refuse karna) aur honesty (uncertainty ko acknowledge karna).

Constitutional AI kya hai?

Constitutional AI principles ka ek set hai jo Claude ke responses ko helpful, harmless aur honest hone ki taraf guide karta hai.

Claude ka knowledge cutoff kab tak hai?

January 2025 ke end tak. Uske baad ki events ya information Claude ko apne training data se nahi pata hoti.

Claude 4 Sonnet ka context window kitna bada hai?

Kitab kehti hai 1 million tokens tak — lekin kuch users 100K–200K tokens report karte hain. Ye discrepancy Discrepancies section me report hai.

Token kya hota hai?

Ek token lagbhag ek word ya word ka hissa hota hai. Kitab ki calibration: “Hello” = 1 token, “Hello, how are you?” = 5 tokens.

Achhe prompt ke chaar parts kya hain?

Context (background), specific request, constraints (limitations ya guidelines) aur desired output (format ya length).

Hallucination kya hai?

Jab Claude facts invent kar deta hai. Kitab ka example: Claude ne coffee aur productivity ke baare me ek non-existent study invent kar di — wo plausible lagi, lekin fabricated thi. Isiliye “trust but verify” zaroori hai.

Few-shot prompting kya hai?

Examples dekar Claude ko guide karna. Zero-shot = koi example nahi; one-shot = ek example; few-shot = multiple examples. Kitab ka example: lamp furniture hai, table furniture hai, bookshelf furniture hai — to chair bhi furniture hai.

Chain of thought kya hai?

Claude se step by step reason karne ko kehna — jaise ek maths problem ko steps me todkar solve karna. Chapter kehta hai ki isse complex tasks par accuracy behtar hoti hai.

Meta-prompting kya hai?

Claude se behtar prompts likhne me madad maangna — use apna prompt critique karne ko kaho ya improvements suggest karne ko kaho. Caveat: AI prompts ko truly optimise nahi kar sakta.

3-2-1 Method kya hai?

3 hours deep work, 2 hours meetings, 1 hour admin. Sarah ne isse 2 hours a day ka deep work reclaim kiya; usne 11:47 AM par email check kiya.

Three-pass editing kya hai?

Pehla pass structure aur clarity ke liye, doosra pass grammar aur style ke liye, teesra pass tone aur audience ke liye.

CRAAP test kya hai?

Sources evaluate karne ka test: Currency (kitni recent?), Relevance (topic se relevant?), Authority (author credible?), Accuracy (sahi hai?), Purpose (kyun banayi gayi?).

Three-source rule kya hai?

Important claims ke liye kam se kam teen independent sources use karo.

Password strength checker kaise score karta hai?

Paanch criteria, har ek 1 point: length ≥ 8, uppercase, lowercase, digit, special character. Score 0–1 Very Weak, 2 Weak, 3 Fair, 4 Strong, 5 Very Strong. Code me getpass.getpass() use hota hai aur KeyboardInterrupt ko try/except se handle kiya jata hai.

Tom ne apni novel kaise likhi?

Wo hafte me 5 din likhta tha, har din 250 words — 50 hafton me 62,500 words, aur weekends par likhne se 70,000+ words. Lekin target 80,000 words tha — ye discrepancy Discrepancies section me hai.

Data analysis ke paanch types kya hain?

Descriptive (kya hua?), diagnostic (kyun hua?), predictive (aage kya hoga?), prescriptive (kya karna chahiye?) aur comparative (comparison kya kehta hai?).

Pie chart me hours ka total kitna hai?

24.2 hours — percentages ka total 100% hai, lekin hours (7.2 + 8.4 + 2 + 2 + 2.9 + 1.7) 24.2 hote hain, 24 nahi. Ye discrepancy D5 hai.

Recursive chain of thought ke paanch levels kya hain?

(1) What is the problem? (2) What are the possible solutions? (3) What are the pros and cons of each solution? (4) What is the best solution? (5) How do I implement the best solution?

Prompt Evolution Engine ke chhah steps kya hain?

(1) Basic prompt se shuru karo; (2) Prompt test karo; (3) Results analyse karo; (4) Prompt refine karo; (5) Dobara test karo; (6) Repeat karo.

Artifacts kya hain?

Persistent, editable documents jo Claude create karta hai — substantial content ke liye. 20 lines se kam ke short answers ke liye artifacts nahi bante. Claude khud decide karta hai kab artifact create karna hai; user ise trigger bhi kar sakta hai.

Projects ki sabse badi limitation kya hai?

Projects me context automatically continue nahi hota — user ko context manually re-share karna padta hai. Projects ek smart folder ki tarah hain — files, documents aur instructions add kar sakte ho.

File processing ki limits kya hain?

20 files per conversation, 30 MB per file. Formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT, RTF, ODT, HTML, EPUB, CSV, JSON, images (lagbhag 8,000 × 8,000 px tak), XLSX Analysis Tool se. Mobile par reduced limits; Pro ko priority.

Integration ke teen levels kya hain?

Level 1 Assistant (simple tasks), Level 2 Partner (complex tasks — sweet spot), Level 3 Amplifier (capabilities amplify karna).

Marcus ki weekly report kitni der me hoti hai?

Pehle 2+ ghante lagte the; ab 45 minutes — 15 minutes data collection, 20 minutes analysis, 10 minutes writing.

Troubleshooting ka systematic diagnostic framework kya hai?

Chaar steps: (1) Error type identify karo; (2) Platform verify karo; (3) Input format validate karo (30 MB per file, 20 files per conversation); (4) Systematically test karo. Flowchart: identify → check basics → test → seek help.

Privacy hierarchy ke teen levels kya hain?

Never share: social security numbers, passwords, financial account details. Think Twice: work emails, health information, bachchon ki details. Generally Safe: public information, general questions.

80/20 rule kya hai?

80% thinking khud karo, 20% Claude se karwao — taaki Claude par dependency na bane aur apni skills bani rahein.

Progressive Adoption ke paanch steps kya hain?

Learn, Test, Evaluate, Adopt, Share. Personal update system: weekly check-in (Friday, 5 minutes) aur monthly deep dive (pehle Monday).

Expertise pyramid ke paanch levels kya hain?

Novice (basic prompts), Developing (prompt frameworks), Competent (advanced strategies), Proficient (workflows and integration), Expert (innovator and teacher). Power-user paradox: kam features ko achhe se use karna behtar hai.

Kitab ke hisaab se 73–78% organisations globally. Ye Chapter 20 ka number hai — aur kitab khud kehti hai ki future predictions uncertain hain.

The Great Convergence kya hai?

AI, human creativity aur business needs ka convergence — ye Chapter 20 ka central idea hai. Bottom line: future hum par happen nahi hota, hum use shape karte hain.
