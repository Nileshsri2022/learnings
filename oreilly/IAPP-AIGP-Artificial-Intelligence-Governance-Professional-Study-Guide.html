Chapter 1 — AI aur AI Governance

AI kya hai, uske types aur techniques kya hain, usse kis-kisko kya nuksaan ho sakta hai, aur governance ki zaroorat kyun padti hai — ye chapter poori kitab ki neev hai. Aath responsible-AI principles yahin introduce hote hain.

AI ke types — capability ke hisaab se

AI ko machines dwara human intelligence ka simulation kaha gaya hai. Capability ke hisaab se chaar tiers:

  • Narrow AI (Weak AI): ek hi kaam ke liye bana — examples: Deep Blue aur LLMs jaise ChatGPT, Claude aur Grok. Lekhak zor dekar kehte hain: aaj ke LLMs narrow AI hain, general intelligence nahi.
  • Artificial General Intelligence (AGI): ek organization manage kar sake, strategic planning kar sake, creative kaam kar sake — koi AGI publicly disclose nahi hua hai.
  • Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI): har domain me human intelligence se aage — exist nahi karta.
  • God-Like AI: lekhak ka additional, chautha tier — ek aisi intelligence jo human comprehension se itni aage ho ki uski reasoning samjhi hi na ja sake.

AI ki functionalities

  • Reactive AI: koi memory nahi (Deep Blue).
  • Limited Memory AI: recent data use karta hai phir discard kar deta hai (autonomous vehicles).
  • Reasoning AI: reason karke conclusions nikaal sakta hai — example: LLMs ne bar examination me above-average human scores se pass kiya.
  • Theory of Mind AI: doosron ke mental states samajh sake — diya gaya example fictional hai (ek mashhoor sci-fi film ka computer).
  • Self-Aware AI: introspective ho, jaise humanoid robot — theoretical.
  • Robotics: kinetic, physical branch (jaise cooking robot).

AI techniques

  • Machine Learning: explicit programming ke bina data se seekhna.
  • Deep Learning: multi-layered neural networks (example: Google Translate).
  • Natural Language Processing: human language samajhna aur generate karna (example: ChatGPT).
  • Supervised Learning: labeled data (example: spam filtering).
  • Unsupervised Learning: unlabeled data me patterns dhoondhna (example: CRM system customers ko apne hisaab se classify kare).
  • Semi-Supervised Learning: chhota labeled set + bada unlabeled set (example: image recognition).
  • Reinforcement Learning: reward aur penalty se seekhna (example: maze navigate karta robot).
  • Expert Systems: knowledge base se rules apply karna (example: medical diagnosis).
  • Zero-Shot Learning: model un tasks ko handle kare jinke liye use specifically train nahi kiya gaya.

AI ke risks aur harms — kis-kisko kya nuksaan

Individuals ko: privacy (RAG se private data leak; retail me facial recognition; training data me usernames/passwords jaisi PII); bias aur discrimination (hiring, lending, financial aid); cybersecurity (neglected least privilege aur segregation of duties; non-anonymized training data); visibility, autonomy aur control ka loss (healthcare diagnosis jise clinician override nahi kar sakta; insurer ka approval decision).

Groups ko: social inequality ka amplification (community ke hisaab se loan rates; social-media profiling); social cohesion ka erosion (echo chambers; sensational content); cybersecurity (critical infrastructure; bank chatbot ko trick karke information disclose karwana).

Organizations ko: operational harm (inventory, fraud detection, customer service); legal aur regulatory harm; financial harm (cost overruns; AI trading losses); cybersecurity (AI intrusion-prevention system ne partner ko ghanton block kiya; model poisoning); supply-chain harm (2020 ka SolarWinds incident cite kiya gaya hai); misalignment (items per worker maximize karne ka instruction — workforce cuts ki taraf le gaya); reputational harm (tone-deaf advertising).

Society ko: job displacement (software engineers, transport workers, customer-service staff; entry-level hiring freeze); economic inequality; autonomy aur agency ka erosion (algorithm se set government policy); ethical aur moral harm (unavoidable accident ke saamne autonomous taxi); cybersecurity aur privacy harm (power-grid system ne data misinterpret kiya aur widespread failures); public opinion ka manipulation (election me ek side ko favour karta social media).

Sidebar — "We've Seen This Mania Before"

Technology hype ka pattern 1980s ke personal computer, 1990s ke Internet aur 2000s ke cloud computing se compare kiya gaya hai — early 2000s ka dot-com crash bhi shaamil. First-mover enthusiasm ko ek recurring risk ke roop me present kiya gaya hai.

Governance ki zaroorat wali 9 characteristics

  • Complexity: healthcare deep-learning diagnosis jise explain nahi kiya ja sakta.
  • Opacity / black box: bank loan rejection jise contest nahi kiya ja sakta kyunki reasoning chhupa hai.
  • Autonomy: swerve karta autonomous vehicle.
  • Speed and Scale: misinformation social media par lakhon tak pahunch jaati hai.
  • Potential Harm and Misuse: authoritarian surveillance; AI weapons.
  • Data Dependency: biased historical arrest data par trained criminal-justice sentencing.
  • Privacy: customers aur data brokers se PII; model ko "untrain" karne ka koi reliable tareeka nahi, isliye data ko ingestion se pehle anonymize karna chahiye.
  • Intellectual Property: ek stock-photo company ka ek image-generation company ke khilaf 2023 ka case, millions of photos par; mid-2025 tak koi US precedent nahi; artistic style khud unprotected hai.
  • Probability vs. Determinism: healthcare systems likelihoods dete hain, aur thresholds ko risk appetite aur tolerance ki tarah set karna hota hai.

Sidebar — "The Global Impact of AI"

Ek science magazine ke hawale se: data centers global electricity use ka lagbhag 1.5% hain, jo 2030 tak double hone ka projection hai — tab AI utni electricity consume karega jitni Japan karta hai. US me, AI data centers 2030 tak electricity usage me increase ka aadha hissa honge.

Responsible AI ke 8 principles

  • Ethics: primary foundation (example: military drones; tools: external ethical audits, impact assessments, stakeholder engagement). Ek "Questions for Further Consideration" box poochta hai: agar koi legal obligations na hon to organization AI ko kaise govern karegi? Kya AI ko kabhi life-or-death decisions lene chahiye? Innovation aur responsibility ko kaise balance karein?
  • Fairness: example — retail hiring tool female candidates ko reject kar raha tha kyunki training data predominantly male employees ka tha. Remedies: diverse representative data, bias audits aur mitigation.
  • Safety and Reliability: fail-safes, interruptability, fault tolerance, continuous monitoring. Example: poor lighting me pedestrian detect na kar paane wala autonomous vehicle.
  • Privacy: data minimization, de-identification. Example: mobile health app.
  • Security: inventory, risk assessment, due care. Example: retail customer-service system data phaila raha ho.
  • Transparency and Explainability: transparency, interpretability aur explainability ko alag-alag define kiya gaya hai; explainable AI (XAI) introduce hota hai. Example: community bank jo loan rejection explain nahi kar sakta.
  • Accountability: example — healthcare diagnostic misdiagnosis arbitration tak jaaye to provider accountable hota hai, manufacturer nahi; deployer ultimately accountable hota hai. Ek sidebar culture of accountability par warn karta hai: AI ya vendor ko blame karna galat hai.
  • Human-Centricity: AI ko logon ko replace nahi, augment karna chahiye; finance, healthcare aur justice me human in the loop. Example: factory workers displace karke community reputation kharab karna.

Chapter 3 — Policies Update karna

Pehle existing policies ka inventory lo, gaps dhoondho, phir nayi policies banao — aur ethics ko design me hi daalo, baad me bolt-on mat karo.

Existing policies update karna

Organizations ko pehle existing policies ka inventory karna chahiye (privacy, security, acceptable use, procurement, HR, records retention) aur assess karna chahiye ki har policy kis AI use case ko touch karti hai, phir gaps identify karo — kitab ka example: ek data-retention policy jo model training data ke baare me kuch nahi kehti.

Policy lifecycle: draft, review, approve, publish, train, monitor, aur revise. Kisi naye AI use case ke aage badhne se pehle use-case assessment aur ek written justification memo recommended gate hai, jisme risk rating attached ho.

Teen concepts jo human oversight ko hataana impossible banate hain: epistemic uncertainty (model kya nahi jaanta, ye na jaanna), automation bias (model par over-trust), aur emergent behavior (capabilities jo sirf scale par dikhti hain).

AI Ethics by Design

  • Fairness toolkits: Aequitas aur AI Fairness 360.
  • Explainability tools: SHAP aur LIME.
  • Privacy measures: data lineage, consent management, synthetic data.
  • Security measures: high-risk deployments ke liye two-person rule aur documentation ke liye model cards.

Model Context Protocol (MCP) ka zikr hai — ek tareeka jisse systems models ko tools aur data expose karte hain. Red teaming, pre-deployment testing aur post-deployment monitoring — teeno ko policy requirements ke roop me present kiya gaya hai, optional extras nahi. Documentation aur incident playbooks required hain, incidents ke baad blameless retrospectives ke saath.

Privacy threats aur mitigations

Threats: model inversion, membership inference, data poisoning, function creep, aur memorization. 2021 ki finding cite ki gayi hai: GPT-style models ko prompt karke training data se Social Security numbers aur email addresses reproduce karwaye gaye the.

Mitigations: k-anonymity, l-diversity, aur differential privacy. Chapter ek "forgetting budget" introduce karta hai — ek limit ki organization model ko data bhulane ki koshish par kitna kharch karegi — aur AI context me right to explanation aur right to be forgotten par discuss karta hai.

Security threats aur mitigations

Threats: adversarial attacks, data poisoning, model theft, aur prompt injection. Mitigations: software bill of materials (SBOM) aur third-party risk management tiers.

AI vendor contracts ke liye clauses list ki gayi hain: data use, audit rights, incident notification, model-change notification. AI-SBOM concept introduce hota hai: ek AI system ka bill of materials jo models, datasets aur dependencies list karta hai.

Sidebar — "Tragedy of the Digital Commons"

Ek 2022 ke lekh ke hawale se: open-source AI models ek shared resource hain jo misuse se degrade ho sakte hain, isliye unhe use karne wali organizations par stewardship responsibility hai — data ke peeche ke annotators aur data workers ke prati bhi.

Chapter 4 — Privacy aur Data Protection Law

Privacy law AI governance ki foundation hai, kyunki AI systems data-hungry hote hain. Notice, consent, purpose limitation, minimization — ye chaaro model training data ko constrain karte hain.

Privacy ki bhoomika — notice, consent, purpose, minimization

Notice and consent: organizations ko batana hota hai kya data collect hota hai aur kyun, aur jahan required ho wahan consent lena hota hai. Consent freely given, specific, informed aur unambiguous hona chahiye, aur jitni aasaani se diya jaaye utni hi aasaani se withdraw bhi ho sake.

Purpose limitation: ek purpose ke liye collect kiya data kisi incompatible purpose ke liye reuse nahi kiya ja sakta — model training ke liye data repurpose karne par ek key constraint.

Data minimization aur Privacy by Design: sirf wahi collect karo jo needed ho. Privacy by Design ko saat siddhanton ke through present kiya gaya hai: proactive not reactive; privacy as the default; privacy embedded into design; full functionality — positive-sum, not zero-sum; end-to-end security; visibility aur transparency; respect for user privacy.

Data Controller ki bhoomika

Data controller processing ke purposes aur means decide karta hai aur primary obligations bear karta hai. Table 4.1 ("Data Controller Responsibilities") list karti hai: lawfulness of processing, transparency, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity and confidentiality, aur accountability.

DPIAs: jahan processing se high risk likely ho (GDPR Article 35), wahan required. Chapter DPIA steps walkthrough karta hai: processing describe karo, necessity aur proportionality assess karo, risks identify karo, mitigations identify karo, DPO se consult karo, aur review karo.

Data protection by design aur by default (Article 25): privacy settings aur data minimization shuru se hi built-in honi chahiye.

DPO: public authorities ke liye aur large-scale systematic monitoring ya large-scale processing of special categories ke liye required. DPO independent hona chahiye, highest management level ko report kare, aur apne tasks perform karne par dismiss ya penalize na kiya jaaye.

Processor relationships: processors sirf documented instructions par act karte hain, GDPR Article 28 requirements ko meet karne wale contract ke under, aur controller ki assist karte hain aur contract ke end par data delete ya return karte hain.

Cross-border transfers: EEA ke bahar transfers ke liye adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, binding corporate rules), ya derogation chahiye. Schrems II decision (2020) ne Privacy Shield ko invalid kar diya tha; EU–US Data Privacy Framework ko successor arrangement ke roop me present kiya gaya hai.

Data subject rights

Rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, aur solely automated decisions ke subject na hone ka right jinka legal ya similarly significant effect ho (GDPR Article 22), profiling sahit.

Sidebar — "The Right to Be Forgotten": erasure (Article 17) aur uski limits — khaas taur par, trained model se erasure technically difficult hai, jo Chapter 3 ke memorization discussion se link karta hai.

Table 4.2 ("Data Subject Rights and AI") har right ko uske AI implication se map karti hai — jaise access ke liye explain karna hota hai ki kis data ne model ko train kiya; objection ke liye us vyakti ke data par trained model ka use rokna pad sakta hai.

Data breach notification

Controller ko personal data breach ke baare me aware hone ke 72 hours ke andar supervisory authority ko notify karna hota hai (GDPR Article 33), aur high risk ho to affected individuals ko bina undue delay notify karna hota hai. Notification failures par fines €10 million ya global annual turnover ka 2% tak hain. DPO sidebar DPO ke duties aur independence ko dobara batata hai.

Sensitive data aur special categories

GDPR Article 9 special categories: race aur ethnic origin, political opinions, religious ya philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic data, biometric data (person identify karne ke liye use), health data, aur sex life ya sexual orientation se related data. Processing prohibited hai jab tak Article 9 exception apply na ho (explicit consent usual exception hai).

Children's data: har member state ke set kiye age (13–16) se neeche parental consent chahiye. Biometric data define kiya gaya hai aur identification use ko high-risk highlight kiya gaya hai.

Chapter 6 — EU AI Act

Duniya ka pehla comprehensive AI kanoon — risk-based approach, extraterritorial scope, aur €35 million tak ke fines. Ye chapter exam ke liye sabse zyada weight rakhta hai.

EU AI Act — basic baatein

EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) 2024 me adopt hua — risk-based approach aur extraterritorial scope ke saath: ye un providers aur deployers par bhi lagta hai jinki AI output EU me use hoti hai, chahe wo kahin aur based hon. Table 6.1 ("Risk Classifications") chaar tiers deti hai.

Risk classification — chaar tiers

  • Unacceptable risk — prohibited: social scoring; public spaces me real-time remote biometric identification (narrow law-enforcement exceptions ke saath); manipulative ya deceptive AI; vulnerabilities ka exploitation; profiling par based predictive policing; facial images ki untargeted scraping; workplaces aur schools me emotion inference.
  • High risk: recruitment, education, credit scoring, law enforcement, migration, regulated products ke safety components, plus Annex III use cases. Requirements: risk management, data governance, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity — aur market par place karne se pehle conformity assessment.
  • Limited risk — transparency duties: users ko batana hota hai ki wo AI se interact kar rahe hain, aur AI-generated content (deepfakes sahit) ko label karna hota hai. Isi chapter ka "Dark Patterns" sidebar warn karta hai un designs ke khilaf jo is disclosure ko obscure karte hain. (Dhyaan do — gadbad #8: sidebar kehta hai dark patterns "Chapter 4 me discuss" hue, jabki asal me wo Chapter 5 me hain.)
  • Minimal risk: spam filters, video games, inventory management — existing law ke alawa koi additional obligations nahi.

Classification requirements

Organizations ko har system classify karna hota hai, classification document karni hoti hai, aur system ka purpose badle to re-classify karna hota hai.

General Purpose AI (GPAI)

GPAI models (jaise LLMs) ki apni duties hain — technical documentation, copyright-policy compliance, aur training content ka summary. Data governance

Data quality ko dimensions se define kiya gaya hai: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness, aur validity. Poor quality model me propagate hoti hai.

Data drift time ke saath data me change hai jo model ko degrade karta hai. Data stewardship ownership assign karta hai — data owner accountable hota hai, data steward day-to-day quality manage karta hai. Maturity ad hoc se defined aur managed hokar optimized tak jaati hai.

  • Data Augmentation: transformations se dataset expand karna.
  • Synthetic Data: real data ki jagah use ho sakta hai, lekin representativeness ke liye validate karna hota hai aur ye khud real personal data leak nahi karna chahiye.

Model training

Training data model ko sikhata hai, validation data use tune karta hai, aur test data use evaluate karta hai — teeno ko alag rakhna hota hai. Hyperparameter tuning un settings ko adjust karta hai jo learning ko control karte hain. Model selection validation set par candidate models ko compare karke wo choose karta hai jo requirements ko sabse achha meet kare — sirf sabse accurate wala nahi.

Testing aur evaluation — 8 types

  • Unit testing: individual components.
  • Integration testing: components ek saath.
  • Validation testing: kya system apni requirements meet karta hai.
  • Performance testing: load ke under speed, throughput aur resource use.
  • Security testing: adversarial inputs, poisoning, extraction.
  • Bias testing: demographic group ke hisaab se disaggregated performance.
  • Explainability testing: kya system apne outputs explain kar sakta hai.
  • Human-in-the-loop testing: kya human overseer asal me errors pakadta hai.

Testing risks aur mitigations tabulate kiye gaye hain: test data ka training data me leak (mitigation: strict separation); sirf clean data par test karna (mitigation: adversarial aur edge cases par test karo); jis team ne model banaya wahi test kare (mitigation: independent testing); retraining ke baad koi regression testing nahi (mitigation: automated regression suites).

Q1AIGP exam ka format kya hai — kitne sawal, kitna time, pass score kya hai?

Q2AIGP exam ke chaar domains aur unke weights kya hain?
Domain I — Responsible AI Principles and Foundations (23%), Domain II — Laws, Regulations, Standards and Frameworks (20%), Domain III — Developing, Testing and Evaluating AI Systems (29%), Domain IV — Managing AI Risks and Ongoing Operations (28%). Domain III sabse bada hai.

    
Q3Narrow AI, AGI aur ASI me kya farak hai?
Narrow (Weak) AI ek hi task ke liye banta hai — jaise Deep Blue ya aaj ke LLMs; kitab zor deti hai ki LLMs narrow AI hain, general intelligence nahi. AGI kisi bhi intellectual task ko human jaisa kar sake — koi AGI publicly disclose nahi hua. ASI har domain me human se aage hoga — exist nahi karta. Lekhak ek chautha tier bhi add karte hain: God-Like AI, jo comprehension se pare ho.

    
Q4Responsible AI ke aath principles kaunse hain?
Ethics, Fairness, Safety and Reliability, Privacy, Security, Transparency and Explainability, Accountability, aur Human-Centricity. Ethics primary foundation hai; accountability me deployer ultimately accountable hota hai — AI ya vendor ko blame karna galat hai.

    
Q5AI governance ki zaroorat wali 9 characteristics kya hain?
Complexity, Opacity (black box), Autonomy, Speed and Scale, Potential Harm and Misuse, Data Dependency, Privacy, Intellectual Property, aur Probability vs. Determinism. Example: opacity — bank loan rejection jise contest nahi kiya ja sakta kyunki reasoning chhupa hai.

    
Q6Bloom's Taxonomy ke levels kya hain aur exam kis par focus karta hai?
Neeche se upar: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, Create. Exam neeche wale chaar — Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze — par focus karta hai, khaas taur par apply aur analyze par. Matlab sawal poochta hai ki kya system high-risk hai, sirf definition nahi.

    
Q7Developer, deployer aur user me kya farak hai?
Developer system banata hai, deployer use me laata hai, user usse affected hota hai. Ek hi organization teeno ho sakti hai. Ye EU AI Act ke "provider" aur "deployer" se alag distinction hai — provider par sabse bhaari duties hoti hain.

    
Q8RACI chart me Responsible aur Accountable me kya farak hai?
Responsible wo hai jo kaam karta hai, Accountable wo hai jo uske liye jawabdeh hai. Example: post-deployment monitoring me Development Responsible hai aur Risk Accountable; regulatory disclosure me Legal Responsible hai.

    
Q9COBIT maturity scale ke levels kya hain?
0 Non-existent, 1 Initial/Ad hoc, 2 Repeatable but intuitive, 3 Defined process, 4 Managed and measurable, 5 Optimized. Warning: low maturity score necessarily bura nahi hai, aur kam hi organizations ko har process ke liye level 5 aspire karna chahiye.

    
Q10Policy lifecycle ke steps kya hain?
Draft, review, approve, publish, train, monitor, aur revise. Naye AI use case se pehle use-case assessment aur written justification memo (risk rating ke saath) recommended gate hai.

    
Q11Model inversion, membership inference aur memorization kya hain?
Ye privacy threats hain: model inversion model se training data ke baare me infer karna, membership inference ye pata lagana ki koi data training me tha ya nahi, aur memorization — model training data ko yaad karke reproduce kar de. 2021 me GPT-style models se Social Security numbers aur emails reproduce karwaye gaye the. Mitigations: k-anonymity, l-diversity, differential privacy.

    
Q12GDPR me consent ki kya requirements hain?
Consent freely given, specific, informed aur unambiguous hona chahiye, aur jitni aasaani se diya jaaye utni hi aasaani se withdraw bhi ho sake. Purpose limitation kehta hai ek purpose ka data incompatible purpose ke liye reuse nahi ho sakta — model training par key constraint.

    
Q13DPIA kab required hota hai aur uske steps kya hain?
Jahan processing se high risk likely ho (GDPR Article 35). Steps: processing describe karo, necessity aur proportionality assess karo, risks identify karo, mitigations identify karo, DPO se consult karo, aur review karo.

    
Q14Data breach notification ki deadline aur fine kya hai?
Controller ko breach ke baare me aware hone ke 72 hours ke andar supervisory authority ko notify karna hota hai (Article 33), aur high risk ho to affected individuals ko bina undue delay. Notification failures par fine €10 million ya 2% global annual turnover tak.

    
Q15GDPR Article 9 special categories me kya-kya aata hai?
Race aur ethnic origin, political opinions, religious ya philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic data, biometric data (identification ke liye), health data, aur sex life ya sexual orientation se related data. Processing prohibited hai jab tak Article 9 exception (jaise explicit consent) apply na ho.

    
Q16GDPR Article 22 kya kehta hai?
Har vyakti ko solely automated decisions ke subject na hone ka right hai jinka legal ya similarly significant effect ho — profiling sahit. Ye AI credit aur hiring decisions ke liye seedha relevant hai.

    
Q17Fair use ke chaar factors kya hain?
Purpose aur character of the use, nature of the work, amount used, aur effect on the market. Transformative use par zor diya jaata hai. AI training par fair use abhi disputed area hai — kai bade cases chal rahe hain.

    
Q18AI author ya inventor ho sakta hai kya?
Current US law ke under nahi. Copyright ke liye author human hona chahiye, aur DABUS cases me conclusion yehi raha ki AI inventor nahi ho sakta. AI-generated output copyrightable nahi ho sakta, aur use karne se infringement risk ho sakta hai.

    
Q19Disparate impact aur disparate treatment me kya farak hai?
Disparate treatment intentional discrimination hai; disparate impact facially neutral practice hai jiska discriminatory effect ho — intent prove karne ki zaroorat nahi. Proxy variables (jaise postal code race ke liye) disparate impact ka classic tareeka hain.

    
Q20Proxy variables kya hote hain? Examples do.
Aise variables jo protected characteristic ke liye khade ho jaayein: postal code → race; name → national origin; graduation year → age; gaps in employment → disability ya caregiving. Model inhe use karke indirectly discriminate kar sakta hai.

    
Q21Dark patterns kya hain aur wo kahan discuss hain?
Interface designs jo users ko aise choices ki taraf manipulate karte hain jo wo warna nahi karte. Kitab me wo Chapter 5 (consumer protection) me discuss hain — halanki Chapter 6 ka sidebar galti se Chapter 4 refer karta hai (gadbad #8).

    
Q22EU AI Act ke chaar risk tiers kya hain?
Unacceptable (prohibited — jaise social scoring), High (conformity assessment + saari duties — jaise recruitment, credit scoring), Limited (transparency duties — users ko batana hoga wo AI se interact kar rahe hain), Minimal (koi extra obligation nahi — jaise spam filters).

    
Q23EU AI Act me fines kitne hain?
Prohibited practices: €35 million ya 7% global annual turnover (jo zyada ho). Most other violations: €15 million ya 3%. Authorities ko false/misleading information: €7.5 million ya 1%.

    
Q24GPAI aur systemic-risk GPAI kya hai? 10^25 FLOPs ka kya matlab hai?
GPAI models (jaise LLMs) ki duties hain: technical documentation, copyright-policy compliance, training content ka summary. Systemic-risk GPAI — training compute 10^25 FLOPs se zyada — par extra duties hain: model evaluation, risk assessment, incident reporting, cybersecurity. Kitab kehti hai 10^25 figure illustrative hai, hard legal limit nahi.

    
Q25EU AI Act ki implementation timeline kya hai?
12 July 2024 published; 1 Aug 2024 entry into force; 2 Feb 2025 prohibited practices aur AI literacy; 2 Aug 2025 GPAI, governance aur penalties; 2 Aug 2026 high-risk obligations; 2 Aug 2027 purane GPAI models ke comply karne ki deadline; 2027 tak full enforcement.

    
Q26NIST AI RMF ke chaar functions kya hain?
Govern (centre — culture aur structures), Map (context aur risks identify karo), Measure (assess aur track karo), Manage (prioritize karke action lo). Ye January 2023 ka voluntary framework hai; RMF Playbook har function ke liye suggested actions deta hai.

    
Q27ISO 42001 aur ISO 22989 me kya farak hai?
ISO/IEC 42001 (2023) pehla certifiable AI management-system standard hai — Annex SL structure ko mirror karta hai. ISO/IEC 22989 AI ki terminology aur concepts deta hai — shared vocabulary (AI, AI System, Machine Learning, Autonomy, Transparency, Explainability, Trustworthiness).

    
Q28Model cards aur datasheets kya hote hain?
Model cards model ka intended use, performance, limitations aur evaluation describe karte hain. Datasheets for datasets dataset ki provenance, composition aur intended use describe karte hain. Dono lifecycle documentation ke standard artifacts hain.

    
Q29Risk matrix kaise kaam karti hai?
5×5 matrix: likelihood (Rare, Unlikely, Possible, Likely, Almost Certain) × impact (Negligible, Minor, Moderate, Major, Severe) — ratings Low se Extreme tak. Mitigations har risk se map ki jaati hain aur deployment ke baad monitor karni hoti hain.

    
Q30Data provenance aur data lineage me kya farak hai?
Ek hi chain do directions me: provenance dataset se peeche uski origins tak trace karta hai (ye dataset kahan se aaya?); lineage original source se aage later datasets tak follow karta hai (ye data kahan-kahan gaya?). Dono consent, licensing aur bias ke sawalon ke liye chahiye.

    
Q31Training, validation aur test data alag kyun rakhne chahiye?
Training data model ko sikhata hai, validation data use tune karta hai, test data use evaluate karta hai. Agar test data training me leak ho jaaye to evaluation bekaar ho jaati hai — mitigation strict separation hai. Retraining ke baad automated regression suites chahiye.

    
Q32As-is, fine-tuning aur RAG me se kab kya choose karein?
As-is sabse fast, sabse kam control. Fine-tuning better fit deta hai lekin catastrophic forgetting aur training-data leakage ka risk hai. RAG answers ko organization ke apne documents me ground karta hai aur hallucination kam karta hai, lekin retrieval corpus ki quality par depend karta hai.

    
Q33Drift detection ke liye kaunse methods hain?
Kolmogorov–Smirnov test aur Population Stability Index — statistical drift measures. Shadow models bhi use hote hain: challenger model production model ke saath-saath chalakar outputs compare karta hai. Drift business risk bhi hai — pandemic ke dauran ek chatbot isiliye fail hua tha.

    
Q34STRIDE kya hai aur MITRE ATLAS kya hai?
STRIDE threat modeling method hai: Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of service, Elevation of privilege. MITRE ATLAS AI-specific threat framework hai. Dono Chapter 11 me assurance ke tools ke roop me aate hain — red teaming aur penetration testing ke saath.

    
Q35Incident response ke 9 steps aur severity levels kya hain?
Steps: detect, triage, contain, investigate, remediate, communicate, recover, document, learn. Severity: Level 1 minor (no harm), Level 2 moderate (limited harm), Level 3 major (significant harm), Level 4 critical (widespread ya severe harm). Disclosures timely, accurate aur legal counsel ke saath coordinate hokar honi chahiye.

    
Q36System retirement aur data residency me kya dhyaan rakhna hai?
Retirement deliberately honi chahiye: user notification, data migration ya deletion, contract termination, rollback option — silent abandonment governance failure hai. Records typically 3–7 saal retain karte hain; models ki disposal me model, training data aur backups delete karke confirm karna hota hai. Data residency ke kaaran data ko kisi particular country ya region me rakhna pad sakta hai, aur systems ko local language, culture aur law ke hisaab se localize karna hota hai.
