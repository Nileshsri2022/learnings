Ch 1 · From Generalist to Specialist

Hosted frontier models kiraye ki intelligence hain — har request par provider ko paise dete hain, aur model ek generalist hai. API ki deewar ka matlab hai: aap uske weights badal nahi sakte, sirf prompt badal sakte hain. Is chapter me wo saare "tax" ginaye gaye hain jo kiraye par jeene par lagte hain.

From Generalist to Specialist

Ek generalist model thoda-thoda sab kuch jaanta hai. Ek specialist — fine-tuned chhota model — ek hi domain me gehra jaanta hai. Yahi chapter ka sawal hai: aapko generalist kiraye par chahiye, ya specialist apna chahiye?

The Cost of Renting Intelligence

Kiraye ki keemat sirf per-request price nahi hai — wo aggregate hoti hai, aur wo aapke control se bahar badhti hai. Neeche Table 1-1 ke illustrative prices dekhiye.

Matlab product loss-making ban jata hai. Per-request cost chhoti lagti hai; aggregate hi asli tax hai.

The Agentic Multiplier

Agents — models jo plan karte hain, tools istemal karte hain aur action lete hain — token use ko 5–10x multiply kar dete hain per task. 2024 me 1% se bhi kam enterprises ke paas agents production me the; ek industry projection ke mutabik 2026 ke end tak 40% se zyada honge — 40x jump.

Ek five-step agent ka example: user okonomiyaki restaurant ki recommendation maangta hai apne aas-paas. Agent ko (1) samajhna, (2) plan karna, (3) execute karna — search, Maps, browser kholna, (4) reflect karna, (5) present karna padta hai — har step ek aur model call hai.

The Instruction Tax

Instructions system prompt me rehti hain aur har request par dobara bheji aur dobara charge ki jati hain. Lambe prompts performance bhi gira dete hain. Fine-tuning instructions ko weights me daal deta hai — ek baar seekh lo, inference par cost zero.

Context Windows and the "Lost in the Middle" Problem

Lamba context bharne se model beech ki cheez bhool jata hai. Jo relevant fact prompt ke beech me daba hai, wahi sabse zyada miss hone ki possibility hai. Context bada hona hi kaafi nahi — model ko use karna bhi aana chahiye.

The Latency Killer

Time to first token (TTFT) 500 ms se kai seconds tak hota hai, aur tokens-per-second bhi matter karta hai. Chatbot 500 ms aur 50 tokens/second tolerate kar leta hai, lekin autocomplete, voice aur real-time tools ko isse bahut kam latency chahiye. Bade shared models terabytes ke weights move karte hain aur requests queue me khadi hoti hain.

The Data Firewall

Ek hospital vision-language-model startup ki kahani: deal usi waqt mar gayi jab CISO ne ward ki images hosted giant model ko bhejne se mana kar diya. Apne hardware par data rakhna ek firewall hai — provenance, logs aur clean retrain karne ki ability, sab aapke control me.

Compliance Issues

GDPR ka right to erasure kisi third party ke model par effectively honour karna impossible hai. Apne model par aap data delete karke retrain kar sakte hain.

The Metadata Leak

Documents bheje bina bhi queries leak karti hain. Example: ek company merger target par research kar rahi hai — queries ka pattern hi competitors ko tip-off de deta hai, aur ye insider trading jaisa bhi ho sakta hai.

Model Drift

Providers hosted models ko constantly update karte hain — chup-chaap, aapki carefully tuned prompts raat bhar me toot jati hain. Owned model ek static, versioned artifact hai jo aapke control me hai.

The Specialist Revolution

Scaling laws bade models ke favour me hain, lekin LLaMA (early 2023) ne dikhaya ki chhote, well-engineered open models apne weight se bahut upar punch karte hain. Open licences + self-hosting ne specialization ko practical bana diya.

Hardware Is No Longer the Blocker

Pehle ek conference ki kahani: Wales me ek researcher ke paas 10 researchers ek GPU share kar rahe the, Tuesday ka slot milta tha. Aaj ek MacBook Pro unified memory ke saath fine-tune kar sakta hai aur 70B model RAM me load kar sakta hai — lab in a backpack.

The Spectrum of Customization

Customization ke teen rung hain: prompts (soft-prompting samet), RAG (retrieval-augmented generation), aur fine-tuning. Kitab sirf fine-tuning cover karti hai — sabse mushkil, sabse valuable rung.

Summary

Kiraye ki intelligence par token tax, instruction tax, latency, data firewall, compliance, metadata leak aur model drift — saat tarah ke tax lagte hain. Specialist banana hi in sab ka jawab hai. Agla chapter batata hai ki models andar se kaise kaam karte hain.

03Chapter 2

Ch 2 · Model Architectures: The DNA of AI

Fine-tune karne se pehle samajhna hoga ki model andar se kya hai. Ye chapter transformer ka flow, attention, tokenization, positional embeddings aur Gemma/Mistral jaise model families ko kholta hai — kyunki LoRA adapters aage jaakar inhi jagahon par lagte hain.

The Flow of Information: How Transformers Work

Text tokenize hota hai (byte-pair encoding, ASCII/Unicode), tokens embedding vectors ban jate hain — direction se meaning encode hoti hai — plus positional encoding judti hai. Phir sequence transformer layers se guzarta hai aur final layer next token predict karta hai. Example: "The man bit the dog" vs "The dog bit the man" — order badalte hi meaning poori tarah badal jata hai.

Attention Mechanisms

Attention ek highlighter jaisa hai — model seekhta hai kis par dhyaan dena hai. Cat/mat wale example me pronoun "it" (position 8) ka reference attention se resolve hota hai. Teen roles hain: Query = main kya dhoondh raha hoon; Key = mere paas kya hai; Value = jo information main carry karta hoon. Formula: softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) V. Training me next-token cross-entropy loss, backpropagation aur gradient descent istemal hota hai, Adam/AdamW optimizers ke saath.

Multihead Attention

Llama 3 8B me 32 heads, 32 layers hain; Gemma E4B me 8 heads, 42 layers, key/value sharing ke saath. 4,096-dimensional hidden state 32 heads me 128 dimensions ke hisse me bat jata hai, phir output projection se wapas mix hota hai. Note: specialization layers ke across hoti hai, heads ke across nahi.

Self-attention versus cross-attention

Self-attention ek hi sequence ke andar hoti hai. Cross-attention me ek sequence dusri sequence par attend karta hai — encoder-decoder models aur multimodal models me istemal hoti hai.

The Feed-Forward Network

FFN 4,096 dimensions ko 14,336 tak expand karta hai, non-linearity lagata hai (SiLU/SwiGLU — sirf linear layers ka stack ek hi matrix BA me collapse ho jata), phir wapas 4,096 par project karta hai. Ye ek key-value memory jaisa kaam karta hai: "Nagasaki" se "okonomiyaki" retrieve ho sakta hai. LoRA adapters yahin inject hote hain.

Layer Normalization and Residual Connections

LayerNorm/RMSNorm + residual (skip) connections gehri stacks me exploding/vanishing gradients ko rokte hain. Yehi wajah hai ki 40+ layers train ho pati hain.

Tokenization

Subword tokenization rare words, non-English spacing, typos aur variants handle karta hai. Llama 2 vocab 32k, Llama 3 vocab 128k. Chinese "你好世界" English tokenizer me 4 tokens banta hai, lekin multilingual tokenizer me sirf 2 ("你好", "世界"). Code ke liye alag tokenizers bhi hote hain.

Byte Pair Encoding

BPE characters se shuru karta hai aur baar-baar sabse frequent pair ko merge karta hai. "low", "lower", "lowest" — "lo" merge hota hai, phir "low" banta hai. Yahi tareeka vocab banata hai.

Understanding Positional Embeddings with RoPE

RoPE (rotary position embeddings) me embedding ko theta, 2theta, 3theta… se rotate kiya jata hai sequence ke saath. Dot-product similarity phir relative distance encode karti hai, aur ye longer sequences par generalize karta hai.

Embeddings

Llama 3 8B ki embedding dimension 4,096 hai. Kitab me Pride and Prejudice ka 2-D map banaya gaya hai (gender vs nobility, wealth point size se): Mr Darcy wealthy lekin nobility unclear ("Mister"); Sir William Lucas kam wealthy, zyada noble; Mr Bennet noble nahi aur financially struggling; Elizabeth apne father jaisi, female; Lady Catherine noble aur incredibly wealthy. Matlab — embedding space me direction aur distance dono meaning rakhte hain.

Decoder-Only Dominance

Encoder-decoder ko "the cat sat on the mat" ko French aur Gaelic me translate karke dikhaya gaya hai (Gaelic verb-first hai, to word order shift hota hai). Decoder-only model me causal attention mask hota hai (aage dekhna mana hai), unified input/output hota hai, aur in-context learning, chain-of-thought aur instruction following milti hai. Har position ek training signal hoti hai.

Grouped Query Attention

Llama v1 (Feb 2023) ka context 2,048 tokens tha; v2 me 4,096 + GQA; Llama 3 me small models ke liye 8,192 aur large ke liye 128k, 128k vocabulary ke saath (Llama 2 se 4x). KV cache hi bottleneck hai — ek key/value head ko kai query heads share karein to cache chhota hota hai. 6 heads share karein to 50% reduction; Llama 3 8B 32 query heads ko 8 groups me baant ta hai (4 per group) — 75% reduction. Trade-off: shared view nuance kho deta hai.

Sliding Window Attention

Mistral 7B (Sept 2023): har token sirf pichle 4,096 tokens par attend karta hai. Attention cost quadratic hai — 2 tokens ko 4 lookups, 10 ko 100, 1,000 ko 1,000,000. Lekin multi-layer pyramid me receptive field depth ke saath linearly badhta hai.

Mixture of Experts and Sparseness

Mixtral 8x7B: 46.7B total parameters, router har token ke liye top 2 experts chunta hai, to per token sirf 12.9B parameters active hote hain. Experts identical shuru hote hain aur training me diverge karte hain. MoE ko fine-tune karna mushkil hai aur zyada VRAM chahiye.

Gemma: Google's Open Approach

Gemma 4 (2B–31B): "-it" = instruction-tuned, "-pt" = pretrained. Hybrid attention sliding-window aur global layers ko alternate karta hai, final layers global hote hain. Local window E2B/E4B ke liye 512 tokens, 12/26/31B models ke liye 1,024 tokens hai. Bade models K ko V ki tarah reuse karte hain. Cross-layer KV reuse: E2B 35 me se 20 layers se reuse karta hai, E4B 42 me se 18 se; global layers me 8 query heads tak ek KV head share karte hain. RoPE variant; context small models ke liye 128k, large ke liye 256k.

Mistral and Mixtral

Mistral 7B ne sliding window attention popular ki; Mixtral 8x7B ne sparse MoE ko mainstream banaya. Dono ka design philosophy ek hi hai: compute ko wahi lagao jahan zaroorat hai.

Considerations for Choosing a Model

Model chunne ke criteria: instruction following, reasoning depth (model ka "khud se baat karna", chain-of-thought prompting), context windows aur "lost in the middle" problem, aur aise benchmarks se bachna jo bade models ke liye banaye gaye hain. Kitab Gemma 4 family par standardize karti hai.

Instruction Following

Instruction-tuned ("-it") models instructions follow karne me pretrained ("-pt") models se behtar hote hain — kyunki unhe instruction-response pairs par train kiya gaya hota hai. Fine-tuning ke liye base chunne me ye farak matter karta hai.

Reasoning Depth

Reasoning depth matlab model ka chain-of-thought se "sochna" — jawab dene se pehle steps me reason karna. Ye capability model size aur training dono par depend karti hai.

Context Windows and the "Lost in the Middle" Problem

Bada context window hona kaafi nahi — Ch 1 ki tarah, beech ka fact miss hone ka risk rehta hai. Model chunne me context length ke saath-saath retrieval quality bhi dekhni chahiye.

Summary

Transformer ka flow, attention ka Q/K/V, FFN ka key-value memory, GQA/sliding-window/MoE ki efficiency tricks, aur Gemma 4 ki hybrid design — ye sab aage ke chapters me kaam aayega, kyunki LoRA adapters inhi structures ke andar lagte hain.

04Chapter 3

Ch 3 · The Fine-Tuner's Workshop

Training se pehle workshop taiyaar karni hoti hai: GPU, VRAM, OS aur software stack. Is chapter ka ek hi mantra hai — "VRAM: One Number to Rule Them All". VRAM hi decide karta hai ki aap kya train kar sakte hain aur kya nahi.

Why Setup Matters

Galat setup par training shuru karna matlab ghanton baad out-of-memory error dekhna. Setup pehle, training baad me — yahi discipline hai.

The GPU: Your First Decision

GPU pehla decision hai kyunki baaki sab uske baad aata hai. CPU ke paas kuch powerful sequential cores hote hain; GPU ke paas matrix multiplication ke liye hazaron parallel cores hote hain. A100 me lagbhag 7,000 cores, H100 me 15,000 se zyada.

Understanding GPUs

GPU ko samajhne ke liye teen cheezein dekhni hain: cores (parallel compute), VRAM (kitna model + optimizer fit hoga), aur bandwidth (data kitni tezi se move hota hai). VRAM hard constraint hai — system RAM me swap karna slow hai aur crash ka risk hai; multiple GPUs par distribute karna ek bade GPU se sasta padta hai.

Consumer GPUs

RTX 4090: 24 GB, MSRP lagbhag $1,400 (second-hand zyada mehenga). RTX 3090: 24 GB, discounted mil jata hai. AMD RX 7900 XTX (24 GB) ROCm ke through chalta hai, lekin ecosystem kam mature hai — Flash Attention aur bitsandbytes NVIDIA-first hain. Mac M-series unified memory: M4 Max 128 GB nominally A100 ki VRAM se zyada hai, lekin throughput 4–6x slow hai, bitsandbytes unsupported hai, QLoRA nahi hai, MPS incomplete hai aur MLX native route hai.

Data center GPUs

A100 40/80 GB, 80 GB card par 2 TB/s bandwidth — LoRA for 70B ya full fine-tuning for 7B. H100 faster, FP8 ke saath, premium price par. L4 (24 GB) aur L40S (48 GB) mid-tier hain. Colab ki figure 3-1 me H100/A100/L4/T4 list hain; figure 3-2 me T4 High-RAM instance: 15.0 GB VRAM, 1.27 compute units/hour, 100 units $10 — lagbhag 13 cents/hour.

Cloud and Pay-As-You-Go

Kaggle: 30 hours/week free T4/P100, datasets integrated. Lambda Labs: SSH, full Linux, A100/H100. RunPod: Community/Secure tiers, preemptible spot, preinstalled stacks. Vast.ai: individual owners ka marketplace, reliability vary karti hai. DigitalOcean: AMD ROCm.

Distributed training with multiple GPUs

Data parallelism: har GPU par model ki copy, batch split, gradients averaged — model har GPU par fit hona chahiye. Model/tensor/pipeline parallelism: model ya layers split karte hain — communication overhead hota hai, lekin bade models train karne ka yahi ek tareeka hai. ZeRO aur Accelerate (accelerate config interactive setup, accelerate launch train.py) ye sab manage karte hain.

VRAM: One Number to Rule Them All

Training vs inference memory — 7B model FP16 me: weights 14 GB + gradients 14 GB + Adam optimizer state (mean + variance, FP32) 56 GB = lagbhag 84–86 GB total, ~2 GB activations ke saath. Consumer GPU ye nahi kar sakta.

Precision: FP32 4 bytes/parameter (7B ke liye 28 GB), FP16/BF16 2 bytes (14 GB), INT8 1 byte (7 GB), INT4 0.5 bytes (3.5 GB). Modern NVIDIA hardware par BF16 preferred hai; FP8 emerging hai.

LoRA (lagbhag 1% trainable): weights 14 GB + adapter 0.14 GB + gradients 0.14 GB + optimizer (FP32) 0.56 GB + activations 2 GB = lagbhag 17 GB — RTX 4090 (24 GB) me fit, A100 ke $14,000 ke mukable lagbhag dasvein hisse ki keemat par. QLoRA (4-bit weights, 3.5 GB) total lagbhag 7 GB — 8 GB GPU ($100–200) ya free Colab T4 (16 GB) me fit.

Choosing Your Operating System

Linux (Ubuntu 26.04 LTS) default hai — CUDA first-class hai. Windows WSL2 ke through (real Linux kernel, CUDA passthrough, alag drivers nahi chahiye). macOS MPS/MLX ke through, abhi bhi evolving hai.

Using Linux — server aur Colab ka standard. Using Windows — WSL2 se Linux ka poora fayda, Windows ki convenience ke saath. Using macOS — M-series unified memory ka fayda, lekin training stack abhi peeche hai.

Your Programming Environment

Python hi ekmatra language hai. Venv vs Conda: Conda CUDA versions manage karta hai (11.8 vs 12.1 etc.); venv lighter hai agar CUDA system-wide installed hai. PyTorch install karte waqt matching Compute Platform chunein — latest mat lein: likhne ke waqt PyTorch CUDA 12.6, 13.0 aur 13.2 support karta tha, jabki latest CUDA 13.3 tha aur unsupported tha. Verify karein: import torch, torch.cuda.is_available(), device name aur VRAM, plus nvidia-smi.

The Software Stack

Core library stack:

  • PyTorch — autograd, GPU compute
  • Hugging Face Transformers — AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained, Llama/Mistral/Gemma/Phi/Qwen ke liye unified API
  • Hugging Face PEFT — LoRA/QLoRA trainable matrices inject karta hai, base freeze rehta hai
  • Bitsandbytes — 4/8-bit quantization, load_in_4bit=True, NVIDIA-optimized
  • TRL — SFTTrainer, DPOTrainer; collation, gradient accumulation, logging, checkpointing handle karta hai
  • Hugging Face Accelerate — mixed precision, devices, distribution TRL ke peeche
  • Hugging Face Datasets — Arrow, memory-mapped, load_dataset("tatsu-lab/alpaca")
  • Weights & Biases — experiment tracking
  • TensorBoard — local tracking alternative

Other Training Techniques

Axolotl — config-driven training framework; YAML likho, training chala do. Unsloth — fast training kernels, kam VRAM me zyada speed.

Local Versus Cloud

Framework: 16 GB+ VRAM locally ho to QLoRA 7B tak kar sakte hain. One-off vs ongoing kaam (local months me payback deta hai). Data sovereignty (healthcare, finance, government) local mandate karta hai. 13B se upar, ya 70B QLoRA (lagbhag 40 GB) ke liye cloud A100 ya multi-GPU chahiye. Hybrid approach: locally chhote model/subset par develop aur debug karo, final job cloud me chalao.

Tiers: Colab Free T4 16 GB — QLoRA up to 7B (minimum); RTX 4090 / Colab Pro A100 24–40 GB — QLoRA up to 13B ya LoRA 7B; cloud A100 80 GB — LoRA 70B ya full 7B.

Reproducibility

Seed 42, pinned library versions, requirements.txt, aur har checkpoint ke saath training_args.bin save karna — reproducibility ki neev hai. Bina iske aapka result dobara nahi banega.

Seed-Based Generation

Batching for Cost Efficiency

Understanding Evol-Instruct

Generating Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

Dusra model verifier ka kaam karta hai, temperature 0.1 par, lagbhag 10% examples flag karta hai. Bada ya independent verifier + human review istemal karein.

Data Diversity Metrics: n-gram diversity = unique n-grams / total n-grams; 0.7 se neeche red flag hai. GPT-OSS-120B dataset ne diversity me low score kiya — lekhak is warning ko honestly flag karte hain.

Hallucination Detection: verifier model galat facts pakadta hai; human review final gate hai.

Ch 7 · The Mechanics of Adaptation

Full fine-tuning me teen problems hain — memory, storage aur catastrophic forgetting. LoRA in teeno ka jawab hai: base weights freeze kar do, aur sirf ek chhota low-rank update train karo. Ye chapter uski mathematics aur hyperparameters kholta hai.

The Problems with Full Fine-Tuning

The Memory Problem: weights + gradients + optimizer (Ch 3 ka 86 GB). The Storage Problem: har task ke liye model ki poori copy — 10 tasks matlab 10 copies. Catastrophic Forgetting: nayi training purani knowledge overwrite kar deti hai — classic example: model naya domain seekh kar general ability bhool jata hai.

Parameter Efficient Fine-Tuning

PEFT ka idea: base model ke 99% weights freeze kar do, sirf 1% train karo. Quality lagbhag wahi, cost dasvaan hissa.

LoRA

LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation): base weights W freeze kar do aur low-rank update ΔW = B·A train karo, jahan A hai (r × d_in) aur B hai (d_out × r). Inference par update W me add ho jata hai (W + BA), to latency cost zero hai. A Kaiming uniform se initialize hota hai, B zeros se — to training exactly base model se shuru hoti hai.

The Mathematics of LoRA

r = 16 aur alpha = 32 ho to scaling factor alpha/r = 2 hota hai. Update ko inference par W me add kar diya jata hai, to koi extra latency nahi. A Kaiming uniform, B zeros — shuruaat base model se.

Why It Works

Fine-tuning ke dauran weight matrix me jo change aata hai, wo low-rank hota hai — change ek chhote subspace me rehta hai. Isiliye rank-16 update lagbhag poora change capture kar leta hai.

Where LoRA Is Applied

Attention projections (q_proj, k_proj, v_proj, o_proj) aur MLP (gate_proj, up_proj, down_proj) par lagaya jata hai. Embedding layers aur language-model head usually targets nahi hote — bade hote hain aur zaroorat nahi padti.

Under the Hood

PEFT library LoRA matrices ko base model ke andar inject karti hai, base weights freeze rehte hain, aur sirf adapter matrices ke gradients compute hote hain. Isiliye memory aur compute dono bachte hain.

Understanding LoRA Hyperparameters

  • Rank (r): 4, 8, 16, 32 — zyada rank = zyada capacity, zyada memory
  • Alpha and the Scaling Factor: usually 2× rank; scaling = alpha/r
  • Dropout: 0.05–0.1, adapter ko regularize karta hai
  • Bias: default "none"
  • Target Modules: kaun se projections (q_proj, v_proj, etc.)
  • Task Type: jaise CAUSAL_LM

QLoRA: An Even Smaller Footprint

QLoRA me base model 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) me quantize hota hai, jabki LoRA adapter full precision me rehta hai — training quality 16-bit fine-tuning ke kareeb, memory ka fraction. Kitab ka worked example Gemma 4 E4B par: 38.9M trainable parameters, total ka 0.49%.

DoRA: Redefining the Adaptation

DoRA (Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation): weight ko magnitude aur direction me decompose karta hai aur dono ko alag se train karta hai. LoRA se thoda behtar ho sakta hai, chhoti extra cost par.

Choosing Your Approach

Full fine-tuning jab hardware ho aur maximum change chahiye; LoRA most kaam ke liye; QLoRA jab VRAM tight ho. Chapter ki Table 7-1 trade-offs summarize karti hai (memory, storage, forgetting, quality).

Direct Preference Optimization

Ch 12 · Diffusion Models

Transformers tokens predict karte hain; diffusion models images ko refine karte hain. Idea ulta hai: image ko noise se destroy karo, phir reverse karna seekho. Inference par pure noise se shuru karke image banti hai. Yahan hallucination feature hai — model detail fill karta hai.

Beyond Transformers

Diffusion models images generate karte hain refinement se, token prediction se nahi. Pehle ke approaches (PixelRNN/PixelCNN) pixel by pixel generate karte the aur slow the.

The Big Idea: Reverse a Destruction Process

Image lo, Gaussian noise se step by step destroy karo (forward process), phir destruction ko reverse karna seekho (reverse process). Model denoise karna seekhta hai; inference par pure noise se shuru karke denoise karke image banti hai.

The Forward Process: Controlled Destruction

Beta schedule (DDPM scheduler me 0.0001 se 0.02 tak linear, 1,000 steps) har step par thoda noise add karta hai; kaafi steps ke baad image pure noise ban jati hai. Code DDPMScheduler 1,000 timesteps ke saath banata hai.

Gaussian Noise

Noise Gaussian distribution se aata hai — har pixel me random values add hote hain, controlled tareeke se. Beta schedule decide karta hai har step par kitna noise add ho.

The Reverse Process: Denoising

Model predict karta hai kaun sa noise add hua tha (predicted vs actual noise ke beech mean squared error loss), use subtract karta hai, aur repeat karta hai. Har step thoda noise hatata hai; inference par 20–50 steps kaafi hote hain.

The UNet Architecture

UNet denoising network hai — Stable Diffusion 1.5 me 859.5M parameters. Down path (compress), bottleneck, up path (expand), skip connections ke saath, har level par text prompt par cross-attention, aur timestep ke liye FiLM conditioning.

The Latent Trick

Pixels par diffuse karne ki jagah compressed latent par diffuse karo — VAE 512×512×3 pixels ko 64×64×4 latent me compress karta hai, 48x compression. Yahi wajah hai ki Stable Diffusion fast hai.

The Text Encoder

CLIP ka text encoder prompt ko 77 vectors ki sequence me badalta hai, har ek 768 dimensions ka. UNet in par attend karta hai.

The Scheduler: Managing Denoising

Scheduler denoising steps manage karta hai — training ke liye DDPM aur DDIM, faster inference ke liye DPM++ aur UniPC. Scheduler har timestep par noise level set karta hai.

Classifier-Free Guidance

Model ko do baar chalao — ek baar prompt ke saath, ek baar empty/negative prompt ke saath — aur prompted result ki taraf extrapolate karo. Guidance scale 7.5 typical hai. Negative prompts ("blurry, low quality") unwanted traits se door dhakelte hain.

Stable Diffusion 1.5

Chapter ka reference model: total 1.5B parameters (UNet 859.5M, VAE 83.7M, text encoder 123M), 512×512 output, LAION par trained. Full pipeline: prompt tokenize karo, text encode karo, noise sample karo, scheduler loop chalao (step by step denoise, CFG apply karo), latent ko VAE se decode karo.

The Full Pipeline

Tokenize → encode → noise sample → denoise loop (CFG ke saath) → VAE decode → image. Har step me scheduler noise level batata hai aur UNet noise predict karta hai.

Going Beyond Stable Diffusion 1.5

SDXL, SD3, Flux, Stable Video Diffusion aur Sora (video ke liye); ControlNet aur IP-Adapter controlled generation ke liye. Production note: brand images ko consistent style chahiye — jo Ch 13 ka kaam hai.

Summary

Diffusion = destroy karna seekho, phir reverse karna seekho. UNet denoise karta hai, VAE 48x compress karta hai, CFG 7.5 prompt ki taraf khinchta hai. Figures 12-1 se 12-9 me forward destruction, reverse denoising, UNet, latent space, pipeline aur scheduler dikhaye gaye hain.

Ch 14 · Optimization and Quantization

Training ke baad model ko chhota aur tez banana padta hai — warna wo 24 GB GPU se bahar nahi niklega. Quantization floats ko integers me badalta hai. Is chapter me scale/zero-point, GGUF formats, bit-width sweep aur QAT — sab kuch numbers ke saath hai.

A Quick Refresher on Floating Point

Model size = parameter count × bytes per weight. FP32 4 bytes, BF16/FP16 2 bytes, INT8 1 byte, INT4 0.5 bytes. Per-layer embeddings (PLE) Gemma-specific detail hai jo yahan note ki gayi hai.

From Floats to Integers: Scale and Zero-Point

Float range ko integers me map kiya jata hai scale aur zero-point se: q = round(w/scale) + zero_point. Symmetric quantization (zero-point 0, zero par centred weights ke liye) vs asymmetric (non-zero zero-point, offset wali activations ke liye behtar). 32/64/128 weights ke groups ek scale share karte hain taaki error limit me rahe.

Symmetry and activations

Weights zero par centred hote hain to symmetric quantization kaam karti hai. Activations ka offset hota hai, to asymmetric behtar hoti hai. Groups (32/64/128 weights) ek scale share karte hain — group chhota ho to error kam, lekin overhead zyada.

The Format Zoo

GGUF (portable, llama.cpp), GPTQ (calibration-based, GPU), AWQ (activation-aware, llm-compressor ke through), EXL2 (mixed precision), bitsandbytes (training only). Quantization ek format + ek fused kernel hai — kernel hi speed deta hai, kyunki inference memory-bound hai.

The Importance Matrix

Importance matrix (imatrix) batata hai kaun se weights zyada important hain — unhe zyada precision milti hai. 300 in-domain calibration examples se banti hai; calibration, training aur test data disjoint hone chahiye. Banane me GPU par kuch minutes, CPU par lagbhag ek ghanta lagta hai.

Understanding the K-Quant Names

Q8_0 effectively lossless (reference), Q6_K near-lossless, Q5_K_M middle ground, Q4_K_M workhorse, Q3/Q2 degraded. Naam me "K" matlab K-quant — mixed precision groups ke saath.

Using GGUF with llama.cpp

Convert, Then Quantize: pehle convert karo (FP16, llama-convert), phir quantize karo (llama-quantize). Example log: 14,235.64 MiB at 16 bits per weight → 5,041.55 MiB at 5.67 bits per weight for Q4_K_M — 15 GB se 4.5 GB. The Importance Matrix: upar dekhein — 300 in-domain calibration examples.

Measuring Quantization Impact

The Bit-Width Sweep — accuracy benchmark par: FP16 14.90 GB, perplexity 6.41, accuracy 84.2%; Q6_K 6.28 GB, 6.43, 84.1%; Q5_K_M 5.42 GB, 6.47, 83.8%; Q4_K_M 4.68 GB, 6.58, 83.1%; Q3_K_M 3.79 GB, 7.22, 78.6%.

F1 sweep par: FP16 14.94 GB, perplexity 5.613, F1 44.0%; Q6_K 6.17 GB, 5.654, 43.4%; Q5_K_M 5.72 GB, 5.747, 43.3%; Q4_K_M 5.30 GB, 6.177, 42.1%; Q3_K_M 4.82 GB, 8.731, 40.0%.

Lesson: Q4_K_M lagbhag 1 point accuracy khota hai, 3x size saving ke badle; Q3 cliff se gir jata hai. Pipeline ka compress() max_acc_drop 0.02 (2 points) par gate karta hai.

Pipeline: convert → imatrix (300 calibration examples) → quantize → evaluate → gate (max_acc_drop 0.02) → deploy. Har step par accuracy measure karo, guess mat karo.

Avoiding Some Common Pitfalls

Galat tensors quantize karna, galat domain par calibrate karna, conversion ke baad chat template bhool jana, aur deployment se alag precision me evaluate karna. Har pitfall ka ilaaj: pipeline ke har step par check karo.

Merging

W_merged = W_base + (alpha/r)(B·A). Merge BF16 me karo, kabhi 4-bit base me nahi — "4-bit merge trap" adapter ko destroy kar deta hai. Parity verify karo: merged aur unmerged model ke next-token logits compare karo (argmax agreement aur max |Δlogit|), poori greedy generation se nahi.

KV cache

Size = 2 × n_layers × n_kv_heads × head_dim × seq_len × batch × bytes. GQA ise shrink karta hai, KV-cache quantization (q8_0/q4) aur shrink karta hai, PagedAttention, prefix caching aur offloading manage karte hain. Speculative decoding (chhota draft model tokens propose karta hai, bada model verify karta hai) 2–3x speedup deta hai; prefill/decode disaggregation dono phases ko alag hardware par split karta hai.

Summary

Q4_K_M workhorse hai — 1 point accuracy ke badle 3x saving. Q3 cliff hai. Merge BF16 me karo, 4-bit me kabhi nahi. QAT best long-term route hai. KV cache ka formula yaad rakhein.

18FAQs · Interview

  
FAQs — Interview Sawal-Jawab

  
36 interview-style sawal, kitab ke content par based. Har jawab Hinglish me, concept ke saath.

  

    
Q01Fine-tuning kya hai, aur prompt engineering se kaise alag hai?
Fine-tuning me model ke weights badle jate hain — model task seekhta hai. Prompt engineering me sirf prompt badla jata hai, weights wahi rehte hain. Prompt har request par dobara bheja aur charge kiya jata hai (instruction tax); fine-tuning me instruction weights me chali jati hai, inference par cost zero. Prompt aasan hai, fine-tuning gehri customization hai.

    
Q02RAG aur fine-tuning me se kab kya chunna chahiye?
RAG tab jab knowledge badalta rehta hai ya cite karna hai — retrieval se grounded jawab milta hai. Fine-tuning tab jab behaviour, style ya task format sikhana hai. Dono complementary hain, competing nahi. Aksar dono saath istemal hote hain — RAG knowledge deta hai, fine-tuning style aur behaviour.

    
Q03LoRA kaise kaam karta hai? ΔW = B·A ka matlab kya hai?
Base weights W freeze kar do. Sirf low-rank update ΔW = B·A train karo, jahan A hai (r × d_in) aur B hai (d_out × r). Inference par W + (α/r)·B·A ho jata hai, to latency cost zero. A Kaiming uniform se, B zeros se initialize hota hai — training exactly base model se shuru hoti hai. Kyun kaam karta hai: fine-tuning ka update low-rank hota hai, chhote subspace me rehta hai.

    
Q04LoRA me rank aur alpha kya hote hain? Kaise chunein?
Rank r update ki capacity hai — 4, 8, 16, 32 common hain; zyada rank = zyada capacity, zyada memory. Alpha usually 2× rank hota hai; scaling factor α/r hota hai. r=16, α=32 ho to scaling 2. Shuruaat r=8 ya 16 se karo, phir tune karo. Dropout 0.05–0.1 regularization ke liye hota hai.

    
Q05QLoRA kya hai aur ye LoRA se kaise alag hai?
QLoRA me base model 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) me quantize hota hai, adapter full precision me rehta hai. 7B model: full FT 86 GB, LoRA 17 GB, QLoRA 7 GB. Quality 16-bit fine-tuning ke kareeb rehti hai. Lekin QLoRA adapter ko 4-bit base me merge nahi kar sakte, aur vision fine-tuning me QLoRA fail hota hai.

    
Q067B model full fine-tune karne me 86 GB VRAM kyun chahiye?
Weights FP16 me 14 GB + gradients 14 GB + Adam optimizer state (mean + variance, FP32) 56 GB + activations ~2 GB = 84–86 GB. Optimizer state hi sabse bada hissa hai. LoRA sirf adapter ke gradients aur optimizer rakhta hai, to 17 GB ho jata hai. Yahi wajah hai ki VRAM "one number to rule them all" hai.

    
Q07Catastrophic forgetting kya hai? Isse kaise bachein?
Nayi training purani knowledge overwrite kar deti hai — model naya domain seekh kar general ability bhool jata hai. Bachne ke tareeke: LoRA istemal karo (base freeze rehta hai), kam learning rate rakho, general data mix karo, aur general-knowledge checks se evaluate karo. Full fine-tuning me risk sabse zyada hai.

    
Q08Chat template mismatch kya hai aur ye silent failure kyun hai?
Har model ka apna chat template hota hai (Gemma ke <start_of_turn> tokens, Llama ke [INST], ChatML ke <|im_start|>). Agar training aur inference ke templates alag hon, to model galat format seekhta hai aur weird answers deta hai — bina error ke. Isiliye template ko tokenizer se apply karo aur pehle debug karke dekho.

    
Q09Loss masking kya hai? Labels -100 ka matlab kya hai?
Prompt/instruction tokens ke labels -100 set kar diye jate hain, taaki loss me ignore hon. Model sirf apne response (assistant tokens) par train hota hai — "response-only training". Isse model jawab dena seekhta hai, sawal yaad karna nahi. TRL ka data collator ye handle karta hai.

    
Q10Training ke liye kitne epochs chahiye? Data size se kya relation hai?
10k+ examples: 1–2 epochs; 1–5k: 3–5 epochs; 600 se kam: 10–20 epochs. Chhota dataset zyada epochs maangta hai, lekin overfitting ka risk badhta hai. Running example me 549 train examples par 20 epochs, validation loss 0.88 par plateau aur step 75 ke baad gap widen — overfitting ka sign.

    
Q11Learning rate LoRA aur full fine-tuning me alag kyun hota hai?
LoRA me 1e-4 se 2e-4, full fine-tuning me 1e-5 se 5e-5. LoRA sirf chhota adapter train karta hai, to zyada LR safe hai. Full FT me poora model move hota hai, to chhota LR chahiye warna diverge ho jata hai. Zyada LR se loss explode ya oscillate karti hai; kam LR se kuch seekhta hi nahi.

    
Q12Gradient accumulation kya hai? Effective batch size kaise nikalte hain?
Chhoti VRAM par bada batch simulate karne ka tareeka: kai chhote batches ke gradients accumulate karo, phir ek update karo. Effective batch = per-device batch × accumulation. Running example: batch 8 × accumulation 8 = effective batch 64. Isse memory bache bina bada batch milta hai.

    
Q13Perplexity kya hai? 27.412 se 8.446 ka kya matlab hai?
Perplexity = exp(loss). Kam perplexity matlab model text ko behtar predict kar raha hai. Base model MedQA perplexity 27.412, adapted 8.446 — 69% improvement. Lekin ratio rule yaad rakhein: 1–2 leakage, 2–4 healthy, 6+ overfitting. Chapter ke numbers boundary par hain, isiliye sirf perplexity kaafi nahi — LLM-as-a-judge aur benchmarks bhi chahiye.

    
Q14Fine-tuned model ko evaluate kaise karein? Sirf aankh se dekhna kaafi hai?
Nahi. Chaar tareeke: side-by-side comparison (100+ prompts, temperature 0), perplexity, LLM-as-a-judge (73% win rate running example me), aur domain-specific benchmarks + safety/refusal tests + general-knowledge checks (catastrophic forgetting pakadne ke liye). Aankh se dekhna sirf pehla test hai.

    
Q15Adapter ko merge kab karein aur kab nahi?
Merge karo jab ek hi adapter deploy karna ho — zero inference overhead milta hai. Merge mat karo jab hot-swapping chahiye ho (kai adapters ek base par), kyunki merged adapter ko unmerge nahi kar sakte. Aur kabhi 4-bit base me merge mat karo — "4-bit merge trap" adapter ko destroy kar deta hai. Merge BF16 me karo.

    
Q16DPO kya hai? RLHF se behtar kyun mana jata hai?
DPO (Direct Preference Optimization) reward model ko skip karke preference ko seedha optimize karta hai. Loss = -log σ(β × margin). RLHF me teen stages hote hain (SFT → reward model → PPO) aur reward hacking ka risk hota hai. DPO simpler aur stable hai. Beta default 0.1, commonly 0.01–0.05. Reference model + KL divergence drift rokte hain.

    
Q17DPO kab istemal karein aur kab nahi?
Jab style galat ho, "better" answer clear ho, aur 1,000–1,500 preference pairs hon. Skip karein: factual errors ke liye, single-correct-answer tasks ke liye, ya chhote datasets ke liye. DPO behaviour sikhata hai, facts nahi.

    
Q18SFT aur DPO me kya farak hai?
SFT knowledge sikhata hai — sahi jawab kya hai. DPO behaviour sikhata hai — jawab kaise dena hai (tone, safety, helpfulness, refusal). Running example me loss 0.68 se 0.14 giri, chosen rewards badhe, rejected gire, margin bada. Dono ek ke baad ek aate hain: pehle SFT, phir DPO.

    
Q19Data contamination kya hai aur evaluation par kya asar padta hai?
Validation ya test data training me leak ho jaye to evaluation meaningless ho jati hai — model ne jawab pehle se dekh rakha hai. Isiliye train/validation/test split karte waqt overlap check karna zaroori hai. Running example me 549 train / 62 validation, seed 42.

    
Q20Synthetic data kya hai? Teacher-student paradigm kaise kaam karta hai?
Bada teacher model examples generate karta hai, chhota student unse seekhta hai. Seeds se diversity aati hai (8×5×8 = 320 combinations → 1,000 seeds). Batching se cost bachta hai (5 questions/call → 200 calls). Quality control ke liye dusra model verifier banta hai (temperature 0.1, ~10% flag). 20% tak khona normal hai; 30%+ matlab prompts galat hain.

    
Q21n-gram diversity kya hai? 0.7 se neeche kyun red flag hai?
n-gram diversity = unique n-grams / total n-grams. 0.7 se neeche matlab data repeat ho raha hai — teacher wahi tarah ke examples bana raha hai. Kitab ke teacher dataset ne low score kiya, aur lekhak ne is warning ko honestly flag kiya. Diversity kam ho to seeds badhao ya temperature badhao.

    
Q22Evol-Instruct kya hai?
Prompts ko evolve karne ka tareeka: constraints add karo, deeper reasoning, specificity ya broadening. 20–30% examples ko ek baar evolve karo, phir answer regenerate karo. Anchoring warning: evolved question apna clinical anchor nahi khona chahiye — jaise 72 saal ke patient par clopidogrel wala example.

    
Q23PII leak ka risk kya hai? Training se pehle kya karna chahiye?
Models training data regurgitate kar sakte hain — PII leak existential risk hai (HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA fines). Detect karo: NER models, regex, ya Presidio. Redaction: placeholders se replace karo, record hata do, ya synthetic replacement banao. Raw PII par kabhi train mat karo.

    
Q24Grouped Query Attention (GQA) kya hai? KV cache kaise bachta hai?
Ek key/value head kai query heads share karta hai. Llama 3 8B: 32 query heads → 8 groups (4 per group) = 75% KV cache reduction. 6 heads share karein to 50% reduction. Trade-off: shared view nuance kho deta hai. KV cache hi inference ka bottleneck hai, isiliye GQA itna important hai.

    
Q25Sliding window attention kya hai? Receptive field kaise badhta hai?
Har token sirf pichle 4,096 tokens par attend karta hai (Mistral 7B). Attention cost quadratic hai — 1,000 tokens ko 1,000,000 lookups. Lekin multi-layer pyramid me receptive field depth ke saath linearly badhta hai: layer 1 → 4,096, layer 2 → 8,192, layer 3 → 12,288.

    
Q26Mixture of Experts (MoE) kya hai? 46.7B parameters me sirf 12.9B active kyun hote hain?
Router har token ke liye top-2 experts chunta hai (Mixtral 8x7B). Baaki experts us token ke liye sote hain. Isiliye total 46.7B hone par bhi per token sirf 12.9B active hote hain. MoE ko fine-tune karna mushkil hai aur zyada VRAM chahiye.

    
Q27Vision language model kaise kaam karta hai? Two-tower architecture kya hai?
Vision tower (SigLIP image encoder, 400M+ params) image ko features me badalta hai; projection layer (MLP, jaise 1152 → 2560) unhe language tokens me map karti hai; language tower (LLM) jawab deta hai. 224×224 image 256 tokens banti hai. Sabse sasti strategy: vision tower freeze, projection + LoRA train karo.

    
Q28Diffusion model aur transformer me kya farak hai?
Transformer next token predict karta hai; diffusion model image ko denoise karke refine karta hai. Forward process me Gaussian noise add hota hai (beta 0.0001 → 0.02, 1,000 steps); reverse me model noise predict karke hatata hai. Inference par 20–50 steps kaafi hote hain. Latent trick (VAE, 48x compression) ise fast banata hai.

    
Q29DreamBooth kya hai? Prior preservation kyun zaroori hai?
DreamBooth ek specific subject sikhata hai (jaise "sks dog"). Rare identifier token subject ko bind karta hai. Prior preservation — generic class images (100–200) ke saath train karna — model ko yaad dilata hai ki "dog" kya hota hai, warna wo class hi bhool jata hai. Recipe: 10–20 photos, rank 4–16, LR 1e-4, 500–1,000 steps.

    
Q30Style LoRA aur DreamBooth me kya farak hai?
DreamBooth subject sikhata hai (ek specific dog/person/product). Style LoRA visual vocabulary sikhata hai (brand look, art style). Dono UNet ki attention layers par LoRA lagate hain. Style LoRA me captions ko style describe karna chahiye, content nahi — warna model galat cheez seekhta hai. Dono ko stack bhi kar sakte hain (subject 0.8 + style 0.6).

    
Q31Quantization kya hai? Q4_K_M workhorse kyun hai?
Floats ko integers me badalna — scale aur zero-point se. Q4_K_M 15 GB ko 4.5 GB bana deta hai, sirf ~1 point accuracy khota hai (84.2% → 83.1%). Q6_K near-lossless hai, Q5_K_M middle ground, Q3 cliff se girta hai (78.6%). Pipeline max_acc_drop 0.02 par gate karta hai. Quantization format + fused kernel hai — kernel hi speed deta hai kyunki inference memory-bound hai.

    
Q32GGUF, GPTQ, AWQ me kya farak hai?
GGUF portable hai, llama.cpp ke saath chalta hai. GPTQ calibration-based hai, GPU par chalta hai. AWQ activation-aware hai. EXL2 mixed precision hai. Bitsandbytes training ke liye hai. Format chunna deployment par depend karta hai — laptop/CPU ke liye GGUF, GPU serving ke liye GPTQ/AWQ.

    
Q33QAT (quantization-aware training) kya hai?
Training ke dauran rounding simulate karo, taaki model quantization ke prati robust bane. Official QAT Gemma models downloadable hain (q4_0 GGUF, w4a16 for vLLM, unquantized QAT base for fine-tuning). Fine-tuning ke liye unquantized QAT base istemal karo.

    
Q34Ollama aur vLLM me kab kya chunna chahiye?
Ollama sabse simple hai — single user, local, Modelfile se setup. vLLM concurrent users ke liye hai — continuous batching, PagedAttention, proper server, NVIDIA GPU chahiye. vLLM LoRA adapters ko on the fly load kar sakta hai (enable_lora, max_lora_rank) bina merge kiye. Shuruaat Ollama se, scale vLLM par.

    
Q35Ek base par kai experts kaise chalate hain? Iska economic fayda kya hai?
Ek base model memory me rehta hai (~8 GB, 4-bit); har request par sahi adapter (71 MB) load hota hai — medical, legal, support alag-alag. Har expert ke liye poora model load karne ki zaroorat nahi. Yahi kitab ka economic heart hai: specialists ki fleet, ek hi GPU par.

    
Q36Token tax kya hai? Fine-tuning use kaise khatam karta hai?
Har token ki keemat hai — input/output prices Table 1-1 me hain. Instructions har request par dobara bheji aur charge ki jati hain (instruction tax), agents token use 5–10x multiply karte hain. Legal contract example: 1,000 users × 5 docs/day = $21–$8,000/day, $20/month subscription ke against. Fine-tuning instructions aur behaviour ko weights me daal deta hai — ek baar seekh lo, inference par cost zero.
